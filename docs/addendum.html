Dawn of Decay — Implementation Addendum

Records every design change made during prototyping (July 2026). Where this document conflicts with the original design docs, this document wins. Organized by the source document each change updates.

Updates to "Dawn of Decay.docx" & "DoD Narrative_Zones.docx" (Structure)

  • DESIGN PIVOT — no explorable overworld. Runs now use a Slay-the-Spire-style node map: the player picks a reachable node on a branching 8-floor path and combat begins immediately. Node types: Fight, Elite, Rest (+30% HP & Mana), Loot (+1 item), Boss (top of map).
  • Zones survive as flavor layers: each map region carries a zone's enemy roster, battle background, and (future) environmental modifiers. Forest of Old is the first zone; its boss slot is currently a stand-in "Withered Warden".
  • Roguelike layer lives between fights: rewards after victory (Rest +25% HP/Mana or Scavenge +1 item), talents/specs are changed from the map's Party screen.

Updates to "DoD Run.docx" (Run Flow)

  • HP and Mana carry over between battles. No automatic refill. Rage still starts at 0 each battle. Faith and Arcane Resonance are battle-scoped.
  • Fallen heroes return at 20% HP after a won battle. Full party wipe ends the run.
  • Shared run inventory: Health Potion (40 HP, targeted), Mana Potion (40 Mana/Rage, targeted), Bomb (50 true damage to all enemies), Revive Potion (fallen ally at 50%), Defense Potion (party +10% armor, 5 turns). Starting stock: 2/1/1/1/1. Loot drops favor potions.

Updates to "DoD Combat.docx" (Combat System)

  • Attack rolls: 5% miss (no effect), 5% parry (defender negates and counters with a free basic attack; Broken units cannot parry; heals cannot miss/be parried), 10% crit (1.5x damage, +25% chance vs Broken). Base rates; stats/talents will modify.
  • Guard action (free, every hero): -40% damage and -50% Pressure taken until the unit's next turn; 1.5 initiative delay; Perfect check sheds 10 Pressure. For the Mage, Guard also vents all Resonance.
  • Items in combat: one item per character per turn, does not consume the turn (dropdown menu next to abilities).
  • Skill checks: timing bar with Perfect / Good / Sloppy zones; every ability carries a unique Perfect bonus. Perfect grants +15% effect and the bonus property.
  • Statuses implemented: Broken, Slow (-25% speed), Burn (6/turn), Bleed (5/turn), Sunder (-35% armor), Ward (-50% Pressure taken), Fortify (+10% armor), Barrier (damage absorb), Focus (+10 Mana/turn), Renewal (+8 HP/turn), Surge (+20% attack), Guard. Status chips show under units with tooltips.
  • Tuned baseline (200-battle simulations): ~85% win rate, 7-9 rounds per fight, damage share Warrior ~42% / Mage ~40% / Cleric ~19%, ~2.5 Breaks per side per fight.

Updates to "DoD Classes.docx" (Classes)

Warrior

  • Rallying Shout (core): free, grants the Warrior 15 Rage, party sheds 15 Pressure, allies regain 20% of their max resource (25 Pressure / 30% on Perfect).
  • Crushing Blow applies Sunder (-35% armor, 3 turns) baseline.

Mage — reworked

  • Arcane Resonance: +15% damage dealt and +3% crit per stack, but +10% damage taken per stack. Stacks build on damaging casts (2 on spell crit), never decay, and are only removed by Guard (vents all stacks). Backlash Ward: +15 Mana on reaching max stacks.
  • Magic Bolt: 25 damage.
  • Arcane Barrier removed from the core kit. Replaced by Arcane Cannon: 30 base damage; recoils on the Mage for 5% of damage dealt +5% per Resonance stack; Perfect skill check = no recoil.

Cleric

  • Core kit per doc: Smite, Mend Wounds, Blessed Purge, Renewal. Faith builds +10 per action; Miracles (Faith spenders) arrive with specializations.

Updates to "DoD UI.docx" (Interface)

  • Map screen: gothic zone title, branching node buttons, party HP/MP readout, Party tab (full party screen: per-hero stats, abilities, specializations, back navigation) and Inventory dropdown.
  • Combat UI: initiative bar with cropped portraits (next 10 turns), Pressure bar shifts purple to pink near Break, numeric HP/resource bars, scrolling battle history log (all actions, damage, statuses, Breaks, deaths), item dropdown, Guard button.
  • Feel: attack lunges, hit flash + knockback, crit numbers explode on screen with shake, Break = hitstop + purple fracture flash + camera punch (per UI doc), 12 placeholder retro SFX.

Change Log

2026-07-04 — Specializations v1 and combat revisions

  • Specializations implemented (selectable and switchable on the Party screen between fights). Each grants a passive plus abilities: Warrior — Berserker (Blood Frenzy; Bloodlust, Wildstrikes), Warden (Bulwark; Shieldwall, Retaliation), Swordmaster (Duelist; Overpower, Momentum). Mage — Pyromancer (Ignite; Flame Surge, Phoenix Rebirth), Cryomancer (Chill; Razor Ice, Blizzard), Arcanist (Echo; Arcane Cannon, Arcane Rift, Reality Fracture). Cleric — Holy (Grace; Dawnbreak, Hymn of Hope), Inquisitor (Zeal; Burning Verdict, Condemn), Occultist (Corrupted Channeling; Hex of Ruin, Dark Benediction). Cleric Miracles are the first Faith spenders (40-60 Faith).
  • Taunt is Warrior core (all specs): free, forces all enemies to attack the Warrior for 5 turns. Warden's second ability is now Retaliation (counter stance, 3 turns).
  • Arcane Cannon is Arcanist-exclusive; removed from the Mage core kit. Focus removed entirely. Mage core = Magic Bolt + Arcane Surge.
  • Razor Ice (Cryomancer) replaces Frost Spike: 15 damage to 2 random enemies; always crits against Slowed targets.
  • Hero Pressure generation reduced ~20-30% across all kits; enemy Breaks now ~1.5 per battle (were ~2.5) so Breaking feels earned.
  • Combat UI: action bar is now Basic Attack | Abilities dropdown | Guard | Items dropdown; free abilities no longer labeled "(Free)"; target selection has a Cancel button to re-pick the ability before casting.
  • New systems supporting specs: AoE attacks, random-multi-hit attacks, Faith costs, armor pierce, missing-HP drain, Taunt targeting, Echo procs, statuses Shieldwall / Empower / Exposed / Cripple / Retaliation.

2026-07-04 (later) — UI flow and Cleric revision

  • Action bar moved to bottom-center of the screen.
  • Cancel is now universal: self-cast, AoE, and auto-targeted abilities get a Cast/Cancel confirm step; targeted abilities keep the Cancel button during target selection.
  • Hovering a portrait on the initiative bar highlights that unit on the battlefield (plus name tooltip).
  • Status effect durations standardized to 3 turns (Slow, Burn, Bleed, Ward, Fortify, Empower, Cripple, Defense Potion). Until-next-turn effects (Guard, Shieldwall), 5-turn Taunt, and 5-turn Renewal unchanged.
  • Cleric: Blessed Purge replaced by Divine Shield — grants an ally a shield absorbing 100 damage (130 on Perfect) that persists until it breaks.
  • Rallying Shout removed from the Warrior kit for now.

2026-07-04 (third pass) — Cast flow and kit trims

  • Confirm prompt removed. Targeted abilities cancel during target selection; auto-cast abilities (self/AoE/random) cancel via a button next to the skill check bar. Cancelling refunds nothing because nothing was spent yet.
  • Action bar now lives in a self-centering container at the bottom center (fixed the drift-to-left bug).
  • Razor Ice shards land 0.45s apart so each hit reads distinctly (applies to all multi-hit attacks).
  • Heavy Strike removed for now. Crushing Blow moved to Swordmaster, replacing Momentum. Warrior core kit: Strike + Taunt.
  • Party screen: hover tooltips on stats (definitions of Armor/Speed/Stability/resources), each ability card, spec cards (passive + granted ability details), and hero list entries.
  • Simulator bot now runs default specializations (Swordmaster/Pyromancer/Holy); baseline 92% wins, 6.4 rounds, 1.7 enemy Breaks per battle.

2026-07-04 (fourth pass) — Talent trees v1

  • Talent trees: every specialization now has a 6-talent tree (2 talents per tier; tier 2 unlocks at 2 picks, tier 3 at 4). Talents are stat passives, ability alterations, or entirely new abilities (Rampage, Bulwark Slam, Flurry, Inferno, Absolute Zero, Singularity, Sanctuary, Excommunicate, Soul Leech).
  • Talent points: every hero earns points from each combat node defeated — 1 per fight, 2 per elite, 3 per boss. Spent from the Party screen (Talents button on each spec card). Talents apply while that spec is active.
  • Boss "choice of three class modifiers" is deferred until runs extend past one zone (the run currently ends at the boss, so the choice would never matter). The 3 boss points are live.
  • Specialization passives now appear as a permanent gold ★ buff chip on the character with the passive's description.
  • Simultaneous AoE abilities (Condemn, Wildstrikes, Blizzard, Flame Surge, Inferno) hit all targets at once again; only sequential multi-hit attacks (Razor Ice, Flurry) pace their hits.

2026-07-04 (fifth pass) — Draft, permanent specs, damage types

  • Hero draft: runs open on a draft screen — choose three heroes from the roster (duplicates allowed). New Run always returns here.
  • Permanent specialization: heroes start unawakened with core kits. After the first combat victory (which also grants the first talent point), each hero must choose a specialization on a dedicated awakening screen — the choice is locked for the rest of the run.
  • Party screen consolidated: selecting a hero now shows one page — stats, class explanation, compact ability chips (hover for details), and the chosen spec's full talent tree. Separate spec cards/tree screens removed.
  • Talent trees grown to 8 talents each (T1 x2 / T2 x3 / T3 x3), including a new parry-chance stat (Swordmaster's Riposte Training).
  • Damage types introduced: arcane, nature, shadow, holy, physical, fire, frost. Every damaging ability is typed (e.g. Magic Bolt/Cannon arcane, Flame Surge/Inferno fire, Razor Ice/Blizzard frost, Smite/Verdict/Condemn holy, Hex/Soul Leech shadow, all weapon attacks physical). Units carry per-type resistances (negative = vulnerability): orcs resist physical slightly; the Withered Warden resists nature 50%, physical 10%, and is vulnerable to fire (-25%). Tooltips show the type; the battle log notes resisted/vulnerable hits.
  • Enemy damage +20% across all orc kits. Post-change baseline: 85% wins, ~0.9 hero deaths per battle.
  • Arcane Rift removed (Arcanist: Cannon, Reality Fracture, + Singularity talent).

2026-07-04 (sixth pass) — WoW-style talents, flow polish, stacking bleeds

  • Talent trees rebuilt WoW-style: nodes have ranks (multi-point stat/numeric talents, e.g. Thick Blood 3 ranks of +10 HP) and branching prerequisites ("Requires: Deep Wounds"); ability-granting talents stay single-rank. Tier N unlocks at (N-1)*3 points spent in the tree.
  • Flow: spec selection now happens immediately after the hero draft (before the map); after every battle victory the game routes through the Party screen before the node map; unspent talent points glow gold on the map's Party tab, the party list, and hero rows.
  • Bleed stacks additively — each application adds another 5-damage wound (chip shows Bl2, Bl3...). Applies to enemies and heroes alike; sim win rate dropped from 85% to ~70% as a result (enemy Jagged Cuts stack now).
  • Berserker's Blood Frenzy chip live-updates with the current bonus (e.g. "+24%").
  • Razor Ice re-targets each shard at launch — no more strikes on corpses or stalled sequences.
  • Node map shows the Forest zone art (from the new "imported files" workflow) behind a readability dim.
  • Distinct hero models require new sprite sheets — currently only Soldier and Orc exist. Drop new sheets in "imported files" using the Soldier format (100x100 frame rows: Name_Idle/Walk/Attack01-03/Hurt/Death PNGs).
