DAWN OF DECAY — THE SYSTEMS RECONNAISSANCE

What the merged talent layer can reach, derived from the code. This is a REPORT. Nothing was authored, built, merged, changed, retired or retuned — no node was proposed, named, sketched or reserved; no system was extended; no constant and no magnitude moved. It is written to be open while 274 talent nodes are authored, so every system section below stands alone and repeats what it needs rather than pointing at another section.

THE LINE, AS THE DESIGNER RULED IT.
“A talent may not touch a rune, an ability, a passive or an engine. Everything else is fair game.”

WHAT THIS PAGE MEASURES AGAINST IT. FP asked which of the 324 nodes happen to be engine-free and found 50. This page asks the opposite question: which SYSTEMS exist that no rune, ability, passive or engine owns — and, in each, what a node could say. For every system it reports four things: what exists; whether it is already parametric (driven by named constants or fields a per-hero value could feed) or authored per site (its numbers written wherever they are used, so a node cannot reach it until a layer is built); what a node reaches TODAY with no new machinery; and what would need building.

SR-HOWTO — HOW TO READ A SECTION

Every heading on this page carries its section's identifier — SR-CHECK, SR-BREAK, SR-POUCH and so on — in the heading text itself, and every subsection repeats it. Two recon documents already share sub-headings across eight and twelve sections; a search for “What would need building” here returns SR-TIMELINE · 4 · What would need building, which says which system it is. Search for the identifier, not the topic word.

Every system section has the same six numbered parts:

  1. What exists — constants with their values, fields, functions, and read sites as LINES (EV's rule: the read site is the line, not the function).
  2. Already parametric? — one of PARAMETRIC (a named constant, table or field a node's value could feed), PARTLY, or PER-SITE (the numbers live at the places they are used). SC_PROFILE_DEFAULT is the shape of the first: a dict of dials, one caller using a non-default copy. STATUS_INFO is the shape of the last: ids and prose, no magnitude, every strength authored at the call.
  3. What a node reaches TODAY — a payload key a node can write now, that this system already reads. See the plumbing box below: today means a BattleUnit field, written at battle spawn.
  4. What would need building — the missing hook, and where it would go.
  5. Who owns any of it — whether a rune, an ability, a passive or an engine owns the system or a part of it, with the reason.
  6. Distinct things a node could say — the count §4 totals. A distinct thing is a different dial or a different condition, never a different magnitude: +10% Break damage and +15% Break damage are one; +Break damage and Break damage on a Perfect are two. Each is marked TODAY (a field exists and this system already reads it), SMALL (one new field and one read line at a site that already exists), LARGE (a new door, layer, run-side reader or piece of UI) or RULING (the line's edge or a standing rule decides it first).

SR-PLUMB — HOW A NODE REACHES ANYTHING AT ALL (read this once; every section repeats the one line it needs)

A NODE IS A NUMBER ADDED TO A HERO'S BATTLE CONFIG AT SPAWN. That is the whole of the machinery. Talents.apply_from_tree runs inside the battle spawn (battle.gd:1202-1203; the sim's test hook at :1216; the hero sheet builds a throwaway copy to draw, party_screen.gd:169), and BattleUnit.setup copies every config key onto the unit with set() (unit.gd:2163-2166). No node's number is read anywhere outside a battle — not by the map, the shop, the pouch, the bargain, the events or the victory screen. The run's only reads of talents are the loadout handoff, the save's cleanup of unknown ids, ability ownership (which reads grants, and no node grants), and display.

SR-PLUMB · The seven traps a new node walks into, each silent

  1. A NAME THE UNIT DOES NOT DECLARE PAYS NOTHING. set() ignores an unknown property without a word. A new dial is one var in unit.gd plus one read line, and a misspelt one is free, inert and invisible.
  2. THE ZERO-START TRAP. The stat arm adds to cfg.get(field, 0) (talents.gd:2859-2861) — not to the unit's own default. The starting config carries only a dozen keys (health, armor, attack, constitution, speed, stability, resource and the like), so a field whose unit default is NOT zero and is NOT in that config comes out as 0 + n: healing_received_mult (default 1.0), parry_chance (−1.0, a sentinel), mod_speed_mult and mod_cost_mult (1.0), mod_bd_mult (100), second_max (100), overcharge_mult (0.5), mercy_threshold (0.5). A +10% healing-received node would set that hero's healing received to a tenth. Nothing errors.
  3. TWO CLASS PASSIVES ASSIGN OVER THE TREE. The spawn writes the class passives AFTER the talent tree, and writes them with =: healing_received_mult = 1.15 for every Cleric (Holy Conduit) and mana_regen_bonus = 10 for every Mage (Evocation) (battle.gd:1274-1279). A node writing either field is silently replaced on that class. And a matching battle modifier later assigns over a node's mod_* field the same way (battle.gd:1783-1876).
  4. A DICTIONARY CANNOT BE WRITTEN. resists and type_dmg_bonus are dicts, and payloads only write scalars (the Ashen Skin comment, battle.gd:1369-1375). A node touching a damage type or a resistance needs a spawn-side line of its own, as Ashen Skin has.
  5. “PARTY-WIDE” MEANS THE BEST HOLDER, AND SOMETIMES ONLY WHILE HE LIVES. _max_hero_rank takes the MAX, not the sum, over the living heroes (15 call lines, 13 fields); _living_hero_with takes the first living holder (29 call sites); a few effects are stamped once at spawn and outlive their holder. Two heroes holding one party-wide node do not stack, and a live read goes quiet when its holder dies.
  6. THE ORDINARY STRIKE LOOP IS NOT EVERY ATTACK. _resolve sends any ability carrying a special to its handler BEFORE the strike loop (battle.gd:8171: if ab.special != "": await _resolve_special(…)), and 70% of the corpus carries one (FJ's live dump: 160 of 227). A node read inside the strike loop pays only on attacks without a special, unless a handler copies the read — which is how No Quarter came to have a second copy inside Guard Change.
  7. A FIELD ANY RUNE WRITES — LIVE OR RETIRED — IS GUARDED BY check_em. Its §1 walks every entry of runes.json, retired ones included, and reds on any rune writing a field a live node writes, unless the field is in UNIT_MATH — which it asserts as an equality. So a new node on armor, max_hp, mana_regen_bonus, resource, healing_received_mult, or the idle mindfulness_ranks, beacon_ranks and capacitor_ranks, takes that gate red until UNIT_MATH gains a row with its reason — an instrument edit the authoring batch owes, not a defect.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR EVERY “TODAY” ON THIS PAGE: TODAY means a field a payload can write, that this system already reads, with none of the seven traps in the way. Where a trap applies, the section says so.

SR-BREAK — BREAK (Pressure, stability, the Broken window, and what Break damage reads)

SR-BREAK IN ONE SENTENCE: NO ENGINE OWNS THE METER, ITS VICTIM SIDE IS FULLY PARAMETRIC, ITS ATTACKER SIDE REACHES 3 OF 31 SOURCES, WHAT “BROKEN” DOES IS LITERALS — AND HEROES CARRY THE SAME METER. It is the richest system on this page today: nine things a node can say with no new code, most of them already said by FP's survivors.

SR-BREAK · 1 · What exists

  • The meter: stability is 100 for every class and all 21 enemy kinds; constitution is the divisor (85–130 on heroes, 60–170 on enemies, the three bosses at 150 / 160 / 170); Ability.pressure is Break damage per hit — 76 of the 227 abilities carry it.
  • Every live basic attack carries pressure: Strike 18, Fireball and Frostbolt 15, Arcane Explosion 10 twice, Smite 16, Shadowrend 16, Quick Shot 14. Pressure-bearing DRAFT cards per spec re-derive EP §2's figures exactly — Sharpshooter 6, Arcanist 5, Berserker / Swordmaster / Pyromancer / Occultist 4, Cryomancer 3, Beastmaster and Survivalist 2, Warden and Holy 1, the Devout 0.
  • The attacker side is in the strike loop: the ability's pressure × the grade (1.25 / 1.0 / 0.5) × 1.5 on a crit, × 0.25 if parried, then per-card riders, then broken_will_ranks and rune_bd_bonus (their only reads, battle.gd:10472-10480), then the brace's ×0.75.
  • The victim side is one block every source passes through (unit.gd:3655-3778): effective constitution, BD × 100 / constitution, Breaking Darkness, Devotion, Hold the Line, the modifiers, Immovable, Ironclad's cap, then the fill — at stability the unit Breaks and the meter is clamped to it, so overflow is lost.
  • ONLY 3 OF THE 31 BREAK-BEARING take_hit CALLS TAKE THEIR VALUE FROM THE STRIKE LOOP (the hit, Long Watch's carry, the Sundering splash). The other 28 — twelve card handlers passing raw pressure and sixteen constants — never see grade, crit, Broken Will or the brace. And there are 15 separate “did this hit Break it” checks, no single one.

§3 — SR-LINE — WHAT THE LINE EXCLUDES, PRECISELY

The brief asks four questions and says the designer rules on the edges. Each answer gives the letter, the spirit and the reason; this page rules on none of them. Two standing rules come first, because between them they decide more nodes than every edge together.

SR-LINE · A · Two standing rules the line runs into

A1 — THE DO CHARTER PERMITS WHAT THE LINE FORBIDS. CLAUDE.md: “A talent modifying the spec's PROTECTED CORE is guaranteed and permitted” — “THE LAST SENTENCE IS THE RULING AND IT IS WORTH 83 NODES” — “DO NOT RE-OPEN THIS AS AN OPEN QUESTION.” The charter's permitted list is a stat, the spec's own passive, the spec's own resource, the protected core, and another node in the same tree. The line keeps the stat, the resource and the other node, and forbids the passive and the core. So it narrows a ruling CLAUDE.md records as settled; the 33 node payloads that edit an ability today were legal under DO and are all out under the line. That is the designer's to overturn, and nothing on this page assumes it has been.

A2 — EN §4 PUTS HALF OF THE BRIEF'S LIST ON THE RELIC SIDE. “If it sets the run up — the purse, the pouch, the shop, the spawn line, what a victory pays, what an elite drops — it is a RELIC.” By read site that is gold, the shop, the pouch's structure, the victory heal, the draft offer's size and the map's rewards. And the rule has no tiebreak for a per-hero run effect: the talent test is “read while a turn resolves, OR must know which spec the hero is”, and a per-hero run effect passes both. The brief says everything outside the line is fair game; EN §4 says otherwise for these, and 15 of the 32 ruling-gated things on this page wait on that seam alone.

SR-LINE · B · Is a STATUS an engine's?

  • THE CENSUS: of 165 statuses a hero can apply, 23 are ENGINE-OWNED, 112 SPEC-EXCLUSIVE, 26 SHARED (13 only through a class-wide card, 13 across specs) and 4 FREE (broken, class_passive, fortify, hexed). 5 more can never be applied (focus, melted, rampage, seeding, ward) and 2 are enemy-only — 172 rows in the table below.
  • BURN IS THE PYROMANCER'S — his engine's currency, nearly every source his — with named exceptions: the Survivalist's Choking Smoke (a deliberate cross-spec feeder), Umbral Mirror's reflections, and two enemies.
  • POISON IS NOT “NOBODY'S”. Every hero-side path is the Survivalist's — his cards, his passive's barb, his lane, the Second Barb rune, and Quartermaster, which puts HIS poison on other heroes' basic attacks — and CLAUDE.md says so as a standing rule (BA §1): “POISON ITSELF IS NOT RESERVED AND STAYS ENTIRELY THE SURVIVALIST'S.” Four enemy kinds apply it too.
  • WHAT “FREE” MEANS WHEN THE LINE MEETS THE CHARTER. The line forbids an engine's status; the standing DO/DP charter forbids depending on a status “the spec has no guaranteed way to apply”. After the merge a spec-exclusive status arrives with that spec's cards, which are drawn — so the 113 are legal by the line and bets by the charter, unless every spec of a class can apply them. The class-wide cards' statuses are free within one class and still a bet on the card being offered (nothing guarantees a hero any class-wide card — FP premise 12).
  • AND THE WAY AROUND ALL OF IT ALREADY SHIPS: READ STATUSES BY COUNT. Overwhelm, Iron Will, Pleasure from Pain, Dominant Presence and Field Medic name no status; they read DEBUFF_IDS. That is the shape a merged tree can use without touching any owner (SR-STATUS).

SR-LINE · C · Is the BASIC ATTACK an ability?

By the letter, yes — slot 0 holds an Ability like any other. In spirit it is universal: every hero has one, every live basic carries pressure, the delay ladder is written against BASIC_DELAY, and a basic resolves at a fixed Good. But the code carries three definitions of it, not one:

DefinitionWhereWhat it catches
ab == u.abilities[0]the skill check's no-bar test (battle.gd:4047-4048), the Sharpshooter's sequence (:23304-23306), the botslot 0 — which four specs override (Fireball, Frostbolt, Arcane Explosion, Shadowrend), so a class's basic is not one card
ab.cost == 0Coated Blades (battle.gd:11981)any free ability — Blood Offering, Blood Price, Reckless Abandon and Divine Plea among them
ab.damage > 0 and ab.cost == 0 and not is_counterQuartermaster (battle.gd:12061-12063)any free damaging cast

Two nodes already key on “your basic attack” — Coated Blades and Quartermaster, both Poison, both the Survivalist's. A ruling that a node may touch the basic attack also has to say which of the three it means, because they are three different populations.

SR-LINE · D · Is an ITEM an ability?

No, by construction: an item is an ITEM_INFO entry resolved by an arm of _use_item, never an Ability object. “It has a handler and a cost” — the handler holds; the cost is a gold price and one item a turn, and an item costs no turn and no resource. It shares machinery with abilities (revive(), the cleanse derivation, next_time) and owns two statuses outright (fortify, hexed). By EN §4 the pouch is relic territory; USING an item in a fight is read mid-turn, which is the talent test. The charter's guarantee rule names abilities and statuses; an item is neither, so whether a node keyed on using one is a bet is a reading, not a rule.

SR-LINE · E · Is BREAK an engine?

No passive's accrual or payout reads the meter — checked across all twelve. The Breaker lane is a TALENT lane (the Swordmaster's nine nodes), not a passive. Fault Line and Turn the Blade generate Break; Bring It Down does not — it is a damage buff (battle.gd:9610-9611). So “no passive owns it” holds. But Break is wired into four engines and one spine (SR-BREAK · 5), and its participation is uneven by EP §2's standing measurement: the Devout drafts no pressure at all.

SR-LINE · F · Edges the brief did not list, found in the code

  1. IS A CLASS PASSIVE A PASSIVE? Four exist: Threatening Presence (the Warrior — the 1.2 threat literal), Holy Conduit (the Cleric — healing received 1.15), Tracker (the Hunter — the opening move), Evocation (the Mage — +10 Mana a turn). By the letter, yes. And two of them assign over fields a stat node would naturally write, so a node there is silently replaced on that class whatever the ruling.
  2. IS A CLASS SPINE AN ENGINE? The proposal calls each a “CORE ENGINE”; FT calls them class cores and ledgers. By the letter a node may not touch Momentum, Channel, Sanctity or Focus — and all four read universal traffic. Channel books every Mana a Mage spends, so a discount or a refund lowers it; Momentum books an exchange whenever a Warrior deals and is reached; Sanctity books every status applied or removed on anyone; Focus is the Hunter's. “May not touch an engine” cannot mean “may not change what an engine reads” without excluding most of this page for three classes. The ruling is which it means.
  3. IS THE CLASS RESOURCE AN ENGINE? Not on the list, and FP's survivors lean on it. But the spend door is Blood Frenzy's and Channel's input — question 2 arriving through the resource.
  4. IS A RUNE-NAMED FIELD A RUNE? EM's charter: “THE FIELD NAME IS THE RULE: rune_X BESIDE X.” Three idle fields a node could write today are rune-named (rune_lifesteal, rune_execute_bonus, rune_resist_pierce) — out by the letter; their non-rune twins are one field each.
  5. A SHARED FIELD IS NOT A TOUCHED RUNE. Nine fields are written by both layers (check_em.UNIT_MATH), and EN §4 separates the layers by scope, not by field.
  6. A COMPANION IS NOT AN ENGINE BY BEING AN ALLY. A node that reaches “allies” reaches a companion under the ally word; anything that summons, or pays for a summon, is the Beastmaster's exclusive axis (DR §1).

§4 — SR-COUNT — HOW MUCH IS ACTUALLY THERE

THE ANSWER, NOT SOFTENED: 274 IS REACHABLE AS A COUNT AND NOT AS A NUMBER OF DIFFERENT THINGS.

Under the designer's line there are about 159 distinct things a node could say across 21 systems. 127 need no ruling, and 50 of those can be said today. A merged tree holds 81 nodes, and today's machinery gives a tree about 50 different things to say — so at least 31 nodes in every tree, 124 of the 324, must be a second magnitude of something the same tree already says. That is the stat creep, measured, before a single hook is built.

Building every small hook ends the forced creep and not the sameness: four trees of 81 drawn from one pool of about 113 ideas are one list dealt four times, and any two of them share at least 49 ideas by arithmetic alone.

DN's sentence priced a restructure at 97 nodes. This page's: the merged layer is being asked for 281 nodes — not 274, because seven of FP's survivors do not survive the line — and it has about 127 things to say.

SR-COUNT · The rule the count was taken by

A distinct thing is a different dial or a different condition. Magnitudes are one thing; one trigger paying in two currencies is one thing; each thing is counted in exactly one section. Costs: TODAY — a field exists, this system reads it, no trap in the way; SMALL — one new field and one read line at a site that already exists; LARGE — a new door, layer, run-side reader, dictionary hook or piece of UI; RULING — the line's edge or a standing rule has to be decided first. This is a judgement applied the same way everywhere, not a measurement; every section lists its things so the count can be taken again.
