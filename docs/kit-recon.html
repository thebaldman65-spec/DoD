DAWN OF DECAY — THE CLASS KIT RECONNAISSANCE

What the four class kits could be made of, read out of the code. This is a REPORT. Nothing was authored, moved, cut, reworded or built — no card was proposed, no clause was cut, no pool, slot, talent node or rune moved, and this page does not pick a kit. It is written to be open while each class's three are chosen, so every class section stands alone and repeats what it needs rather than pointing at another section.

THE RULING, AS THE BRIEF GIVES IT.
“Every class gets a small guaranteed kit. THREE abilities, INSIDE the slot count” — a protected core took a slot, and this is the same object. “Salvage what fits, author the gaps.”

THE HOLE IT ANSWERS. A hero who takes the Warrior's, the Mage's or the Cleric's spine at class selection — the Rune of the Vanguard (Momentum), the Invoker (Channel) or the Hierophant (Sanctity), names proposed at GK — opens with his class's basic attack and nothing else, because a spine carries no lineage. RULED, NOT BUILT: the pool merge, after which no hero has a lineage kit at all.

THE TEST EVERY CARD BELOW IS PUT TO. A class kit ability must work for every hero of its class, with any engine or none. So it must not read an engine; must not read a status only one engine — or only one lineage's cards — produces; and must not need an enabler that travels with an engine.

KR-HOWTO — HOW TO READ A SECTION

Every heading on this page carries its section's identifier in its own text — KR-WARRIOR, KR-MAGE, KR-CLERIC, KR-HUNTER — and every subsection repeats it, systems-recon.html's convention. A search for “The class-wide draft pool” returns KR-CLERIC · 3 · The class-wide draft pool, which says whose it is. Search for the identifier, not the topic word.

Every class section has the same seven numbered parts:

  1. The basic attack — what it is, what it costs, what it does, and whether it is one ability or several.
  2. The engines and what travels with them — each engine rune of the class, its enablers, and the kit each one opens with.
  3. The class-wide draft pool — the cards every hero of the class can draw.
  4. The protected cores that are not enablers — what each lineage opens with beyond what its engine brings.
  5. The test, card by card — one row per candidate.
  6. The count and the cover — how many pass, and what the passing cards could guarantee.
  7. What a pick costs here — what moves when a card leaves the place it is now.

THE THREE VERDICTS, AND THE TWO NOTES.

WordMeans
CLEANReads no engine, no status only an engine or one lineage's cards lay, and needs no enabler. It works alone for every hero of the class. The cheapest salvage there is: the card moves as it stands.
ALMOSTOne clause — or its Perfect — reads an engine and is inert without it. Cut that clause and the card stands alone. A rewording, not an authoring job, with one exception named where it occurs (Hold Breath, which also needs a line of code moved).
BOUNDThe card cannot be cast, or does next to nothing, without its engine or an enabler that travels with it. Salvaging it is authoring.
WORDINGA word on the card or its chip names an engine or a lineage the hero may not hold (“the Devout's max health”). No mechanic is cut; the text is false for some heroes.
FEEDSThe card lays something an engine reads (Burn, Chilled, a debuff) without reading the engine itself. Not a failure: the card works alone, and it is worth more beside that engine.

A BASIC-ATTACK OVERRIDE IS NOT A SLOT ABILITY. Fireball, Frostbolt, Arcane Explosion and Shadowrend replace the class basic in slot 0 and sit outside the slot count (Classes.enabler_slots, classes.gd:1339-1354). They are tested and reported, and counted apart from the slot candidates.

KR-ANSWER — THE ANSWER, BEFORE THE WORKING

TWELVE ABILITIES ARE NEEDED. NONE HAS TO BE WRITTEN. Every class already holds at least nine cards that pass the test clean (eight for the Cleric on the stricter reading KR-TEST records) — 64 candidates across the four classes, 44 clean, 13 a clause away, 7 bound. So it is not twelve to write and not four: it is twelve to PICK. What is scarce is not supply. It is three things the pick moves, and none of them is free:

  1. A class-wide card taken into the kit leaves every hero's draw of that class — and it is a spine-taker's whole supply. A hero with no lineage draws every card of his run from his class's pool alone, the draft and the zone-boss award both (run_state.gd:2202-2214, 3801-3810). That pool is six cards (the Mage's seven). Three taken from it leave him three to earn (the Mage four).
  2. A lineage core taken into the kit is already in its lineage's opening kit until the pool merge retires lineages (GK's interim) — so the builder that adds a class kit owes an answer for the hero who would hold it twice.
  3. The class-wide cards were authored WEAKER than spec abilities, on purpose (“weaker than spec abilities and unconditional”, classes.gd:518-526), and EB §1 ruled that the protected core is the baseline. A kit made of class-wide cards gives the baseline role to the cards written as the fallback.

And the part that is not supply at all: no Hunter opens thin today. The Hunter has no spine, so all three of his engines carry a lineage and its kit. The hole the kit answers is the Warrior's, the Mage's and the Cleric's until the pool merge, and every class's after it.

KR-TEST — THE TEST, AND HOW A CARD WAS PUT TO IT

KR-TEST · What counts as reading an engine

  • A direct read: has_engine(id), or one of the three spine switches.
  • The engine's own meter: the second resource (second_resource, which is Mercy, Resonance or Focus and is set up only for a hero holding that engine — battle.gd:1178-1186, 1347-1368), Faith (paid only while a hero holds Conviction — _gain_faith, battle.gd:16503-16505), Ruin (laid only while a hero holds the Old Gods — _gain_ruin, battle.gd:15958), Loyalty (refused without Pack Bond — _gain_loyalty, battle.gd:14533), a Glacial HOLD (recorded only under the engine — battle.gd:12742) and a stance's effect (read only under has_engine("seasoned") — battle.gd:9790, 10320).
  • An enabler's presence: a living companion (only the Pack Bond summons bring one), Consecrated Ground's status on the ground.
  • NOT a read: the class resource (Rage or Mana); a status any card of the class can lay; an engine's payout on the card's number — Overburn's damage term, Mercy's +5% healing a stack — which is the engine reading the card, and pays on every card alike.

KR-TEST · What a party damage buff reaches

Warcry, Exhortation, Blessing of Zeal and Hunter's Mark are read in the ordinary strike path (battle.gd:9689, 8423-8425, 9240, 9892) — and Hunter's Mark in a companion's blow as well (battle.gd:22833). An ability that resolves in its own special handler computes its own damage and reads none of them, which FW measured as about seventy per cent of the corpus. So “every hero deals 20% more damage” is true of the blows that pass the strike loop. It is true of all four cards alike, and it is written here once rather than on each row.

KR-TEST · The precedent, applied

FY's SR-REACH found that TODAY in the systems recon meant a field exists and a read site reads it, for some hero, and never said which. The same slip is available here: a card is “engine-free” in a lineage whose hero always holds the engine. So every card below was read for a hero of the class holding no engine at all — a legal state since GK (Run.toggle_engine) — and for one holding a different engine of the class. The column that says what a card does for a hero with no engine is the one the verdict rests on.

KR-TEST · Where the two readings disagreed

  • Arcane Explosion — resolved toward the census pass. Its “Builds 1 Resonance” is the build every damaging cast pays a Resonance holder (battle.gd:12418-12457), not a clause of the card. Cutting the words cuts no mechanic, so it is CLEAN with a WORDING note, not ALMOST.
  • Ministration — kept CLEAN; the census pass read ALMOST. Its heal is multiplied by _healing_done_mult, which adds Mercy's +5% a stack (battle.gd:14727). That term is the engine's own payout on every heal (Mercy's text says so), and a Cleric without Mercy gets exactly the card's 20%. Read the stricter way, the cut is a term the engine applies to every heal, which is not the card's to lose.
  • Divine Shield — kept CLEAN; the census pass read ALMOST. The barrier stamps a hidden divine rider that only Faith and two Devout cards read (unit.gd:3396-3398), and the recast table counts the rider as an improvement (battle.gd:6069, 6104-6106). Without Conviction the rider pays nothing and harms nothing, and the card's text never mentions it: it FEEDS Faith. Its WORDING note stands either way.
  • Quick Shot — the same fact, filed differently. The Hunter pass read the class basic as ALMOST for its unreachable Perfect; this page reports the basics apart and records the same fact in each class's part 1.
  • Every other card read the same way twice. Read the stricter way, the Cleric would hold eight CLEAN rather than ten. No count that decides anything moves: nothing has to be authored either way.

KR-CLASSES — THE FOUR, ONE SECTION EACH

KR-WARRIOR — THE WARRIOR

Rage is the Warrior's class resource (classes.gd:61): every Warrior starts a fight at 0 and builds it. It is not an engine, so a card that builds or spends Rage passes the test. Taking the Rune of the Vanguard (Momentum) at class selection opens with Strike alone — the hole this kit answers.

KR-WARRIOR · 7 · What a pick costs here

  • From the class pool: every card taken is one a Vanguard-taker can no longer earn. Six today; three if all three come from it. His run's supply stays six — three of them guaranteed from the first fight.
  • From a lineage core: a Berserker, Warden or Swordmaster already opens with his own three. A kit that includes one of them would hand that hero a second copy unless the builder refuses it.
  • Slots: a kit of three inside the cap of seven. A Vanguard-taker opens at 3 of 7; a Berserker or Warden at 6 of 7; a Swordmaster at 5 of 7 (lineage_slots + 3, no overlap) — until the pool merge.
  • The sim bot casts Bloodlust, Wildstrikes, Mocking Blow, Shieldwall and Guard Change only inside its engine branches (battle.gd:4656-4760). A class kit owes it a class-level branch, or a sim never exercises the kit.
  • Texts the code contradicts, on these cards: Charge's Daze never acts (removed at the start of the target's own turn, battle.gd:3295, before its miss roll reads it, 7981) and its Perfect (“Dazed for 2 turns”) has no code; Cleave's Perfect (“{atk:22}”) has no code; Battle Trance's “each of your turns” pays three times in four, because the heal (battle.gd:3371) runs after the status ticks (3295); Shieldwall's chip calls its blocks Heavy Plating blocks; Strike's Perfect.

KR-MAGE — THE MAGE

Mana is the Mage's class resource, regenerated at 22 a turn — 12 plus Evocation, the Mage class passive. Taking the Rune of the Invoker (Channel) at class selection opens with Magic Bolt alone. No class-wide Mage card lays Burn or Chilled: both are laid only by the Pyromancer's and the Cryomancer's cards, so a card that pays off either needs one of those beside it in the same kit.

KR-MAGE · 1 · The basic attack

Magic Bolt — free, 25% of Attack (arcane), 14 Break damage, 2.0 initiative (classes.gd:112-118). ONE SLOT, FOUR DEFINITIONS: Magic Bolt while no Mage lineage engine is held; Fireball while Overburn is, Frostbolt while Glacial Hold is, Arcane Explosion while Resonance is (classes.gd:5398-5418). A carried engine's override takes slot 0 while Magic Bolt stands there and joins as a second free attack otherwise (classes.gd:1319-1331), so a Mage holding two of those engines opens with two free attacks. Slot 0 resolves at a fixed Good (battle.gd:4118-4119): Magic Bolt's Perfect (“Restores 10 Mana”) never pays.
