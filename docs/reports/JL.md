## §1 — A SPEND IS A FREE ACTION

**THE RULE** (`scripts/battle.gd`: `_declare_intent`, `_warband_free_spend`, `_enemy_turn`; `check_jl` §1 drives it): **a body that buys a spend still takes its turn.** The spend rides the body's own declaration beside its action — chosen after the action, so after every heal and ward, JK's order (`_warband_spend`, asked by `_declare_intent`) — and only where the pool can pay it beside the action's own reserve (`_enemy_ready`'s new `held`); it sets its own price aside there (`_malice_free` reads the rider); and it is bought first on the body's turn: the door's refusals asked again (the list, the cooldown, Heads Down, the Long Silence), a Raise's body asked still down and unraised, its cooldown started, its arm in `_resolve_special` paying — **and no delay charged**, so the body then acts as declared, at its own action's delay. **One rides a declaration, so one a turn.** A stun, a freeze or a hijack discards both; a wind-up's turn declares none; a taunt keeps a body from buying, as it kept it from the support half (JL's RULINGS OWED 4). **The Rally waits for two standing bodies** (`MALICE_RALLY_BODIES` 3 → 2). **Nothing else of JK's moved**: the three moves on their kinds' lists, the prices, the cooldowns, the reserve, the declaration, the order.

**PREMISE 1 — ONE STORE, TWO NAMES.** The intent is one dictionary a body; it carries the spend as a second entry. The plate is one label that trims rather than overruns (`clip_text`, the ellipsis), so it names both, the spend first — *▲ Rally · → Slash* — and the hover begins *First, free: Rally* and its words, then *Then:* the action's (JL's RULINGS OWED 1). **What the player sees is the spend coming**, a turn ahead, on the body that will buy it. **Measured** (`tools/jl_plate_measure.gd`, a scratch probe on `check_jl`'s board: every kind's every spend beside every move it owns, 61 pairings, each line built by the battle's own `_refresh_intent_plate` and measured in the label's own font against its 126 px at font 9; `logs/plate_final.log`): every Rally and Raise pairing fits (the widest, *▲ Rally · ⇉ Immolating Wave*, 124 px), and eight Entrench pairings run past it — beside the Hollow Crown's Sundering Decree (147), Crown of Thorns (141) and The Long Dark (129), the Grave Totem's Grasping Roots (135), the Orc Shaman's Chain Lightning (134), the Orc Ritual Chanter's Warding Chant (134) and Cleansing Rite (131), and the Withered Warden's Roots of Wrath (131) — and trim the move's last letters there, the hover naming both whole. **The first draft's figures were wrong**: read by hand with the strike's arrow on every move, they named four trims and *Sundering Strike* as the widest Rally line; the plate writes that move's category, *● Sunder*, and the kept measure finds eight.

**PREMISE 2 — THE RESERVE STAYS.** A spend is still declared a turn before it lands, so the reserve is reachable for spends as for heals, and nothing in it is dead. **One hazard was new**: a body whose action is a heal and which buys a spend first could have bought with its own heal's Malice, since `_malice_free` leaves out the asker; `_enemy_ready`'s `held` counts the action's own reserve beside the spend's price (`check_jl` §1 drives a Totemist one point short of both: the heal, and no Raise). **A second, found by the fresh reader**: the body's turn re-validated its declaration — clearing the heal's reserve — before it bought the spend, so a body a Raise rider stood up, which declares at once, could set that heal's Malice aside and the heal landed short (reachable on 15 turns of the second arm). **The turn now takes the rider off the declaration and buys it before re-validation** (`_enemy_turn`), the heal's reserve held through the buy; `check_jl` §1 drives a Totemist's Healing Wave with a Raise riding at exactly both prices — the heal restores its whole 68, where the old order landed 28 — and C02b reverts the order. It moved the drive's dice, so the arm of record is the third (§3). **One branch died**: JK's Raise case in `_revalidate_intent` (a Raise's target is down by design) — a spend is never the declared action now, so nothing reaches it; deleted and named (JL's RULINGS OWED 2).

**PREMISE 3 — FOUR BRAKES, AND THE BANDS PRICED A TURN NO SPEND GIVES UP.** One spend a turn (one rider); its cooldown, 2 to 4 of its kind's turns; the policy's *none rallied or declared* and *none entrenched or declared*, so one live Rally and one live Entrench a warband at most; and the pool — which seldom binds, 76.8% of JL's fights ending at its cap. **JK's bands priced each spend against the basic it displaced**, which no spend displaces now: what would be re-derived is a spend's worth against what the pool pays for it, or the pool's income a fight. **Not re-derived**, as the brief ordered (JL's RULINGS OWED 3). A buyer strikes rallied three times now, since it buys before its own blow: nine rallied blows on four bodies where JK priced eight.

## §2 — FOUR HEXES, AND THEY TAKE CAPACITY

**THE SENTENCE** (`scripts/unit.gd`'s `GALL_HEAL_CUT`, `SAP_CUT`, `MIRE_SLOW`; `scripts/battle.gd`'s `DOT_STATUSES`; the clocks in `data/enemies.json`): Gall cuts the healing a hero receives by 50% for 3 turns; Sap cuts what a hero's resource bar gains by 50% for 3 turns; Mire makes a hero act 25% slower for 3 turns; Fester deals 3% of its applier's Attack a stack at each of the hero's turn starts and gains a stack each turn, until it is cleansed.

**EACH A MOVE ON ITS KIND's LIST** (special `warband_hex`, its status the move's own `applies_status`), laid through the status door with the enemy that cast it as its source. **Who carries which** (`docs/reports/JL-hexes.tsv`; `check_jl` §2 derives the requirement off the data): the Orc Hexer Gall and Sap; the Grave Totem Mire; the Rot Behemoth Fester — three of the five kinds the data names `hexer`; the Orc Archer and the Orc Bloodcaller carry none, and the Archer meets the heroes most (265 of JL's 946 fights) (JL's RULINGS OWED 5). **The selection** puts a ready hex some hero in reach lacks beside the attacks it picks from at random, as it always picked the afflictions, and aims one at a hero who does not carry it — never a companion, which takes no turn and holds no bar; no draw or evasion moves it once aimed, and as a `special` it rolls no miss (JL's RULINGS OWED 7). Each carries a support cast's 2.5 delay, the afflictions' own. **The word**: the four are called *hexes* beside the status Hexed and the Hexer, kept and flagged as Rally was (JL's RULINGS OWED 12). The attack half's `damage > 0` would never have reached a move that deals nothing.

**PREMISE 1 — THE CLEANSES.** All four are in `DEBUFF_IDS` and none is sticky, so all three filters reach them: `_cleansable_debuffs` (the Cleansing Draught, the bot's aim), `purge_debuffs_taken` (Unburden, an Empowered Holy Heal or Divine Plea) and `dispel_one_debuff` (Dispel, Field Dressing, the Medic, the cleanse talent). **The Cleric's Unburden is the answer every run holds**, a kit card; `check_jl` §2 drives it lifting a Fester, and holds the absence (C07b strikes it from the kit).
