**IN PLAIN WORDS.** *The warband* is the enemy side; *Malice* is its shared bar, filled by the heroes' damage and kills. **Until now only an enemy healer could spend it, and two fights in three held none, so the bar filled and did nothing. Now every enemy can spend it**: some stand a fallen ally back up, some make the whole enemy side hit harder for a few turns, some make it tougher. Over sixty paired runs the enemies spent nearly three times as much Malice, but **fights were not measurably harder** — a spend costs an enemy its turn, the blow it would have struck, and Malice spent on a spend no longer pays for a heal. Nothing was tuned; that is the designer's call.

## §1 — THREE SPENDS, AND EVERY KIND CAN BUY ONE

**THE SENTENCE** (`scripts/battle.gd`, seven constants beside the pool; `scripts/unit.gd`'s `ENTRENCH_MULT`): A Raise stands a fallen body of the warband back up at 40% of its maximum health, once a body a fight, for the health it stands it up with; a Rally makes every standing body of the warband deal 30% more damage with its blows for 3 turns, for 40 Malice; an Entrench multiplies their armor by 2 for 3 turns, for 30 Malice. **The pool buys health at one Malice a point; everything else has a flat price.**

**THE SHAPE, THE CHEAP ONE.** Each spend is a move on its kind's list in `data/enemies.json` (specials `malice_raise`, `malice_rally`, `malice_entrench`, aimed at its own side). **The support half chooses it last** (`_warband_spend`, called at the foot of `_enemy_support_action`), after every heal and ward, so JJ's healers' policy stands untouched; it asks `_enemy_ready` for its cooldown and for its price on the body it names, exactly as a heal does, so **a declared spend sets its price aside** through JJ's reserve and re-validation asks no pool. Its arm in `_resolve_special` pays. The intent plate shows it as a bolster, named — *▲ Rally* over the body that bought it. **The policy** (JK's RULINGS OWED 4): a Raise on the largest fallen body the pool can pay to stand up; a Rally while three or more bodies stand and none is rallied or has declared one (each strikes rallied twice, so three buy 1.8 of a basic blow for a spend that takes 1.25 of a basic's turn); an Entrench while none is entrenched or has declared one. **Each takes 2.5**, the warband's support casts' delay. A hero's Dispel strips a body's Rally and Entrenched, as it strips a ward — it takes three beneficial effects. **The brief's *neither needs a condition* is the spend's, not the policy's**: a Rally-only kind buys nothing once two or fewer of its warband stand.

**EVERY KIND HOLDS ONE — THE REQUIREMENT, DERIVED.** `check_jk` §1 walks every kind off the data: a move the pool prices that the intent's own classifier does not call a mend, a Rally or an Entrench on every list (the floor), and — on a board where the pool is full, a body is down and nothing is hurt, warded or rallied — every kind's own selection declaring one. All twenty-one do. **Who holds what** (`docs/reports/JK-spends.tsv`, JK's RULINGS OWED 3): a Rally on thirteen — the damage kinds and the Hexer; an Entrench on five — the three tanks, the Orc Shaman and the Hollow Crown; a Raise and an Entrench on three — the Orc Totemist, the Ritual Chanter and the Grave Totem. **Of the eleven kinds that owned no cooling move, all eleven hold a spend now** — the figure JL is sized against — and since every spend cools, no kind is left without a cooling move; their attacks are still a basic and something weaker.

**PREMISE 1 — NO SUMMON PATH, SO NO OVERRUN — RULED, NOT BUILT.** The warband is added to in one place, `_spawn_units`, laid out by its size (`ENEMY_LAYOUTS[n]`), plated, zoned, stamped with the bargain and the runes and given its opening turn there; a companion is the only body that joins a fight in flight, and it joins off the order. **What an Overrun would take**: a place in the turn order (a `next_time` at the back of it and the turn bar redrawn), a plate and a layout slot past the six the field holds, the spawn's scaling, the bargain's and the runes' stamps, its declaration, and a decision on what a summoned body's death pays the heroes. `check_jk` §1 holds the absence both ways: no function but `_spawn_units` adds to the warband, and the state's record says there is no Overrun and what it would take (JK's RULINGS OWED 1).

**PREMISE 2 — RAISE RISES UN-BROKEN.** `revive` leaves `broken` alone, and needs to: `_die()` lays a body's Break down with it and `take_hit` lays none on a body that is down (JD §3). `check_jk` §1 fells a body with the blow that Breaks it, raises it, and reads it standing, un-Broken, at its share of its health, the pool paying exactly that health; fallen again, neither its selection nor the move stands it up a second time. **Beyond `revive`**: the declaration it died with goes (it carried a reserve), its plate redraws what is still cooling, its first turn comes a basic swing after it rises, at its own speed (Resurrection's own line), and re-validation, which re-aims a declaration whose target is dead, leaves a Raise's fallen target alone — a fallen body is the move's own (`_revalidate_intent`'s case 1).

**PREMISE 3 — RALLY REACHES NO HEAL.** Read once, in the strike loop beside Divine Wrath and Zeal; a warband heal's figure is a share of the body healed (`_warband_heal_figure`) and never passes that line. `check_jk` §1 drives it: the warband's blow on its own body lands 1.31 times as hard under a Rally (95 → 124; the rule's 1.30, after rounding), and a rallied Totemist's Healing Wave restores exactly its figure, is priced at it, sets it aside and pays it — the reserve's arithmetic standing. **It reaches blows, not the Burn and Poison the warband lays** (FOUND AT JK). **Its clock is 3**: a status counts down at its bearer's turn start, before it acts, so a body strikes rallied on one turn fewer than its clock, and 3 is the brief's *two of its turns* (JK's RULINGS OWED 5).

**PREMISE 4 — ENTRENCH HAS ONE DOOR.** `effective_armor()`, beside Bulwark's own multiplier and under the 0.85 ceiling — the highest warband armor, the Withered Warden's 0.35, doubles to 0.70. `check_jk` §1 reads the door double a body's armor (0.150 → 0.300) and a blow on that body land at the door's figure (46 → 38, the door's 37.9); the code reads the status at two sites, the door and the policy's one ask.
