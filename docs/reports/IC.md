## §1 — THE FIVE READERS OF A BLOW THAT NEVER LANDED

### §1a — THE VOW IS LIVE. §1 IS BUILT WHOLE AND PRICED AS A LIVE RUNE.

**Its writer** is its own payload — `stat: rune_vow_of_silence 0.5` in `data/runes.json`, applied at the spawn. **Its pool** is every
Cleric's: scope `class:cleric`, no `retired`, not refused (`Talents.live_refusal` passes a payload with no condition), no
`ENGINE_READ` row and no `requires_ability`. **Nothing retired it.** Driven (`check_ic` §1a): at a fresh run it is in the eligible
pool of all three lineages (17 runes for the Holy Cleric, 18 for the Inquisitor and the Occultist, core runes and the crest's four
included), and in 600 seeded draws each door offers it — the

unworn, worn from the holding, and **on the Cleric when the fight spawns** (`rune_vow_of_silence` 0.5). Unlike Lifewell at IA, a new run
reaches it.

### §1b — ONE QUESTION A STRIKE: FEASIBLE, WITH ONE EXCEPTION NAMED

**The single question is feasible at the top of the strike.** `_vow_silences(victim)` reads the frame's dealer and the victim's side,
and both are fixed for a strike: the frame is the attacker's (`_resolve` sets it at entry, the miss branch re-sets it, and every site
in the swing that borrows it — Feint's return, the ground's reflect, Spite — puts it back before the door), and the victim is the
strike's target. So `var silenced := _vow_silences(strike_target)` is asked once, right after the target is fixed and the miss rolls
are passed, **above the computation**.

**The computation still runs whole**: it spends statuses and banks that belong to the cast (Exhortation, Opening, Aegis Reversal's
bonus, the Bastion's bank). Only the figure is withheld — by **one branch** standing above the first reader:
`var refused := silenced and final > 0`, and `final = 0` when it holds. Every reader below reads the blow that landed: the recap's
credit, `total_dealt`, the Ruin leech, the door (handed 0, so the gate floats nothing and the strike floats SILENT itself), the float
and the line.

**THE ONE EXCEPTION: WHETHER A BLOW REACHED ITS BODY IS NOT ITS FIGURE.** Below the branch, `final > 0` meant two things: *the damage
this hit dealt* and *the blow was not missed, blocked or absolutely parried*. A silenced blow is the second without the first — its
Break lands (HF's ruling) — so a zero blow everywhere would also have stopped **Anointing's Ruin** riding a vowed Occultist's own hits
(he anoints the party, himself included). That is a magnitude the vow does not name, so it is not changed (CQ §6): `connected`
(`final > 0`, read before the branch) is what Anointing reads (ruling 4). Every other `final > 0` below the branch a Cleric can reach
reads a figure — Umbral Sigil's echo (half of what landed, now nothing) — and those that only another class reaches (Drumfire, Arcane
Arrows, Arcane Echo, Crossfire) are unchanged for a hero who cannot wear the vow.

**Asked under the frame that credits the blow, and a borrowed frame asks for itself**: the ground's reflect inside a raider's swing is
the layer's (HY §1c), asks `_vow_silences` under its own frame (IA's line), and never inherits the strike's answer — driven both ways
in §1c(d), where the swing's answer is *not silenced* and the reflect's is *silenced*.

The rule is written beside IA's in `docs/combat-rules.md`'s frame block, completing it: *a figure printed or paid off a blow is the
blow that landed, never the blow that was computed — and the cheapest way to obey it is to know before you compute.*
