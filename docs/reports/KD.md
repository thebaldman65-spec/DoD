## §0 — WHAT THE RULINGS DECIDE, AND WHAT THEY LEAVE

**The designer's**: a relic belongs to one hero (CX, standing since CX and RULED AND NOT BUILT until KD); the after-victory heal and Mana, the fallen hero's recovery and Bottled Storm's opening floor stay every hero's, and each relic is handed to a hero after the classes are chosen, on the first map (JY's ruling 12, accepted at JZ); the six hooks that set up the run — the purse, the pouch, gold found, the shop's prices, the elite's spoils and the victory's gold — were party-wide and staying so before CX (CX §2's *as ruled*). **And the figures**: the twelve stat relics pay their holder twice what each paid every hero (*Double them*, KD's question; the other two options were *Keep today's figures* and *Four times*).

**What was left, and KD took (NEEDS A RULING, six readings)**: a relic whose hooks all pay every hero is handed to nobody; one hero may hold several; a holder is kept for the run; the hand-out has no Later; an owed relic pays nobody wherever the run resumes; the bot spreads its relics. **KD opens no balance question**: the only figure the build moved is the designer's.

## §1 — THE HOLDER: EIGHT HOOKS, TWELVE RELICS, TWICE THE FIGURE

- **`Relics.HOLDER_HOOKS`** (`scripts/relics.gd`) is the eight battle-spawn stat hooks — `hero_attack_mult`, `dmg_type_mult`, `hero_max_hp_mult`, `hero_armor_add`, `hero_speed_add`, `hero_crit_add`, `hero_con_add`, `hero_resist_add` — the hooks CX found already ran once per hero. **`Relics.is_held(id)`** is true when any hook of a relic is one of them: twelve of the twenty-five (Emberheart, Frostbound Sigil, Nightbloom Charm, Ancestral Whetstone, Ironbark Ward, Fleetstep Talisman, Oxheart Fetish and Wardrums of the Old Horde, common; Dragonbone Idol, Runed Bulwark, Prism Veil and Horn of the First War, rare). **No relic carries a holder hook beside an every-hero hook** — `check_kd` §1 asserts it entry by entry, so a relic written later is one kind or the other.
- **The spawn** (`battle._spawn_units`) reads hero *i*'s own relics: its eight stat reads take `Run.hero_relic_add(i, …)` / `hero_relic_dict(i, …)`, the sums over `Run.hero_relics(i)` through the aggregators every read already used (`Relics.hook_add` / `hook_dict`, their signatures unchanged). Bottled Storm's floor stays `Run.relic_add` — every hero's, ruled. **`Run.relic_add` and `relic_dict` refuse a holder hook** (an error and 0), so a party-wide read of a held stat cannot come back. A held relic nobody holds pays nobody.
- **The figures, the designer's**: Emberheart, Frostbound Sigil and Nightbloom Charm +40% (both schools each, from 20%); Ancestral Whetstone +10% critical chance (5%); Ironbark Ward +10% armor (5%); Fleetstep Talisman +10 Speed (5); Oxheart Fetish +20% maximum health (10%); Wardrums +30 Constitution (15); Dragonbone Idol +20% strike damage (10%); Runed Bulwark 20% physical resistance (10%); Prism Veil 20% resistance to every element (10%); Horn of the First War +24% strike damage (12%). Every text reads its figure through its token, so the words moved with the figures. `check_kd` §2 holds each against HEAD's figure doubled — the relation is the ruling, so the table is the before.
- **The words**: each held relic says *The hero holding it* (`hero`, §4.9's word for one of the four; no pronoun for a hero) — twelve texts. CX counted thirteen texts worded party-wide; KD rewords nine of them and the three school relics, whose texts named nobody, and the four on JY's ruling 12's hooks keep their words. `check_kd` §3 holds every text: a held relic names its holder, an every-hero one does not, none says *All heroes*.
- **The header of `relics.gd`** says who a stat hook pays, and its claim that every hook is read at one site — FOUND AT FW's first item — is mended and the item archived.

## §2 — THE HAND-OUT, ON THE MAP

- **`Run.relics_owed()`** is the held relics carried and handed to nobody, in the order carried; **`Run.hand_relic(id, idx)` is the one door a holder is written through** — the map's buttons and the bot's policy both — and refuses a relic not carried, not held or already handed, and an index that is no hero's (`check_kd` §4 drives each refusal). `Run.relic_holders` keeps `{relic id: party index}`.
- **The overlay** (`map_screen._open_relic_handout`, z 66, an arrival modal): the relic's name and words, *It pays only the hero holding it, for the rest of the run. Hand it to which hero?*, and a button a hero naming the relics he already holds. One relic at a time; the press hands it, saves, toasts, and the walk goes on. **It opens first in the arrival walk** (`_arrival_next`), before any hero's pick: the relics were carried before any pick was earned. **No Later** (KD's reading 4).
- **The framing card** covers the first map's arrival on a profile's first run, and its comment said *nothing is owed then* — false from KD, so *Take the road* now begins the walk when the map was an arrival. `check_kd` §7 drives both: the hand-out up on arrival, two relics to one hero one after the other, an every-hero relic opening nothing, and the card's dismissal starting the hand-out.
- **An event's relic grant** (`events.gd`) adds a held relic unhanded and says *— handed to one hero on the map*; the next arrival hands it.
- **What a hero holds is named on his map card's tooltip** (*Relic held:* and its words) and in the run's report (*Relics carried: Dragonbone Idol (Swordmaster)*); the readout's header carries each relic's holder index.

## §3 — THE SAVE, v17

`relic_holders` is saved beside `active_relics` (whose shape does not move) and read with a default of `{}`, then repaired (`_repair_relic_holders`): an entry for a relic not carried or not held, or an index that is no hero's, is dropped and said in `load_notes`. **`SAVE_VERSION` 16 → 17**, the destructive case a bump is for (IB §0.2): without it a v16 build would read the save and write the holders away on its next save; with it, a v16 build refuses the save and keeps it (HL §5). **A v16 save loads with its held relics owed** and hands them at the next map arrival; a save resumed into a fight fights it with them paying nobody (KD's reading 5 — `Run.resume_scene` reaches the map only when nothing is pending). The floor stays at 10. `check_is` §2's census of the save's keys sorts the new key; `check_kd` §6 round-trips the holders, loads a v16-shaped file with them owed, and drops a stale entry and says so. `check_kb` §3 pinned the version literal (16), which the instrument rules forbid a suite; it reads the relation now (≥ 16).
