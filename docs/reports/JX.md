**THE VERDICT: DONE — GREEN BUT FOR THE TWO CARRIED REDS.**
- **Any zone stands at any stage.** A run is three stages and then the end boss; each stage draws among the zones not yet drawn, so a run holds three different zones of the four — one of 24 shapes, every zone in three runs of four. **The stage scales the difficulty**: every body's health and Attack are its stage's multiple and its tier's, whichever zone stands there, as they already were, while each zone's boss and kinds bring their own base figures; what JX removed is what tied a zone to a place — `SLOT_POOLS` and the per-slot rosters.
- **One roster a zone**: the Forest's finale roster folded into its opener's, the Frozen Plains' the same — 24 kinds re-tagged, `docs/reports/JX-rosters.tsv` the record of the fold and the one door the gates that measure an earlier warband read it back through.
- **The Bone Shepherd swings Bone Smash** (the old basic's figures, physical); **Soul Leech is an ability** — 46 damage at JI's line (1.42), cooling 2, its paid heal as before; Noxious Fumes and Call the Drowned stand; the borrowed Rally is gone.
- **JK's floor is gone**, by the designer's ruling: no gate asserts that every kind holds a Rally or an Entrench, and `check_jk` §1 prints the kinds holding neither — the Shepherd alone.
- **JW's eight rulings are answered** (1, 5, 6, 7 and 8 accepted; 2 by the stages; 3 reversed in its first half; 4 amended) and archived; **JX's seven are owed** (NEEDS A RULING), the first the zone bosses' base figures, which the stage multiplies but which still differ by zone.
