# Batch KH — The Orcs to the Scarlands, and the Forest of Old's Own Seven

**THE RECORD ARRIVED AS THE DESIGNER's WORDS IN CONVERSATION, NOT AS A DECISION RECORD, SAID HERE FIRST**: KG's close left the content question KF had put beside its balance one open — whether the Forest of Old and the Scarlands go on sharing their Orcs, and the Ash Hurler's zone — and the designer answered it (25 November): *"lets move the orcs to scarlands and lets come up with enemies for the forest of old"*. KH put four questions after it, each with its options and the recommendation first, and each came back as the recommendation — the Orcs' four companions *"Behemoth, Totem stay"*, the Forest's new kinds *"These seven"*, the figures *"Each copies its Orc"*, the damage type and resists *"Nature, like the Forest"*. `RECORD_KH.md` in KH's work folder holds the ask, the facts KH stated before the questions, every question as put with every option and its preview, and the answers, verbatim. **Lane A, the whole floor**: KH adds seven enemy kinds and moves what two zones field. **The record carries no lane, names no source and asks no open question**, so KH was run on what it carries, the lane its own (§0).

**THE VERDICT: DONE — GREEN BUT FOR THE TWO CARRIED REDS.**
- **The Orcs stand in the Scarlands alone.** Every Orc kind, the Wolfrider and the Ash Hurler are tagged for roster 2 (the Scarlands) alone, and the Rot Behemoth and the Grave Totem for roster 1 (the Forest of Old) alone (the designer's answer 1), so **no two zones share a kind but the Hollow Crown**; the Orc Whelp and the Orc Totemist, the Forest's alone before, stand in the Scarlands for the first time. **A run that draws no Scarlands — about one in four — meets no Orc.**
- **The Forest of Old fields seven kinds of its own** beside the Bog Troll, the Rot Behemoth, the Grave Totem and the Withered Warden, each the Orc it replaces in every figure (answer 3) — the **Thornling** (the Whelp), the **Bristleback** (the Wolfrider), the **Spore Lobber** (the Archer), the **Heartwood Sentinel** (the Shieldmaster), the **Grove Tender** (the Shaman), the **Blightcap** (the Hexer) and the **Antlered Huntmaster** (the Chief, the Forest's elite) — every damaging move renamed and dealt in nature, each resisting nature 50% and soft to fire (answer 4). Shielding, Healing Wave, Rally and Entrench keep their names. The kinds a roster holds: **1: 12, 2: 17, 4: 9, 6: 10**.
- **No band moved and no figure was chosen by KH**: every figure is its Orc's, by the designer's answer 3, which named the cooldowns; the three the line would price otherwise are kept where the combat rules keep a designer's figure — the Huntmaster's Maul copy uncooled in `check_ji`'s `TUNED`, the Grove Tender's Entrench and the Bristleback's Rally at their originals' 2 in `HELD`. The reference board moves from 130.0 and 37.025 to 127.5 (KF's again) and 36.984 (`check_jk` §2's median basic, read to three places — it prints 37.0) and no band of any kind moves.
- **A run saved inside the Forest or the Scarlands keeps its old fights until the next zone** — a zone's warbands are made with its map. **Start a new run to meet them.**
- **The sim, testing the code**: every arm ran clean on both trees, and each copy fights as its Orc in eight forced lineups. **The Forest's Rage Company elite fights read easier on KH's tree — 156 wins of 200 against HEAD's 133, outside two standard errors — and the resists the designer ruled are not why** (153 of 200 with the Orcs' resists put back): it is what the Forest draws for the theme. Every other cell is a tie. Whether that is wanted is the designer's (NEEDS A RULING, item 5).
- **Back to the designer: one balance question, in three parts, with its options** (NEEDS A RULING, item 5) — the Forest's elite fights reading easier, the Withered Warden's escort raising through the Grave Totem alone, and the Forest's cheapest fights fielding two kinds of power 1 where five stood: three things KH's recommended options built, which the designer took and their texts did not show. KH's four readings are taken (NEEDS A RULING). **Still the designer's**: the two look-list rows left NOT YET.
