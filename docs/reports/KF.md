# Batch KF — A Plate Tells a Buff from a Debuff; a Zone Fields Its Own Kinds

**THE RECORD ARRIVED AS THE DESIGNER's WORDS IN CONVERSATION, NOT AS A DECISION RECORD, SAID HERE FIRST**: *"there needs to be a distinction on the hero name plates between buffs and debuffs. enemies are not showing up in there correct zones. give me a list of all enemy types and their abilities."* (24 November, after playtesting KE's build), with the designer's look-list notes pasted beside it (`RECORD_KF.md` in KF's work folder holds both, verbatim). The list was answered in conversation (`enemy-list.md` beside the repository, read off `data/enemies.json`, and regenerated on KF's data); KF built the other two asks. **Lane A, the whole floor**: KF changes what a fight fields and what a plate draws. **The record carries no lane, names no source and asks no open question**, so KF was run on what it carries, the lane and the open questions its own (§0).

**THE VERDICT: DONE — GREEN BUT FOR THE TWO CARRIED REDS.**
- **A hero's plate tells harm from help.** On a hero's and a companion's plate a harmful chip is framed red and 2 px and a helpful one green and 1 px, and its hover's first word names it: *Debuff* (what `DEBUFF_IDS` names, the game's registry, which most cleanses lift — Broken, the Bleed chip and a sticky Poison are debuffs no cleanse lifts), *Cost* (a harm a hero's own card lays that no cleanse lifts: Berserk's risk, Formless's recoil, Threshold's lock, a spent Overcharge, and Boil Over's floor, which nothing lays today) or *Buff* (every other status, and every display chip on the hero side, none of which is in either list). The engine chip and every enemy's plate are unframed. Every status STATUS_INFO holds and every one a line lays by its literal id — 179 — is classed in a census of record, `docs/reports/KF-cuts.tsv`, which `check_kf` §1 holds to the code, so a status written later reds until it is classed.
- **The Frozen Plains and the Drowned Barrow field their own kinds.** The ten Orc kinds JS's ruling 6 sent into every new roster leave rosters 4 and 6; they stand in the Forest of Old and the Scarlands. The Orc Chief, the game's one elite, stays in both, and the Hollow Crown keeps its tags. Every node of every zone still composes; the Barrow, with no mender of its own, never fields three themes.
- **A run saved inside either zone keeps its old fights until the next zone** — a zone's warbands are made with its map. **Start a new run to see the change.**
- **Back to the designer: one balance question, with its options** (NEEDS A RULING, item 0) — what the two zones field beside their own six kinds: an elite each and a Barrow mender, new kinds copied from the Orc Chief and the Orc Shaman — a later batch's work, with three figures of the designer's (recommended); the Chief and one Orc healer as named exceptions; or as built. Beside it, **the designer's word on the two look-list rows left NOT YET** (FOUND AT KF). KF's seven readings are taken and stand for the designer to reverse.
- **The sim, testing the code**: both zones compose at every budget on their own kinds; as the bot plays them both are kinder (at budget 12 the Frozen Plains won 45 of 50 against HEAD's 31, the Barrow 43 against 27) and the Frozen Plains' fights run longer. How hard they press is the designer's; KF makes no figure.
- **The plates and the zones are driven headless and never seen**: the look-list holds both for one look, the designer's thirteen passed rows of 24 November go to the archive, and TO WATCH IN PLAY asks whether the frames read at a glance and whether the zones read as samey on six kinds.

## §0 — WHAT THE ASKS DECIDE, AND WHAT THEY LEAVE

**The designer's**: the hero plates distinguish buffs from debuffs; enemies show up in their correct zones. **What was left, and KF took** (NEEDS A RULING, seven readings): what counts as a debuff on a plate (the registry), the costs a hero's own card lays (a third word), Broken, the hero side only with the display chips framed, a frame rather than a regrouping, which zones the Orcs leave, and the Hollow Crown's tags. **What KF did not take, because it is balance** (KA's word): what fields the two new zones' elite nodes, and whether the Barrow gains a mender — laid before the designer with options, and nothing built on it. **The record names no zone**: the reading taken is the two zones whose own kinds the travelling core joined, and the designer's playtest readouts of 24 November confirm it — two cited, both debug-touched (FOUND AT KF says how they were read).

## §1 — THE PLATES

**At HEAD** every chip on a plate was a filled box in its status's own colour with its tag or figure; nothing on it said whether it helped or harmed. **The build** (`scripts/unit.gd`): `BattleUnit.status_cut` is the one answer — *debuff*, *cost* or *buff*, a composition cutting as the worst of its ingredients — and `_refresh_chips` asks it of every display chip and status on the hero side, the engine chips passed over. `_draw_chip` lays the frame as a `ReferenceRect` between the fill and the tag, red and 2 px for a debuff or a cost, green and 1 px for a buff, taking no mouse; the row's readers count a chip as one `ColorRect` (`check_hs` §2b, `check_ie` §2a, `check_ig`, `check_ii` §0.5a, `check_hz` §1c), so the frame is never one. The tooltip leads with *Debuff:*, *Cost:* or *Buff:*. **The census of record** (`docs/reports/KF-cuts.tsv`): 179 statuses, each with why — the 171 in `battle.gd`'s STATUS_INFO (38 debuff, 5 cost, 128 buff) and eight laid outside it (Broken, the Bleed chip and Melt Armor's chip, 3 debuff; Anointed, Fortified, the Standard Bearer's aura, Unrelenting Assault and an enemy's wind-up, 5 buff) — 41 debuff (the registry's 41), 5 cost, 133 buff; `check_kf` §1 walks every id a line of `unit.gd` or `battle.gd` lays by its literal and reds on one the census does not class; the costs were found by reading every status outside the registry whose words name a harm to its bearer (the blocks' reader's H1 found the third, Threshold's lock). **Not built**: an enemy's plate (its non-debuffs are its own boons or the party's marks, which the unit cannot tell apart); the `+N` chip's hover (FOUND AT KF).
