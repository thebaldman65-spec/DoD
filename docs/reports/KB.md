# Batch KB — KA's Rulings Answered: Heal, and the Hunter's Two New Cores

**THE RECORD ARRIVED IN CONVERSATION, NOT AS A DECISION RECORD, SAID HERE FIRST**: seven messages from the designer between 18 and 22 November, answering KA's four rulings, writing Volley's and the Ambusher's rules with Claude one rune at a time, and ruling the heal's 20% in their own words and every other figure from options Claude laid out — pasted whole, with the six proposals and five numbers they accepted, in `RECORD_KB.md` in KB's work folder. They carry no lane, name no source and ask no open question (`docs/ways-of-working.md`, *A MALFORMED RECORD IS REPORTED, NOT GUESSED AT*), so KB was run on what they carry, with the lane its own (**Lane A**: the conversation builds two core engines, renames two cards and moves the save).

**THE VERDICT: DONE — GREEN BUT FOR THE TWO CARRIED REDS.**
- **Every Cleric starts with Heal**: one ally healed for 20% of the **Cleric's** maximum health, 26% on a Perfect — the card was Ministration, and the name is kept for a later card. **Holy's card is Greater Heal**, its figures unchanged. A saved run reads both names by the version it was saved at.
- **Volley is a Hunter core rune**: in one attack, each arrow the Hunter or a companion lands on an enemy adds Bleed buildup — 4 for the first to land, 8 for the second, 12 for the third — and a volley of two or more that lands every arrow counts its last one twice; once an arrow has landed on an enemy, each later arrow at it rolls at least 100% of its damage. Nothing carries between attacks. (Where the designer said *arrow k* and *the second arrow*, KB counts the arrows that landed — its reading, KB's ruling 2.)
- **The Ambusher is the other**: before an attack at an enemy he picks, the Hunter may **overdraw** — up to 2 copies of the attack's arrow at that enemy, each pushing his next turn out by the card's initiative over its arrows, never more than 4.0 in all, no Mana. An attack on every enemy and Twin Hunt, Harvest and Cull take no copies — narrower than the designer's *any card where the Hunter shoots* (KB's ruling 2). The action bar's *Overdraw +N (V)* button asks for them, and the turn bar shows where the turn lands before he commits. The bot overdraws only to finish its target.
- **The Tracker and the Skirmisher are ordinary runes, *Common Foe* and *Pounce***, their rules and rune ids kept; a saved hero who held one as his core keeps it as an ordinary rune, and his core slot is empty (the save is v16).
- **KA's four rulings are answered and archived.** **KB's three rulings**: the saved hero (taken, correctness); the rules' edges, read narrowly, and the texts, rendered from the rules — **both the designer's to settle in conversation**. **KB opens no balance question**: every figure built is the designer's.
- **A new gate, `check_kb`** (90 checks: Heal, the bot's picks, the save's names, Volley, the overdraw, the conversion, the load step, and master's figures for all four runes read against their constants); 30 gates and suites re-pointed to the new game, each to its intent.

## §2 — VOLLEY (ruling 1; the designer's rule)

- **The rule as built**: in each attack, every arrow a Volley Hunter or his companion lands on an enemy adds Bleed buildup to it — `VOLLEY_STEP` (4) times its place among the arrows that landed on that body this cast: 4, 8, 12, …; **a clean volley** (two or more arrows at a body, every one landed) pays the last share again (`VOLLEY_CLEAN_MULT` 2); **the rider**: once an arrow has landed on a body, each later arrow's damage roll starts at `VOLLEY_RIDER_FLOOR` (100%) instead of 90%. Nothing carries between attacks.
- **Where it lives**: a record kept for one cast and dropped after it, built the way the Weaver's Echo keeps its own — armed at `_resolve`'s top for a holder's damaging cast, emptied for any nested resolve, restored at the tail, where the clean volley is paid. **An arrow lands at the strike loop's `connected`** (not missed, not Blocked); its share rides the strike's one Bleed call. **A companion's blow is an arrow** in `_companion_hit`, the one function every beast's blow passes, and it never misses. The rider is the same `randf_range` call with a raised floor, so it spends the same dice.
- **Its text** (the rule's card, read through `Classes.engine_desc`, KB's ruling 3):

  > Volley: in each attack, every arrow the / Hunter or a companion lands on an enemy / adds Bleed buildup to it: 4 for the / first to land, 8 for the second, 12 for / the third, and so on. When two or more / arrows fly at an enemy and every one / lands, the last one's buildup counts / twice. Once an arrow has landed on an / enemy, each later arrow at it rolls at / least 100% of its damage.

## §3 — THE AMBUSHER (ruling 1; overdraw)

- **The rule as built**: before an attack the Hunter shoots at an enemy he picks, he may ask for copies of its arrow at that enemy — each an ordinary strike of the loop with its own miss, Block, crit and roll; each costs the card's initiative over its arrows (its authored count: its multi-hit count, a chosen pair's two or trio's three, else one — never a Perfect's extra arrow, KB's reading), no Mana; at most `OVERDRAW_MAX_ARROWS` (2) copies and no more than `OVERDRAW_MAX_DELAY` (4.0) of initiative added. A special — a companion's attack among them — an attack on every enemy and a random-hit card take none: **narrower than the ruling** (proposal 5: *any card where the Hunter shoots*), for a reason in the code — Twin Hunt's, Harvest's and Cull's Hunter blows are resolved by their own handlers, and an attack on every enemy picks no main target for a copy to fly at (KB's ruling 2).
- **The control**: an action-bar button, *Overdraw +N (V)*, chosen before the card as Empower is — each press asks one more and wraps to none, V the same — so nothing waits between the pick and the cast; hovering a card shows on the turn bar where the turn lands, clamped as the cast will clamp it. **The bot overdraws only to finish its target** — the fewest copies whose plain damage at the middle of the roll, through its armour, reaches its health — and draws no die doing it.
- **The price** rides the one delay line the cast already schedules, so Momentum and speed scale the copies as they scale the card.
- **Its text** (KB's ruling 3):

  > Ambusher: before an attack, the Hunter may / add up to 2 copies of the attack's / arrow, each shot at its main target. / Each copy delays the Hunter's next / turn by the attack's initiative divided / by its arrows, no more than 4.0 / initiative in all, and costs no Mana. / An attack on every enemy, a companion's / own attack, Twin Hunt, Harvest and Cull / take no copies.

  The button's face reads *Overdraw +N (V)*, and its tooltip: *Add copies of the next attack's arrow, / each shot at its main target. Each copy / pushes the next turn further out. V adds / one, up to 2, then none. A card takes / only the copies its cap allows; the turn / bar shows where the turn lands.*
