**IN PLAIN WORDS.** Until JO, the rule that says which enemy moves wait between uses ignored Break damage, so a move that mostly fills a hero's Break bar looked weak. **Now a point of Break damage counts for what a hero loses when the bar fills — a turn — spread over the bar.** Counting it on both sides (the move and the plain attack it is compared with) moved seven of the ten moves JN raised up a little to stay worth taking, and left three alone. **And no enemy now hexes a hero without hitting them**: the four hexes ride blows — two on moves that hit and laid nothing, two on their own moves now given some damage — so a hex is a reason to fear a blow, not a wasted enemy turn. A single hex blow can miss and can be drawn away, like any other blow, and an area one reaches the companions too. Nothing about balance was measured: that is the designer's, by playing; this batch only made sure the code works, and counted that each hex actually fires.

**THE WORDS THIS REPORT USES.** *IMPLEMENT ONLY*: the brief's order to build what it names and author nothing else. *JI's line*: a move's worth against 1.4 times its own kind's basic attack's; at or past it a move cools. *The Break price*: a point of a move's Break damage at a hero turn's damage over the meter it fills. *A kind's basic*: its first move that deals damage and costs nothing. *The ten*: the second damaging moves JN raised. *Held*: a cooldown the repaired line would move, kept at the data's figure for the designer to rule. *A hex rides a blow*: its status is a damaging move's own, laid on a hit. *The tree that lands*: HEAD and JO's work. *A ruling*: a choice that is the designer's; the batch takes its recommendation and lists it under NEEDS A RULING. *The routing*, *the blocks' reader*, *the close-read*, *the new text's reader*, *the positional readers*, *the fresh reader*: as JN's report defines them. *A control*: a deliberate break of the tree that a gate must catch by name. *The battery*, *the recon*, *the pre-run*, *the acceptance*: every gate in the project, run on a copy and then in the repository.
