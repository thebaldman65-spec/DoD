## §2 — THE OPEN QUESTIONS, EACH ANSWERED AND ITS BRANCH NAMED

1. **How many kinds do *rot* and *grave* hold?** Read as the Forest of Old's rosters: **rot's (1) 18 and grave's (3) 17** at the open, and every roster 16 to 19. **The branch taken: at ten or more — drop Fen Lurker, then Barrow-Sworn.** Both dropped; four added. **Closing count per roster: 1 (rot): 20, 2: 17, 3 (grave): 19, 4: 16, 5: 19.**

8. **Which of JT's two figures is the landed one?** **The readings'**: `docs/ways-of-working.md` 30,900 B (+10,931) and `docs/instrument-rules.md` 225,609 B (+5,970), as `git show 330a956` reads them. §1's 30,667 B (+10,698), *512,208 and 224,881* and +5,242 B were an earlier build's; **corrected in JT's report §1, struck with the landed figures beside them**.
