## §2 — THE PARTY's THREE ANSWERS, READ OUT

**The readout is `docs/reports/JP-answers.md`, kept with the tree**: 64 doors, each a file, a function and a line quoted from it
as the tree that lands holds it — `check_jp` §2 reads every quote in its function every battery and reds on a door that does not exist or a
row that quotes nothing (C04a–c). **It authors nothing**: no card, talent, rune, status or ability, and no figure for one; where it says
what a card *would have to add*, it names the code's shape and its cost, never the card. What follows is its short form.

**1. TAKING MALICE OUT OF THE POOL.**
- **Where it is added**: `_malice_fill`, the one way in, called by the damage door (`_malice_on_damage`: a point every 10 the hero side
  removes, and every point a blow spends past a body's last — the overkill term — in one call) and the death door (`_on_unit_died`: 25 a
  body of the warband, *whoever dealt it*). **Where it is spent**: `_malice_pay`, the one way out — a warband heal (what the body gained),
  a Raise (the health it stands a body up with), a Rally and an Entrench (their prices), all in `_resolve_special`'s arms.
- **Whether anything can subtract from it**: nothing else. The pool has two writes, `malice += got` and `malice = maxi(malice - points, 0)`.
- **A floor at zero**: yes, in `_malice_pay`'s own line.
- **Whether the strip would show a fall**: yes. `_refresh_malice_strip` is called from the pay as from the fill and sets the bar's width
  and its *n/150* off `malice` each time (not in a sim).
- **Whether a hero's blow could carry a drain the way a warband blow carries a hex**: the shape exists — the strike loop in `_resolve` reads
  a blow's own fields on every hit, for both sides (`applies_status` lays a hex that way since JO, `delay_push` moves the struck body's
  clock), and costs no turn — but **nothing drains the pool today**: `applies_status` lays a status on a body, and the pool is the fight's,
  on no body. **A card would add** a field read beside `delay_push`, after the Block and the miss, taking points out through a door of its
  own — or through `_malice_pay`, whose ledger would then book the drain as the warband's own spending, so a fourth ledger key keeps the
  instruments true — and a sentence in the strip's tooltip. **And it meets a declaration**: a declared spend is
  bought at the body's turn without asking the pool (`_warband_free_spend`), so a drain between a declaration and its buy lets a Rally or
  an Entrench land underpaid through the floor, the ledger booking its full price as spent — a latent shape whose writer is the drain (FOUND AT JP); a heal is bounded at resolution by
  `_malice_free`. **The blow is a damaging card that is no `special`**: `_resolve` hands a `special` off before the strike loop. **Cost**:
  one `Ability` field, about five lines, a ledger key, a tooltip sentence and a gate driving a blow that lands, one blocked and one missed.
- **The +25-a-kill and the overkill terms, switched off conditionally**: the kill term is one unconditional call in `_on_unit_died`, which
  `unit._die` reaches before the body's statuses are cleared — so a status on the dying body is in scope at that line, and the killer is
  reachable there by `_frame_dealer()`; one `if` is the change. The overkill term is the `past_last` half of `_malice_on_damage`'s one call, the victim and the
  dealer in scope. **Cost**: a condition each, and a gate driving a kill or an overkill both ways.

**2. REFUSING A HEX.**
- **Gall and Fester are area blows and roll no miss**: true at source, and **no area blow can be parried** either. **The Block is the one
  roll left** (it negates the whole hit and its on-hit status), no companion's and no Broken body's.
- **`block_chance` has no universal baseline**: `0.0` on every hero but the Warden (`0.10`); on the warband's side the Shieldmaster carries `0.05` — with Heavy Plating's
  slice (widened by **Shieldwall** and a **Split Shield's Bulwark Line**, which the brief's list missed), Interpose's charges and a Warden's
  Covering Guard; no talent's payload and no live rune writes it.
- **Every door a status passes onto a hero**: the strike loop's status line in `_resolve` (a hit, no death, the move's `status_chance`,
  `1.0` when it names none — none of the four hex blows names one), then `battle._apply_status`, then `unit.add_status` (Fleeting's
  duration term, the one term on the body that receives).
- **Whether anything refuses a status rather than removing it after**: **yes — three refusals stand in `_apply_status`'s head**: a boss's
  hard-control immunity (the warband's side only); **Ironclad**, a Warrior's card, three named ids and no hex; and **Hallowed**, laid by an
  **Empowered Divine Plea** on the hero it heals, three turns as laid, refusing every id in `DEBUFF_IDS` — **the four hexes among them**. Umbral
  Mirror's rebound would turn a warband debuff back on its caster, but its field has no writer. **No resistance roll exists on a
  hero and no ward absorbs a status.** **What a further refusal would need**: one `if` beside the three, reading a status on the target —
  the door is built; a card adds what lays the status.
- **Which cleanses reach which hex**: all four hexes are in `DEBUFF_IDS` and none is sticky, so **every cleanse reaches every hex**:
  Unburden, the Empowered Holy Heal and the Empowered Divine Plea (`purge_debuffs`), Dispel on a hero (`dispel_one_debuff`), Field Dressing and the Rune of the Medic (one each), the tier-2
  talent *Cleanse Debuffs Each Turn* (two at a turn start, each off a random ally) and the Cleansing Draught (`_cleansable_debuffs`).
- **What a base Block for every hero would touch**: `var block_chance := 0.0` is a default a spec's figure replaces (`unit.setup` writes
  every config key over the field), so a base there reaches every hero but the Warden and every warband body but the Shieldmaster; the
  Warden's `0.10` replaces it, so what a Covering Guard lends (his live chance, Heavy Plating's slice on top) rises only if his row rises.
  A base in every spec's row is the other way. It is the roll every blow meets — every fight's damage, Break damage and on-hit statuses,
  not the hexes alone — and the gates that drive a Block move with it; the hero sheet's *Block* row reads the spec's own figure
  (`party_screen.gd`), so it shows a base only if every spec's row carries it, and `gate_fixture`'s deterministic `-10.0` is written over
  the field after the spawn and does not move. **A balance question, the designer's.**

**3. READING WHAT THE WARBAND DECLARED.**
- **The field**: `intent`, on every body; set by `_declare_intent` (at a turn's end, a fight's opening, a hold's release and a Raise) and by a wind-up's
  arm; cleared at the body's own turn (`_revalidate_intent` copies and empties it before the move resolves), by a wind-up landing
  (`_enemy_turn`), by a stun, a freeze, a hold or a Break (`_discard_intent`), by a hijack, by the death sweep and by a Raise. **Its readers are all the warband's machinery and the plate**:
  the plate and its two names, its hover, the turn bar's icon, the enemy's turn, the pool's reserve and the spends' checks.
- **Whether a declared move is reachable to a card**: **no card, talent or rune reads it today**; a card's **handler** can (an arm of
  `_resolve_special` has the picked body in scope: `target.intent`, `target.cooldowns`, the battle's `malice`); a talent's or a rune's
  **condition** cannot (`Talents.condition_met` reads a node held, a card owned and the party's standing heroes — no enemy, no declaration,
  no pool).
- **Whether the warband has a Break meter of its own — the premise read the other way**: **every body of the warband has one**, the same
  `take_hit` meter a hero's is, and **its Break discards its declared move** (the turn loop's Broken branch). **A hero declares nothing**
  (`_declare_intent`'s first line), so a hero's Break discards nothing of the kind, and **there is no warband-level meter**. The party can
  already cost the warband a declared move — Break, stun, freeze or hold the body, hijack its turn with a madness, or hold it to its basic
  with Heads Down — and the warband has no lever on a hero's choice.
- **Whether a card could condition on a declared move's worth, its cooldown or the pool's level**: on its **cooldown** and the **pool**,
  yes, from a handler; on its **worth**, not without first moving JI's line into the game — the line is `check_ji.line_blow`, a gate's
  static, and a second copy in the game is what the figure rules forbid.
