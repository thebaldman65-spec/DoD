## §4 — THE OPEN QUESTIONS, EACH ANSWERED AND ITS BRANCH NAMED

1. **Does a slot-2 zone take one roster or a pair, and does a new roster inherit a travelling core?** **One** — the Scarlands' shape, one slot, one roster (6); **and yes**, the twelve-kind core, as rosters 4 and 5 took it. Closing counts 1: 18, 2: 17, 3: 17, 4: 16, 5: 19, 6: 19; JU's four and the Barrow's three fit, nothing overfills.
2. **What does the Shepherd's heal cost at the rate the game already uses?** A point of Malice a point restored, half of what Soul Leech lands, through `_warband_heal` — the existing rule, no new rate. **Not measured** (§2, §5): the pool the heroes' damage and every warband death fill pays it — ten points of their damage a point of his heal — and a long fight fills it; whether that is negligible the designer reads in play.
3. **Do the twelve names collide?** One exact hit, *Claw*, renamed *Sodden Claw*; the near-misses ship flagged (FOUND AT JW): *Swipe* in the Grave Husk's *Rattling Swipe*, *Black Rot* beside the Rot Behemoth, *Rotting Fist* and the Rot bargain, *Rotfiend* holding the bargain's name *Rot*, *Bone Shepherd* beside *Bone Rattle*, *Soul Leech* a deleted talent's name (its field `soul_leech_step` stands) beside the *Rune of the Leech*, *Call the Drowned* beside *Call the Wilds*, *Corpse Explosion* beside *Arcane Explosion*. *Noxious Fumes*, *Fen Lurker*, *Rake*, *Lacerate* and *Submerge* are clear. **The tints are the batch's** (bog green, fen teal, bone) — a ruling.
4. **Is there a hit chance a debuff can reduce?** **Yes** — `_miss_chance`; Noxious Fumes adds 25 points for 3 turns (§2 says what it means).
5. **Does a status deal a share of maximum health, or stack?** None dealt a share of maximum health (the Wasting bargain's tick does, as a modifier's); Fester and Poison stack — **Black Rot takes an arm of its own beside theirs** (a stack an application, no clock: Ruin's shape) and **a tick of its own** at the foot of the DoT pass (`_black_rot_tick`); what it reuses is the stack counter, the turn-start pass and `DEBUFF_IDS`' cleanse.
6. **Is *20 bleed* an existing status?** **Yes** — Bleed's buildup meter; Lacerate adds 20 (`bleed_build`), and *bleed* is a label.
7. **Is *adjacent* meaningful for warband bodies?** **Yes** — the ADJACENT keyword, `_adjacent_enemies`, formation order; Corpse Explosion heals, of its living neighbours that can be picked, the one with the lower share of its health.
