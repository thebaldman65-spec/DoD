2026-10-27 — Batch IZ: THE PLACING, TWO RE-KEYS, AND A REMOVAL

The bargain deck holds twenty-one modifiers now, and a rung can deal every one of them. Dull Edge and Tinderbox are removed: neither cost any heroes anything the measurement could find, even the heroes each should have hurt most. Frenzied is re-keyed: it multiplied every unit's speed, which moves nobody's turn, and now it takes thirty points off every unit's speed, so the slowest are slowed most — its card still reads Everyone acts -30% faster, and the wording is the designer's. Three bargains went back to their old numbers, because a sharper measurement found they had cost something all along: Slick Footing +0.5 initiative, Fleeting one turn, Bloodletting 15 Bleed. Severities now: 1 Frenzied, Muffled, Fleeting, Hoarfrost, Slick Footing, Encumbered and Bloodletting; 2 Parched, Brittle, Warded and Feverish; 3 Deadened, the Long Silence, the Thin Veil and Miasma; 4 Rot and Overgrown; 5 Thin Air, Mirrorbound, Wasting and Bloodless. Full working: docs/reports/IZ.md.

§1 — THE OLD NUMBERS ON THE SHARPER MEASUREMENT. Measured again at their old numbers, Slick Footing costs the heroes 0.71 points of health at a bargain fight's end and Fleeting 0.92, each past the measurement's own noise; Hoarfrost at three turns costs nothing, so its nine stay. Encumbered reads 1.09 (a 1 — its 2 rested on a blunter reading), Feverish 3.15 and Warded 1.96 (both 2s). Four of the eleven bargains called free two batches ago were never free: the old measurement could not see costs that small. A sharper reading of the same heroes now replaces a blunter one rather than standing beside it. The severity cuts were worked out again on the sharper readings, and two moved a little (1.70 → 1.78, 3.70 → 3.74); no bargain crossed one.

§2 — WHERE EACH NUMBER SITS. Bloodletting, Slick Footing and Fleeting are back at their old numbers, each a 1 there; Parched stays at no resource (a 2), Hoarfrost at nine turns (a 1) and Muffled at half. Severity 4 holds two, Rot and Overgrown: that is the hole, and filling it is a new bargain the designer authors.

§3 — DULL EDGE IS REMOVED. Crit is something every build makes, and Dull Edge's rule on it was worth about 0.15 points of health per ten points of crit, so no number could make it a bargain and no set of heroes could save it. A saved game whose offer names it loads with that option dropped.

§4 — FRENZIED ADDS. An amount on every speed moves the turn order where a multiple did not. Added, thirty points changes nothing measurable and three hundred helps the heroes (0.70 points); taken away, thirty points costs them 0.60 — so the subtraction shipped, at severity 1. No unit's speed falls below a tenth of its own.

§5 — MUFFLED AND TINDERBOX ON THE HEROES THEY SHOULD HURT. Muffled was measured on talented heroes at Ruin, whose plan is Break: it costs them 1.66 points, a 1. Tinderbox was measured on the default heroes with the Pyromancer in place of the Cryomancer — three times the fire — and still cost nothing (0.24, inside the noise): the heroes deal about twelve times the warband's fire, so a fire bonus for everyone mostly feeds them. It is removed too.

§6 — WARDEN's BARGAINS STAY. Dealing Warden's bargains from Wanderer's bands (safe 1–2, gambles 3–4) would end its bargain fights 5.8 points of health kinder, so the bands stay where they are, and Warden's two gambles are still Rot and Overgrown until a severity-4 bargain is authored.

§7 — THE CHANGELOG IS CUT. The forty-nine oldest entries, Batch FT through Batch HR, moved to the archive byte for byte: this file starts at Batch HS now, and before this entry it was 142,566 bytes, where it was 390,847.

2026-10-26 — Batch IY: A GRADE IS THE HIGHEST COST MEASURED, AND NINE HANDLES TURNED

Nine bargains changed their one number, and five of them are dealt again. Bloodletting adds 45 Bleed a hit (was 15); Hoarfrost's Chilled, which everyone begins the fight under, lasts nine turns (was three); Slick Footing makes every ability cost +1.5 initiative (was +0.5); Fleeting takes two turns off every status (was one); Parched opens a bargain fight with no resource at all (was half); Dull Edge takes 10% crit chance (was 5%); Muffled lets half of Break damage land (was three quarters); Tinderbox gives every fire strike +75% (was +25%). Frenzied is unchanged. No bargain's rule changed, only its number — and each number is a measured step, not a final placing, which is the designer's. Bloodletting now carries severity 3, Parched 2, and Fleeting, Hoarfrost and Slick Footing 1; Dull Edge, Tinderbox, Frenzied and Muffled still cost nothing the measurement can find, so no rung offers them. Deadened moved to severity 3 and Brittle to 2, because they cost fully talented heroes far more than untalented ones. Full working: docs/reports/IY.md.

§1 — A SHARPER MEASUREMENT. The simulator compares a bargain against no bargain run for run, but two runs used to part ways at their first bargain fight, so the comparison bought little. Now every map node and every won fight's spoils draws its dice from the run's own seed, so the two runs meet the same dice at every node. The comparison's error fell to 0.55 of what it would be unpaired (it was 0.94): a cost can now be told from nothing at about 0.6 points of health, where it took 1.5.

§2 — A COST THE MEASUREMENT CANNOT SEE IS NO COST. A severity used to be read from a cost alone, even one smaller than the measurement's own noise. Now a cost has to stand clear of that noise before it earns any severity, and the severity is the highest cost measured on any set of heroes: a bargain may be priced above what it costs some heroes, never below what it costs others.

§3 — WHAT THE NINE NUMBERS DO. Each of the nine was turned by one step and measured again: Bloodletting +4.37 ± 0.49 points of health at a bargain fight's end, Parched +1.90 ± 0.40, Fleeting +1.63 ± 0.42, Hoarfrost +1.03 ± 0.29, Slick Footing +0.98 ± 0.32. Bloodletting overshot its target. Hoarfrost stops costing more once its Chilled outlasts the fight, so its nine turns were set against a fight's length. Frenzied costs nothing even at three times the speed, because speeding every unit up changes nobody's turn order. Dull Edge at its floor helps the heroes, since the enemies lose more crits than they do. Where the numbers should finally sit is the designer's call. The combat log's line for a Chilled stack now names the clock it set, where it always said three.

§4 — THE GLOSSARY's COUNT. The glossary says how many bargains every road deals; that count is now worked out from the grades (15 today), so it follows the next re-grade on its own.

2026-10-26 — Batch IX: THE BASELINE's OWN ERROR BAR, AND WHY THE FREE MODIFIERS ARE FREE

Six bargains are no longer dealt, and fourteen moved grade. Dull Edge, Parched, Tinderbox, Frenzied, Muffled and Fleeting cost the party nothing the measurement can find, so they carry no severity now and no rung offers them until they are re-authored. Every other bargain's severity is now read off what it costs, so eight moved — Thin Air up to 5, which makes it Ruin's alone; Warded up to 2; Deadened, Brittle, Slick Footing, Bloodletting, Feverish and Miasma down one or two — and the reward each pays follows its grade, as it always has. On Warden, the two gambles every offer deals are now always Rot and Overgrown, the only two severity-4 bargains left. No bargain's rule changed. Full working: docs/reports/IX.md.

§1 — THE NUMBER EVERYTHING ELSE IS MEASURED AGAINST. The last batch measured every bargain on one party and compared each against the same party fighting with no bargain at all — but gave that comparison figure no error bar. Measured again (60 runs, the same seeds), it reproduced exactly, and its error is now printed beside it: the party ends a bargain fight at 94.4% of its health, give or take 0.5. Set against it run for run, 11 of the twenty-three bargains cost less than the measurement can tell from nothing.

§2 — WHY THE FREE ONES ARE FREE. Each of the seven cheapest was laid on the heroes alone, to see whether the enemies' half was handing the party back what its own half took — the way a removed bargain once healed whoever dealt more damage. None does. Neither half of any of the seven moves the party past the measurement's noise: each is simply too mild, so the fix is to turn its one number, not to change its rule. Two of them — Frenzied and Hoarfrost — do change the fight: the party's healing moves with them, up when the heroes are slowed and down when they are quickened, and takes up the difference before the fight ends. That was seen in the figures, not set out to be proved.

§3 — A SEVERITY IS WHAT A BARGAIN COSTS. The grades were ranks cut at fixed counts; they are bands of cost now, on one named party — the simulator's default four, with no talents, on the easiest road: a severity 1 costs that party about 0.9% of its health at a bargain fight's end, a 2 about 2.5%, a 3 about 4.9%, a 4 about 8.8% and a 5 15.6% or more, the boundaries set halfway between the measured levels. One caution travels with the scale: at the number of runs taken, a severity 1 cannot yet be told from no severity at all.

§4 — WHERE THE PARTY MATTERS. Five bargains cost the strong, fully talented party and the weak, untalented one more than a band apart — Thin Air most, three bands. Which words should say so is the designer's.

2026-10-25 — Batch IW: THE LADDER WITH RESOLUTION, AND THE DECK's MIDDLE

Nothing a player sees on a card, a rune or a status moved. §1 — THE LADDER, ON A PARTY THAT CAN FEEL IT. The deck had been measured on a party so strong that nineteen of its twenty-three twists ended a fight within a few hundredths of full health — too close to rank. Nine candidate parties were tried first, the rule for choosing written down before any ran, and the full measurement was taken on the one the bargains hurt most clearly: the simulator's default party with no talents, at the easiest difficulty. The measuring tool was checked before it was trusted: re-run, it reproduced the last batch's numbers exactly, and switching its readings off changed nothing in the runs.

§2 — WHAT THE MEASUREMENT SAYS ABOUT THE DECK. On that party the twists spread out further and fall in a different order — losing the resource that returns each turn costs it nearly as much as the harshest twists, and the costlier abilities hurt it more than their grade says — but at two of the four places the grades are cut, the twists either side still cost the same. So no grade changed: the nineteen twists whose grade the measurement does not support are listed for the designer, each with the number that could be turned, beside the question of which party the grades should be ranked by.

§3 — THE ELEVEN FIGURES, TAKEN AGAIN. §4 — THE NAMES, THE LANES AND THE ZONE SWITCH. The Rune of the Vanguard, the Rune of the Invoker and the Rune of the Hierophant are the designer's names now, not proposals — the project's own writing had already adopted them as nouns.
