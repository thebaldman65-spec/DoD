2026-07-20 — Batch 20: Arcanist rework + talent tree (Resonance engine), Ice Lance trim

  • Ice Lance damage reduced: 35% of Attack (was 40%).
  • Arcanist core rework — the Resonance engine. Magic Bolt is reforged into Arcane Explosion: free, 10% Atk arcane / 10 BD / 2.0 int, no cd, hits TWO distinct random enemies simultaneously and builds 1 Resonance (2 on a crit) like every damaging cast (no perfect bonus). Death Ray is REMOVED (vaulted — kept for future return).
  • Arcane Cannon retuned: 25 Mana (was 30), 40% Atk (was 30%), 2cd (was 1), BD now scales — 5 × current Resonance stacks (was flat 15); still +7.5% damage per stack and 15% recoil. New perfect: the cast costs 3.0 initiative instead of 3.5 (was +5 BD).
  • Arcane Barrage retuned: 20 Mana (was 25), 6 bolts × 8% Atk (was 5%), 3 BD per bolt (was 4), 2.5 int (was 3.5); bolts now target ONLY the (up to) 3 living enemies with the lowest health, so the volley rakes the 2-3 weakest. Perfect still fires a 7th bolt.
  • New core ability — Stabilize: free, 2.0 int, 3cd; consumes ALL Resonance: +5 Mana and +10% damage reduction (2 turns, "Stabilized" chip) per stack consumed; unusable at 0 stacks (Perfect: also heals 5% max HP). Resonance now persists until Stabilize consumes it.
  • Sixth fixed talent tree — Arcanist (from the new tree JSON), 12 nodes: Mindfulness (every 6/5/4 turns ALL cooldowns tick 1 extra — turns needed drop 1 per point), Arcane Mastery (+1%/rank crit per stack), Mana Attunement (2%/rank max Mana per stack gained), Temporal Rift (crits 3%/rank chance to echo 25% at a random enemy), On the Edge (surviving a hit below 25/30/35% — threshold rises 5 per point — grants 1 Resonance), Conversion (10%/rank of damage taken paid as Mana), Critical Mass (every 3rd crit +20%/rank damage, +10%/rank max Mana), Suppressing Fire (each Barrage bolt +0.25%/rank of Attack over the previous), Unlimited Power (overflow at max stacks → +2%/rank damage and +2%/rank max Mana, battle-long, live chip), Stable Alignment (one attack cannot remove more than 35/30/25% of max health — cap drops 5 per point).
  • New talent-granted abilities: Overcharge — 20 Mana, 2.0 int, 5cd, once per battle: max Resonance becomes 8; stacks 6-8 weigh 1.5× toward the per-stack passive bonuses (damage dealt / crit / damage taken; Perfect: 1.65×); Arcane Cannon +15% recoil and Arcane Barrage gains 15% recoil while active. Magi's Wrath (capstone) — 30 Mana, 15% Atk arcane to ALL enemies, 4.0 int, 4cd; +4% damage per stack, BD = 2.5 × stacks; recoil 15% of damage dealt, −3% per enemy hit (Perfect: costs 3.5 initiative).
  • Supporting changes: the Resonance nameplate readout shows the true (Overcharge-weighted) damage bonus; talent tooltips now render quarter-percent values exactly (Suppressing Fire); the sim bot plays the new rotation (Wrath → Overcharge → Stabilize when hurt at full stacks → Cannon → Barrage); Arcane Explosion's two blasts always pick distinct enemies while two stand.
  • Verified: full-tree autoplay battles — every talent and ability observed proccing in the log (Overcharged Cannon recoil 42 on a 139 hit ✓, Stable Alignment capping a boss slam at 25% max HP ✓, Unlimited Power compounding ✓); 20-battle sim clean.

2026-07-16 — Batch 12: Attack stat & node scaling, Resistances, Warden tree v2, melee-only parry

  • Attack stat + % scaling: every unit has an Attack stat; every ability's damage is now a PERCENT of the user's current Attack. Base Attack by role: Damage 100 / Tank 75 / Support 50 (per-spec stat blocks coming class by class). Existing damage numbers were converted to preserve current values at base Attack (e.g. Smite 44% of the Cleric's 50). Bruiser moved under the Damage role — the Swordmaster attacks at 100.
  • Node scaling: heroes gain +1% of base Attack and max HP per combat node won (persisted as combat_wins); enemies gain +2% of base per node tier across the whole game (replaces the old ×1.35-per-zone jump). Armor, Resistances, Speed, Constitution, Crit, Block, and Parry never scale. Enemy HP pools are multiples of 10 (rounded up after scaling; bases raised: Raider 140, Archer 110, Chief 250, Tyrant 370).
  • DoTs scale: Burn (6%) and Poison (3%/stack) snapshot the applier's Attack when applied. Companion beasts hit for % of the hunter's Attack (10/20/15%).
  • Resistances (hero stat): six per-element resistances (Fire/Frost/Nature/Holy/ Shadow/Arcane) that work like armor for their type; hover "Resistances" on the stat page. Per-spec values land class by class (0% placeholders today). Orc Shaman: fire 25%, frost 25%, nature 50%.
  • Ability displays: battle tooltips show computed damage from live Attack; the stat page shows damage AND "% of Attack"; the awakening screen shows both; the stat page gains an Attack row (with scaling explanation and win count).
  • Melee-only parry: ranged attackers (Hunter, Mage, Orc Archer, Orc Shaman) can no longer be parried. Parries log their source — reflexes / Sword Mastery / Parry Up — and blocks log theirs — Shieldwall / Heavy Plating / base Block.
  • Warden tree v2 (from the updated tree JSON): Tenacity is live — +5 max HP (battle-long) per Heavy Plating block; Rally is live — party +15% healing received for 2 turns per Heavy Plating block (new "Rallied" chip); Endurance reduced to +1%/rank armor per unhealed turn (was 3%). The Hardiness placeholder is gone.
  • New Warden ability — War Stomp: 20 Rage, 3 shockwaves × 15% Atk / 15 BD to random enemies, 3.0 int, 4cd; allies regain 10% of their resource.
  • Talent tooltips scale with investment: no more "per rank" — the displayed value grows as points go in (3% → 6% → 9%; unlearned nodes preview rank 1).
  • Class passive removed from the draft screen (stays on the awakening screen and party sheet).
  • Saves: loading a run now swaps saved talent-tree snapshots for the live definitions (learned ranks carry over; removed/shrunk nodes refund points), so balance edits reach old saves.
