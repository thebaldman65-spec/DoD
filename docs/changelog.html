2026-08-29 — Batch DV: THREE THINGS DU FOUND, AND THE CHANGELOG CUT

Four items. Three are DU §5's boss-pick findings and the fourth is a threshold the project's own rule says to act on. ONE THING CHANGED IN THE GAME — a door that was letting the player waste a turn the bot already refused — and the other three resolved to measurement, because the brief withheld the ruling each of them needed.

§1 — CLASS_POOLS IS A LOST FEATURE, NOT SCAFFOLDING, SO NOTHING WAS DELETED. The standing rule is that dead code is deleted rather than zeroed, and the brief made the deletion conditional on establishing WHY it is dead: a structure orphaned by a change is a lost feature, and one that was never wired is scaffolding. It was wired, and it was live. Three independent sources agree: classes.gd's own header describes the Batch AH award as "1 from its SPEC_POOLS entry and 2 from its class's CLASS_POOLS entry (Run.roll_ability_offer)"; run_state.gd records that Batch AN §4 re-pointed the award at the spec pool alone and left the structure standing; and test_batch_an §1 asserts roll_ability_offer() is deleted, so the function that read the class pools is gone and the pools are not. The AN changelog entry says the same in its own words. Per the brief, that makes it a design question rather than a deletion, and this batch has no ruling on it.

AND THE AUDIT SHARPENS DU'S FIGURE RATHER THAN REPEATING IT. CLASS_POOLS is 61 entries across four classes, 61 distinct names, and every one resolves in the corpus — DU's count is exact. What DU did not measure is where those names live otherwise, and the answer splits three ways: 27 are some spec's OPENING KIT (Heal is the Holy Cleric's, Blizzard the Cryomancer's), so what AN really retired was cross-spec acquisition inside a class; 27 are reachable through a live pool; and SEVEN ARE REACHABLE NOWHERE AT ALL — Rallying Shout, Mana Shield, Arcane Surge, Reality Fracture, Dawnbreak, Sanctuary and Divine Wrath. Each is authored, resolving, and fully implemented down to its handler, its status chip and its glossary prose, and the dead class pool is the only list in the game that names it. Checked against every other channel: no rune grants any of them (runes.json), and Classes.talent_granted_names() is empty. Deleting the structure would delete the last listing of seven finished abilities, which is a second and independent reason the brief's conditional lands where it does. Classes.ability_corpus() walks class_pool(), so the corpus of 227 counts all seven.

§2 — TWO OF HOLY'S THREE ZONE-BOSS AWARDS ROLL EMPTY, AND THE FIX IS PRICED RATHER THAN AUTHORED. The award count is THREE, derived rather than recalled: Run.SLOT_COUNT is 3 and _resolve_boss's own comment reads "THREE ZONE BOSSES, THEN A FOURTH BOSS AFTER ZONE 3 … the END BOSS awards NO ability pick". Measured against it, every spec's boss pool and the shortfall it carries before a single card is drafted:

  • holy 1 — also draftable 1, boss-only 0. SHORTFALL 2, and 3 if he ever drafts Divine Plea.
  • inquisitor 2 — both draftable, boss-only 0. SHORTFALL 1, up to 3.
  • berserker 3, pyromancer 3, cryomancer 3, occultist 3 — two draftable apiece, so up to 2 can roll empty.
  • warden 4, swordmaster 4, arcanist 4 — the Warden's three boss-only entries mean all three of his always land.
  • beastmaster 5, sharpshooter 5, mystic 5 — no overlap with their draft pools at all, so 3 of 3, always.

42 entries, 40 distinct names. Both channels write the same bm_abilities list, which is why a drafted card removes itself from the boss offer. The shape is the draft's inverted: the three Hunter specs DS had to deepen because their DRAFT pools were the shallowest in the game hold the DEEPEST boss pools, and the two Cleric specs hold the shallowest.

THE FALLBACK IS A DESIGN DECISION AND THE BRIEF WITHHELD IT, SO FOUR CANDIDATES ARE PRICED AND NONE IS TAKEN. A card from the spec's own draft pool it does not hold — costs nothing to build (bm_abilities already takes both channels) and is the closest thing to what the award promises, but it dissolves the distinction between the two channels and hands the boss the draft's job. A class-wide card — draws on CLASS_DRAFT_POOLS, which is live at 24, and would give the class channel a live purpose for the first time since AN, which is the only candidate that touches §1's finding; the cost is that class-wide cards are authored WEAKER than spec cards on purpose, so the fallback reads as a consolation prize. A rune — mechanically the cheapest of the four, because roll_rune_candidates and rune_picks_owed already exist and the map's owed-pick overlay already resolves them; it changes what the reward IS, which is either the point or the objection. Gold — free to build and the weakest, since a zone boss already pays randi_range(110, 130) and more of the same is not a reward the player will remember.

AND ONE THING THE PRICING TURNED UP THAT MAKES THE CARD-SHAPED OPTIONS WORTH LESS EXACTLY WHERE THE HOLE IS WORST. ABILITY_SLOT_CAP is 7 and the Holy Cleric carries FOUR protected cores — the only spec that does — so he has three earnable slots, the fewest in the game. The spec whose boss pool is emptiest is also the one with the least room to put a card in. Gold and a rune cost no slot; both card options do.

§3 — THE PLAYER MAY NO LONGER RECAST AN ARMED PHOENIX, AND THE REFUSAL IS EXACT. _resolve_special writes attacker.ashes_return = ASHES_RETURN_PERFECT unconditionally, so a hero who had already armed Ashes of Al'ar could recast it for 30 Mana and a turn, write the same constant and change nothing — every turn, at cooldown 0. The autoplay heuristic has refused exactly that since BB (u.ashes_return <= 0 and not u.ashes_used) and the player's door did not, so the bot was playing better than the player was permitted to. It is refused at _ability_usable now — the one door every other refusal uses — and the darkened button says which of the two reasons applies, because a greyed button with no reason is the bug CO §3 exists to prevent.

IT IS NOT IN RECAST_GATED, AND THAT WAS MEASURED RATHER THAN ARGUED. The brief called for membership; membership there is INERT and would have shipped as a change to nothing. That system reasons about STATUS writes — _recast_refused asks _recast_targets for a pool and _recast_writes for the chips a cast would lay — and ashes writes an integer FIELD. Driven live on an armed Mage: _recast_targets returns an empty array, so the loop never runs and _recast_refused returns false with the card fully armed. Adding the string to the list changes no behaviour whatever; it would only have taught check_co to report the card as never exercised. Inventing a pseudo-status to carry the field would put a name in _recast_refusal_note that STATUS_INFO cannot render and the player holds no chip for — a second path wearing the first one's clothes.

THE TEST COMPARES AGAINST WHAT THE CAST WOULD WRITE, NOT AGAINST ZERO, AND A NEGATIVE CONTROL PROVES THAT MATTERS. CO §1's rule is that a refusal blocking a cast which would in fact have done something is strictly worse than the waste being fixed. Written as ashes_return > 0 the gate refuses a genuine 25 → 40 improvement the day ASHES_RETURN acquires a caller; written as >= ASHES_RETURN_PERFECT it does not. Both forms were driven live and they disagree exactly there — see §6. ashes_used is its own arm because unit._ashes_guard() returns early forever once the phoenix has risen, so re-arming after that is inert whatever the field says. Reported and not collapsed: ASHES_RETURN (25) has no caller at all — the handler writes ASHES_RETURN_PERFECT (40) unconditionally — so it is a dead constant of exactly FIREDRAW_TAKE's shape, and collapsing it would move a magnitude.
