2026-09-01 — Batch EK: ARCHETYPE TAGS

A shared vocabulary that names what a card is FOR, carried by every ability in the corpus and every authored rune, and shown to the player on the draft card. MECHANICALLY INERT AS SHIPPED: no clause reads a tag, no card's behaviour changes and no magnitude moves. The vocabulary was MEASURED out of the corpus rather than proposed — and the measurement overturned the brief's own example. Full working: docs/reports/EK.md.

§1 — THE SIX, AND WHY THEY ARE MECHANICS RATHER THAN STATUS NAMES. The brief's example vocabulary was the status one ("the Pyromancer's Fireball is a Burn card"), and the corpus was measured before anything was authored. IT DOES NOT SUPPORT SIX STATUS NAMES. Grouped into families, the six biggest status systems in the game — Burn, Frost, Bleed, Poison, Ruin, Mark — reach 40 of the 154 draft cards, leaving 114 under no tag at all, and the brief's own test says a vocabulary with a large remainder is the wrong vocabulary. The reason is structural rather than accidental: a status system belongs to ONE spec, so inside the pool a player is actually drafting from it does not vary — the Pyromancer's pool is 12 Burn cards of 13, and a tag on twelve of the thirteen cards on offer tells the player nothing. The six shipped are AFFLICTION, SHELTER, BREAK, METER, AMP and CLOCK, they cover 154 of 154, and they vary inside every pool — five to ten distinct tag combinations against ten to thirteen cards. The status-family option is priced in the report so the designer can overturn this.

§1b — TWO OF THE SIX WERE RENAMED BEFORE SHIPPING, ON CJ's PRECEDENT. The BR §1 sweep ran against every ability, talent node, status label and rune name. AFFLICTION, BREAK, METER and AMP collide with nothing. WARD became SHELTER — Ward is a live status chip and the Rune of the Triage Ward carries the word — and TEMPO became CLOCK, because Tempo is a live chip AND three talent nodes. That is CJ's Iron Will collision exactly, and CJ's answer was to rename. A new vocabulary is free to move today and costs 227 rows to move later. And the engine check the brief asked for is clean: none of the six is one of DR's engine names, so an engine and a tag can never collapse into one word.

§2 — EVERY CARD TAGGED, DERIVED FROM THE READ SITE. 227 abilities and 65 runes, at most two tags each with the first the primary. The fields are useless on this corpus and that is CN's rule biting hard: heal is 0 on all 154 draft cards and 123 of them carry a special. A card's read site is its arm in battle._resolve_special, PLUS every block keyed on its display_name in the hero strike loop (58 abilities carry one, and Blood Debt's whole payload is one of them), PLUS the card-specific helpers those call, PLUS — for a setup card that resolves nothing at cast — whatever reads the status it lays. Aegis Wall applies aegis_wall and does nothing else; its healing lives in the BLOCK handler, and without that last hop it reads as untagged along with 39 others.

§3 — SHOWN ON THE DRAFT CARD, WHICH IS WHERE THE DECISION HAPPENS. One line under the card's own button and above the description, in its own tint, built by Classes.card_tag_line — CK §1's one-builder rule, one layer down. The layout cost is measured and not papered over: one 11px line a card, three cards a column, +42px on a column CK already measured at 557–671px against a 388px viewport. Nothing is clipped; the font was not shrunk and the computed block was not truncated. Where else they should appear is REPORTED and not done — the hero sheet, the battle tooltip, the rune offer and the blacksmith, each with its cost, in docs/reports/EK.md §3.

§4 — INERT BY DECISION, AND THE DECISION IS ASSERTED. No clause reads a tag count. No card's behaviour changes. No magnitude moves. Ship the vocabulary, let the designer see it on a card, then build on it — every alternative bets that the tag set is right before anyone has seen it in play, and 227 rows is an expensive bet to unwind. Inertness is the one property nothing else in the tree would notice losing, so check_ek §3 sweeps every .gd in the repo comment-stripped and requires the set of files naming a tag to be EXACTLY five, with ZERO asserted separately in the six files a mechanic would have to live in.

§5 — THE GATE, AND THE LIVE HALF THAT IS NOT IN IT. check_ek is new and joins GATES: coverage over Classes.ability_corpus(), the vocabulary pinned at six, inertness, the BR §1 sweep re-run LIVE so a future collision trips, and CK's one-builder rule. §1's population is the corpus walk and that is DA §3's rule applied rather than exempted — the first draft read both draft-pool accessors, check_da called it correctly, and the exemption was NOT taken: the corpus is a superset of the draft, the boss pools and the cores, so one assertion answers four questions and WALK_EXEMPT stays at two. A vocabulary that renders on nothing passes every static check in the tree (DS's Heads Down shape), so the draft card's tag line is driven live in check_map_screen, which now withholds its check_map_screen: OK verdict on a mismatch — that target carries no failure counter, so taking the pinned line away is the only way to turn its report into an assertion.
