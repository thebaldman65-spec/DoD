The payout is a multiplier on the DELAY, read from ONE function at THREE scheduling sites — the post-cast schedule, the gated-failure schedule and the initiative PREVIEW — rather than a seventh term in effective_speed(), which every next_time write divides by. Measured rather than argued: a control that adds one made the live delta read x0.6296 where the engine's own arithmetic says x0.6800.

§3 — SANCTITY IS AN EVENT COUNTER AND NOT TRAPPER'S STATE READER, AND ITS POTENCY LAYER WAS MEASURED BEFORE IT WAS BUILT. Trapper's breadth term reads a target's status LIST at strike time over the curated debuff allowlist; Sanctity counts APPLICATION EVENTS from any source on anyone. A status that lands and leaves is invisible to the first and is a real event to the second. The anti-farming rule is one sentence: ONE EVENT PER (TURN, BODY, STATUS) — eight apply-and-remove cycles on one body in one turn book ONE (the control that removes the dedupe reads 16), a refresh books nothing, a removal that removes nothing books nothing, and a cleanse books what it actually took. A natural expiry is NOT a removal, ruled here: a clock running out is time passing, not a hero acting.

AND THE POTENCY HALF WAS NOT BUILT, WHICH IS THE FINDING. STATUS_INFO holds 156 status ids and not one magnitude; strength arrives as the per-call power and tick arguments and everything else lives in the handler that reads the status. Duration is a parameter at all 249 authoring sites, so one line reaches every one of them. Potency is a parameter at 81, and 93 of the 156 declared ids carry no magnitude at any site at all — so a general potency multiplier would reach about a third of the statuses and read as working. What shipped is the staged version: DURATION ALONE, keyed on the APPLIER, as the fourth clause of a sentence the file already writes three times (Permafrost, Emberkeep and the two row-8 nodes all lengthen a status scoped to the src). Its coverage is COUNTED and printed rather than described: 214 _apply_status call sites, 110 carrying a source, 104 not.

§4 — NINE NEGATIVE CONTROLS, ALL NINE BIT, EACH WITH A DIFFERENT SIGNATURE. A hero given Channel in scripts/ (3 reds); the build half taught to read the ability (1); a seventh term in effective_speed() (2); the dedupe removed (4); the permanence guard off the duration payout (1); a payout's switch guard deleted (4); Channel no longer sparing physical (2); ONE of the three initiative read sites dropped (3 — and the live delta then read x1.0000, this project's most common shipped defect wearing its own name); and the cleanse door booking a count instead of the ids (1). The tree was restored byte-exactly afterwards.

§5 — THE THREE LIVE A/Bs ARE SEEDED, AND THAT IS THE INSTRUMENT'S OWN SCAR. randf_range(0.9, 1.1) is on every blow. Six-pair sums still left a physical control reading between x0.9537 and x1.0349 across three runs — a spread that swallows a real finding and manufactures a false one in equal measure. Re-seeding puts both arms on the same draw: Channel now reproduces its own arithmetic live at x1.1799 against x1.1800, the same card read as physical moves by x1.0000, and Momentum's live next_time delta is x0.6800 exactly. Three other confounds had to be closed first: the victim died on the control arm and the second arm read 0; the Arcanist's Resonance ramps with every cast and biased the ratio to x1.2422; and the first reading of every pair is a warm-up that measured 0 twice. The physical control had to become THE SAME CARD with its type changed — its first draft used the Sharpshooter's Aimed Shot and read x1.1015 on a payout that pays physical nothing, because his basic is a SEQUENCE and his Focus climbs.

§6 — master.html IS NOT EDITED AND ITS STAMP IS NOT BUMPED, AND THAT IS A RULING RATHER THAN AN OMISSION. That document shows only what is currently in the game (the designer's 07-20 rule), and nothing a player can meet changed: all three payouts return their identity value for every hero that exists. Three engines nobody can reach are not in the game, and a stamp is a claim about the document. It moves when a spine is attached.

FLAGGED, NOT TUNED — five things need the designer and none of them blocks the next batch. Channel's partition (not physical, or all damage including the basic attack); Channel's rate, where 40 Mana a step gives a Mage 2 steps by his fifth turn at the observed spend of about 16 Mana a turn — too slow to be felt in an ordinary fight and about right for a boss; Momentum's rate (8 steps x 4% off the delay, roughly a free turn every three); Sanctity's rate, which has to be read against the whole party's status traffic rather than against his own casting; and how a second meter DISPLAYS, where a chip is the existing answer (Faith, Loyalty and Ruin all use one) and costs nothing, but a chip cannot show a fill.
