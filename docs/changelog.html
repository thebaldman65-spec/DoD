§2 — MOMENTUM'S RATE IS AT ITS STOP, AND THE BRIEF'S PREMISE WAS THE REVERSE. The target was roughly a third of the cap by the end of a normal fight — 2.67 of 8 steps — on the premise that the meter would outrun it. It does not. At FT's one step an exchange, with a blow met counting, a trash fight ends at means of 1.84 (Berserker), 2.75 (Warden) and 2.27 (Swordmaster), and the cap is reached in under 2% of them. An exchange books at most once a turn, and two exchanges a step would put every spec near one step. So the rate stays one step an exchange, now a named constant, MOMENTUM_EXCHANGES_PER_STEP. The exchange is rare for structural reasons: the first span of a fight holds no action of his, his last action lands in a span the fight never closes, and in the rest the enemy reaches him in under half. The spread is fight length and how often each deals — the Warden takes 10.2 turns a trash fight to the Berserker's 6.7 — and a per-spec rate would close it by making a class core a spec engine.

AND IT DOES NOT COMPOUND — DRIVEN LIVE AGAINST STUBBED, AS THE BRIEF ASKED. With the payout switched on for the Warrior (in the probe, and only there), it buys him a quarter of a turn to a whole turn more a fight and moves the end-of-fight meter by +0.07, +0.12 and −0.01 steps in trash; in boss fights the meter falls. A hastened turn closes a shorter span, and the enemy reaches a shorter span less often — so late in a fight, where the brief expected the meter to feed itself hardest, the share of spans booking an exchange drops. No lower cap is needed; the report shows what one would do. And duration does not compound either: with Sanctity's payout live the three Cleric meters move by −0.10 to +0.07 steps, the one clear movement DOWN — the Devout's longer statuses land again less often.

§3 — SANCTITY'S RATE IS 16 EVENTS A STEP, ON THE HALF THAT EXISTS. FT's 6 was not a slow rate but a full meter: every Cleric party reached the cap in 78–97% of trash fights, because the ledger counts every status anyone applies to anyone. At 16 a normal fight ends at 3.03 (Devout), 2.06 (Holy) and 2.28 (Occultist) steps — 2.46 across the three against a target of 2.5. The spread is the party's traffic, not the Cleric's: the Devout's party makes 40% more events than the Holy's, and the Holy drives only one event in twenty of her own meter.

AND WHAT THE MISSING POTENCY HALF WOULD BE WORTH, SO THE RATE IS RE-READ RATHER THAN TRUSTED. Both halves are keyed on the applier, so the question is what each Cleric applies with himself as the source. For the Holy, everything her payout reaches carries a magnitude (Renewal, Vespers) — but that is 1.3 applications a fight. For the Devout, nothing: his reachable applications are all Consecrated Ground, and the Divine Shield's barrier — the one whose power is the absorb — is applied without a source. For the Occultist, 1.5%: his engine is Ruin, battle-long and magnitude-free at the funnel. 16 is a rate for this payout and is owed a re-reading the day the other half lands; CLAUDE.md says so where the potency batch will read it.

FT's TWO CONSTRAINTS STILL HOLD, AND THE BEAST HALF IS DRIVEN NOW. One status on a summoned Ursus is one event, the same status on its Beastmaster is a second body, and eight apply-and-remove cycles on the beast in one turn are still one. §2 — MOMENTUM READS THE EXCHANGE, AND “A TURN” IS THE SPAN BETWEEN HIS TURNS. Damage TAKEN per turn existed twice over; damage DEALT per turn did not exist in any shape, and building it was the batch's real work. The window is the one decision a reader will get wrong: battle_turn counts UNIT turns across the whole field, so a hero deals on index N and is struck back on N+3 — a meter keyed on that index would see the two halves of one exchange as different turns and read zero forever. It carries trance_taken's window instead, with two accumulators of its own because Battle Trance CONSUMES that field. The payout is a multiplier on the DELAY, read from ONE function at THREE scheduling sites — the post-cast schedule, the gated-failure schedule and the initiative PREVIEW — rather than a seventh term in effective_speed(), which every next_time write divides by. Measured rather than argued: a control that adds one made the live delta read x0.6296 where the engine's own arithmetic says x0.6800.

§3 — SANCTITY IS AN EVENT COUNTER AND NOT TRAPPER'S STATE READER, AND ITS POTENCY LAYER WAS MEASURED BEFORE IT WAS BUILT.
