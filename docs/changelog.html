  • The hypothesis, verified and corrected: the batch went in blaming the DOD_SIM_BUDGET default of 6.   • What the harness actually simulates (the answers every past sim number rests on): (1) every battle spawns a FRESH party at base power — the scene reloads between battles and only the stats dictionary survives. (4) Relics and boss trophies are absent (the DOD_SIM_RELICS / DOD_SIM_ABILITIES hooks exist but sim.sh never arms them). 2026-07-31 — Batch P: the Arcanist's ramp stops flatlining

  • The diagnosis: Resonance built +1 per damaging cast, capped at 5, and persisted all battle — so a RAMP spec hit its ceiling around turn five of a 7-9 round fight and then every further cast wasted its own mechanic. Backlash Ward paid exactly once per battle (it fired only on the transition INTO max), and Unlimited Power — one talent in one lane — was the only fix, which no spec should need just to stop wasting its resource. Stabilize was a reset, not a decision: it consumed ALL stacks, +75% damage to zero in one cast, which is why the bot only ever pulled it as a panic button.
  • Backlash Ward repeats: EVERY Resonance gain that arrives while already at maximum restores 15 Mana — a maxed Arcanist is a Mana engine fuelling the Cannon and Wrath casts he wants to be making at full stacks. Unlimited Power's overflow branch still takes priority when learned (verified in the log: Ward on the climb, Power at the top).
  • Stabilize leaves 2: consumes only the stacks ABOVE 2 — +5 Mana and +10% damage reduction (2 turns) per stack consumed, unusable at 2 or fewer, Perfect still heals 5%. At 5 stacks that is 15 Mana and 30% reduction while KEEPING +30% damage and the crit. The deliberate trade: the emergency full-vent got weaker (no more 50-80% walls) in exchange for venting often without erasing himself — the Berserker's Frenzy floor, the Pyromancer's ember, the Cryomancer's remainder.
  • Tree: the Arcanist moves off the last Batch 31 conversion onto purpose-designed node-gated lanes (tiers at 2/4 nodes in-lane, capstone at 6) — every tree in the game is now purpose-built. RESONANCE is the stack engine, OVERLOAD the risk half, CONTROL survival. Suppressing Fire and Temporal Rift move Control → Overload (an offensive Barrage ramp and a crit echo were never Control). Every id survives and the twelve conversion fillers re-spec in place, so saved ranks migrate: Harmonics (was an Arcane Mastery duplicate → Arcane Explosion +1 Resonance/rank ×2), Conduit (was an Attunement echo → +2%/rank damage per stack ×3), Resonant Core (was +max HP → max Resonance +1/rank ×2), Charged Bolts (was an Explosion dial → damaging casts at max restore 5%/rank max Mana ×3), Cannoneer (was flat Cannon damage → its per-stack term +2.5%/rank ×3), Barrage Master (was a Mana discount → +1 bolt/rank ×2), Volatility (was flat crit → Cannon and Wrath +5%/rank damage AND recoil ×3), Arcane Ward (was +armor → the damage-taken penalty falls 1%/rank per stack ×3), Still Mind (was flat damage reduction → Stabilize leaves +1 stack/rank ×2), Feedback Loop (was Meltdown, a dmg-dealt/taken dial → 10%/rank of recoil paid as Mana ×3, pairing with Conversion so a committed Control build turns self-harm into fuel). The cross-lane Meltdown ↔ Still Mind exclusive is gone; the new exclusive is Arcane Ward ↔ Still Mind, in-lane in Control — run permanently hot, or vent constantly for almost nothing: never stabilise, or stabilise all the time.
  • Capstones: Magi's Wrath (Overload, unchanged), Singularity (was a stat pile → Resonance has NO maximum, the damage-taken penalty stops rising past 5 stacks; with no cap there is no overflow, so Ward/Power payouts retire for that build), Master of Moments (was a stat bundle → Stabilize consumes nothing and pays full value from current stacks, every 3 turns).
  • Bot rotation: Stabilize at 4+ stacks below 80% health (was max-stacks below 70% — correct for a full reset, far too conservative for a vent). The sim bot now vents 0.9 times per battle instead of almost never.
  • Verified live: both scenes parse clean; the lane audit passes (all 12 trees 24 nodes / 3 capstones / 7-7-7; arcanist node-gated, exclusive pair in-lane, all 24 ids preserved). Autoplay: Backlash Ward fires three casts in a row at max; Stabilize logs "3 stacks vented (2 remain)" and the bot vents mid-fight repeatedly; with Unlimited Power force-learned the overflow converts instead of triggering the Ward; Charged Bolts, Feedback Loop, Resonant Core (cap 7), Singularity (9 banked, no Ward) and Master of Moments ("5 stacks channelled, none consumed") all proc on cue. 2026-07-31 — Batch O: the Cryomancer's cold finally holds

  • The diagnosis: his win condition was 4 Chilled stacks on ONE target, but his main applier (Razor Ice) scattered stacks across 2 RANDOM enemies, every reapplication reset a shared 3-turn clock so whole piles evaporated at once, and the freeze itself WIPED the stacks — deleting Hypothermia and Hungering Cold, the per-stack auras, at the exact moment they mattered. Concentrating 4 stacks on a chosen enemy was close to luck, and the payoff destroyed the engine (the same fault the Berserker's bleedout and the Pyromancer's detonation had). Half the passive was dead weight besides: a 25% Frostbite proc whose −50% healing does nothing against the many warbands with no healer.
  • Permafrost v2 — the name has always meant this: Chilled stacks applied by the Cryomancer never expire (scoped to HIS applications; chill from any other source keeps the 3-turn clock). Frozen enemies still take 15% increased damage from all sources. The Frostbite clause leaves the passive — Rime still applies it on cast, where the status is chosen rather than rolled. And freezing now leaves 1 Chilled stack instead of 0 (the Berserker's Frenzy-floor principle: cashing in shouldn't erase the engine), so the rebuild starts from something and the per-stack auras keep ticking. A boss that resists the freeze keeps sitting on all 4 stacks — Break it, chill it once, and the ice takes hold.
  • Kit: Razor Ice concentrates instead of scattering — THREE shards at a SINGLE chosen target, one stack each (20 Mana / 20% Atk / 10 BD / 2.5 / 3cd unchanged; Perfect still 25% per shard). Blizzard is worth its cost at 30 Mana and 3.5 initiative (was 35/4.0) — with permanent stacks its field-wide 1-2 stacks are real preparation, not evaporation. Ice Lance gains +10% of Attack per Chilled stack on the target (65% at three stacks) — it finally pulls its weight BEFORE the freeze, not just during the auto-crit.
  • Tree: the Cryomancer moves off the Batch 31 conversion onto purpose-designed node-gated lanes (tiers at 2/4 nodes in-lane, capstone at 6): DEEP FREEZE reaches and holds Frozen, SHATTERPOINT is the burst payoff, WINTER is the field aura. Capstones: Shatter (unchanged), Absolute Zero (re-spec: freezes keep ALL 4 stacks and the target refreezes the moment its freeze ends — total lockdown, every per-stack aura permanently maxed against it), Eternal Winter (re-spec: EVERY enemy gains a stack at the start of each of his turns — inevitability).

Autoplay receipts: Blizzard preps the field ("never thaws" on every stack), Razor concentrates the Chief to a freeze, the freeze leaves x1 (15 of 15 freezes in the 8-battle sample), the pile rebuilds 2-3-4 and refreezes the SAME target — the cycle the spec was designed around; an uncritted 59-damage Ice Lance at 3 stacks (35% + 30% — the formula lands exact). 2026-07-31 — Batch N: the Pyromancer's burn engine finally ignites

  • The diagnosis: Flamewave extended Burn but never APPLIED it — the spec's only AoE could not start a fire — and Wildfire's spread was strict-Adjacent, so corpses choked it. The only reliable igniter was the single-target Fireball, which made Inferno Master's five-burning-enemy cap physically unreachable: the passive promised a payoff the kit could not deliver.
  • Kit: Flamewave now applies 2 turns of Burn to every enemy — already-burning targets gain +2 turns instead, the existing behaviour (Perfect: 3 either way). Wildfire spreads the target's Burn to EVERY other living enemy at half duration rounded up (was Adjacent-only and corpse-blocked; the pre-hit capture stays, so a killing blast still spreads). Detonation becomes a real Nuker payoff: 25 Mana (was 20), 25% Atk (was 15%), and it consumes 150% of the burn's remaining damage (tick × turns left × 1.5; Perfect re-applies 2 turns, was 1).
