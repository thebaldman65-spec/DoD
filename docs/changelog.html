DAWN OF DECAY — Changelog

Newest first. The Master Document holds current truth; this records what changed and when. (Earlier history lives in the retired Implementation Addendum.)

2026-07-12 — Master Document audit

  • Full consistency pass over the Master Document against everything shipped since 07-09: fixed spots where later batches had superseded earlier text — initiative-bar portrait size, the 180px/144px nameplate contradiction, hotkeys described as raw kit slots (they map to menu slots since the summon picker took W), the old "Summon Companion ▸ submenu" wording, "Items ▾" (now the [Alt] Items picker), the Berserker/Pyromancer size-test framing (the test is decided — Pyromancer size is the template), duplicate section number 4.8, and the relic pool count (5). §13 Known Gaps rewritten around the current art pipeline, the unassigned design hooks (Weaknesses, Counter Attack), and the honest sim win rate.

2026-07-12 — Batch 9: parry rework, new Cleric abilities, Alt items, compact UI

  • Sprite template DECIDED: the larger (Pyromancer) size wins — all character art going forward is authored at the 236px/1:1 template. The Berserker's 124px sheets are legacy-sized until re-generated.
  • Parry reworked: a parried hit now lands with 75% less damage and 75% less Break damage instead of being negated — and no longer triggers an automatic counter. Counter Attack is now a defined term: answering a parry with an immediate basic attack, granted only by specific effects (nothing grants it yet).
  • New — Resurrection (all Cleric specs): MIRACLE, 40 Faith, 4.0 int — returns a fallen ally to life with 20% health and resource.
  • New — Divine Wrath (Devout): 25 Mana, 3.0 int — the party deals +15% damage and acts +15% faster for 3 turns (Perfect: 4).
  • New — Umbral Sigil (Occultist): MIRACLE, 20 Faith, 3.0 int — brand one enemy; every other member of its party takes 50% of all attack damage the branded target receives (3 turns, Perfect: 4).
  • Occultist identity: his Smite is corrupted into Shadowrend (same numbers, shadow damage); Mind Flay now costs 30 Mana instead of 25 Faith. Regular Smite stays holy.
  • Tuning & damage types: Overpower costs 30 Rage (was 20); Poisoned Arrow and the Orc Archer's Poison Arrow deal nature damage; Shrapnel Charge deals fire (Explosive Shot already did).
  • Alt — item inventory: Alt toggles an item picker (Tab cycles, Space uses, X closes); the Items button opens the same window. Space continues past victory screens.
  • Compact UI: all battle UI downsized ~20% — nameplates 144px, smaller initiative portraits, log, action bar, popups, pickers, and skill-check bar — the battlefield owns the screen now.
  • Rules clarified in the doc: talent node ranks are additive (every extra point adds the same stated amount).

2026-07-12 — Batch 8: summon picker on W, Pyromancer size-test sprite

  • Summon Companion reworked: it now sits at the TOP of the Beastmaster's ability list on hotkey W. Picking it opens a beast picker window — Tab cycles Ursus / Canis / Aguila (skipping unaffordable ones), Space summons the highlighted beast, X closes, clicks still work. The rest of the kit shifts keys accordingly (Barbed Arrow E, Poisoned Arrow R, Kill Command A). Hotkeys now map to menu slots rather than raw ability indices.
  • Pyromancer test sprite: the east-facing pose from the new imported rotation (file was named "cryomancer test" but the art is the fire-torch Pyromancer) is in-game as a size experiment — native 236px frame at 1:1, untinted, ~55% taller than the Berserker, so the two sprite densities can be compared side by side before locking the character template. Static pose only until animations exist; own-art specs now live in one SPEC_ART table.

2026-07-11 — Batch 7: spec names, portraits, plate polish, X-cancel, walking Berserker

  • Spec names everywhere: once awakened, heroes go by their spec — "Berserker", not "Warrior" — on battle nameplates, the initiative bar, the combat log, and the party screen (list + detail title). The class name only shows pre-awakening. (Internals now key on the class id, so the sim bot and bow sounds are unaffected.)
  • Nameplates polished: ~20% smaller (180px) so the hero stack no longer overlaps, and each plate now carries a portrait — dedicated art when the unit has it (the new Berserker portrait, also used in the initiative bar and party list), else a sprite close-up. The turn indicator is now the plate itself: the acting unit's plate glows with a gold border (the yellow ▼ arrow is gone), and hovering or Tab-targeting a unit lights its plate white.
  • X cancels: whenever a Cancel button is on screen (targeting, cancellable skill check), the X key presses it.
  • Walking Berserker: units with real locomotion art walk to melee range and back using their walk animation (facing the way they move) instead of the abstract lunge — currently the Berserker; new characters get it as their sheets arrive.
  • Summon hotkeys surfaced: the collapsed "Summon Companion ▸" entry shows its beasts' keys ("[W/E/R]") — pressing the key summons directly without opening the submenu.
  • Combat log can hide: a small – button tucks the log away; a "Log" button brings it back.
  • Background at maximum zoom-out: battle art now renders at the smallest scale that still fills the screen. Making the scenery read smaller than this needs background art authored to the Berserker resolution template (next art milestone).

2026-07-11 — Batch 6: nameplates, Tab controls, turn lock, native-res background

  • Nameplates: every unit's name, HP bar, resource bar(s), Break meter, and status chips moved off the sprites onto nameplate stacks behind each party — heroes' plates along the left screen edge, enemies' along the right. Hovering a plate highlights its unit on the field; dead units' plates dim. Both parties are grouped tighter on the field now that the sprites carry no UI. Plate styling is a plain placeholder until the full UI asset set arrives.
  • Tab controls: Tab opens/closes the Abilities list while choosing an action; after an ability is picked, Tab cycles through the available targets and Space/Enter confirms. A full turn is playable from the keyboard: hotkey → Tab → Space → Space on the skill check.
  • Debug turn lock: the battle debug tools moved into a DEBUG ▾ menu (bottom-right) and "Hero Turn Now" was replaced by a proper turn lock — pick a hero and every turn is theirs until you unlock or switch heroes, for testing a kit over many consecutive turns.
  • Background at native size: battle art is no longer overscanned ~30% — it scales to just cover the 1280×720 view (plus a shake margin). The Berserker sheet is the official resolution template going forward (figures ≈60–90px in ~124px frames at ×1.25; backgrounds authored ≈1280×720, shown native).
  • Berserker untinted: heroes with their own art keep original file colors — the draft-slot tint no longer recolors the Berserker.

2026-07-10 — Batch 5: Weaknesses, ability hotkeys, Berserker test sprite, Renewal

  • New mechanic — Weaknesses: a unit can be WEAK to a damage type, taking 25% extra damage from it (stacks with resists; weak-to-nature also amplifies Poison ticks). Weakness hits flash WEAK! over the target and are tagged in the combat log. Who is weak to what will be assigned later — for now the Ash-Wrought Tyrant's frost vulnerability uses the new mechanic.
  • Ability hotkeys: Q / W / E / R / A / S / D / F pick abilities by kit slot during your turn (Q = basic attack), shown on every button ("[W] Bloodlust"). Unaffordable or gated abilities ignore their key; hotkeys work even with the Abilities menu open, but never during targeting or skill checks.
  • Berserker test sprite: the three GIFs from imported files (idle / move / attack) are converted into game sheets — a Warrior specced Berserker now fights with the fur-cloaked axeman in battle. Native 124px frames at 1.25× scale, feet aligned with the party line. Missing clips fall back gracefully (Attack02/03 replay Attack01; Hurt/Death hold the idle frame) until those animations arrive.
  • Renewal is Holy-only (leaves the Cleric core kit) and heals 15 HP per turn (was 8); a Perfect cast now also heals 15 instantly (was 8). Still 20 Mana, 5 turns, 3.0 initiative.

2026-07-09 — Batch 4: new orcs, Warden rework, Dazed, encounter + combat rules

  • New enemy — Orc Shieldmaster (orange): 150 HP / 25% armor / 85 speed / 120 Constitution. Shielding wards one ally at a time (lowest HP first, itself included) with 25% damage reduction for 3 turns — it ALWAYS re-casts when nobody holds the ward; otherwise Strike (28 physical / 20 BD). New "Shielded" status chip.
  • New enemy — Orc Shaman (blue): 110 HP / 1% armor / 100 speed / 80 Constitution, ranged. Lightning Bolt (35 nature / 20 BD) or Chain Lightning (15 nature + 15 BD to the whole party).
  • Withered Warden reworked (zone 1 boss, no longer a Chief stand-in): 500 HP / 35% armor / 75% nature resist / 80 speed / 150 Constitution. Timber Slam (60 nature / 30 BD + Dazed), Roots of Wrath (25 nature / 20 BD to the whole party + Poison), Wild Growth (heals the most wounded of its warband for 20% max HP; 60% chance when someone is under 70%).
  • New status — Dazed: attacks are 20% more likely to miss, 3 turns.
  • Encounters: every fight now fields 3–5 enemies with types rolled at random from the basic pool (Raider / Archer / Shieldmaster / Shaman). Elites (Chief) appear only on elite nodes, bosses only on the boss node (each brings 2–4 random basics).
  • Combat rules: enemy parry baseline is now 2.5% (heroes stay at 5%); bleedout damage explicitly ignores armor; Poison now counts as nature damage, so nature resists shrink the ticks (the Warden barely feels it).
  • Hero info screen: each stat now explains itself when hovered (instead of one tooltip dumping every stat); the talent-point line left the stats block (the count already sits above the talent tree); the requirements text below the talent tree is gone (lock reasons still show on node tooltips).
  • Battle debug panel: per-hero "Turn →" buttons replaced by one Hero Turn Now button (the next turn goes to a hero regardless of order) plus an Enemy attacks OFF toggle (enemies skip their turns while checked). New DOD_SIM_ENEMIES env var forces the enemy lineup in test battles.
  • Docs: the Word exports (Master Document + Changelog) now build in Arial, size 14.

2026-07-09 — Batch 3: spec-save fix, always-on dev debug, Death Ray gate

  • Fix — reroll reverting talents: the run was never saved after spec confirmation, so quitting before completing another node made Continue resurrect the old specs and trees (this is what made the Berserker tree "revert"). Specs now save immediately on confirmation and on debug reroll, and loading migrates old saves to current fixed trees (points refunded).
  • Fix — missing battle debug menu: debug tools were gated behind the DOD_DEBUG env var, which was only set when launched from the assistant. They are now always available in dev (unexported) builds — exported releases still hide them.
  • Death Ray: castable only at 5 Arcane Resonance stacks (greyed out otherwise); its 150 damage is a fixed payoff — the per-stack damage bonus no longer double-dips on it.

2026-07-09 — Batch 2: talent tree styling, battle debug panel, Arcanist identity

  • Talent tree restyled to match the "Appearance example" reference: dark backdrop with faint grid lines, 72px square emblem nodes (gold border on hover, green when maxed), point counters beneath each node, black hover tooltip with white bold name + yellow description, click-to-learn. Backdrop and node icons are placeholders for future art.
  • Battle debug panel (DOD_DEBUG=1, under the combat log): Full Restore (HP/resources/secondaries) and per-hero "Turn →" buttons to hand any hero the next turn.
  • Fix — Poison instant procs: the "no tick on the turn applied" rule was never wired into the damage loop, so Poisoned Arrow's stacks all ticked immediately on the victim's next turn start. Poison now genuinely skips its first tick.
  • Fix — Explosive Shot BD readout: the log printed the ability's base BD while the meter moved by the post-Constitution amount (85-Constitution archers take ~18% more, 130+ chiefs less — that's the per-enemy variation, by design; crits also deal ×1.5 BD). Logs now report the applied BD so the numbers always match the meter.
  • Arcane Resonance is now the Arcanist's passive. The Mage class no longer has the Resonance bar — only the Arcanist spec gains it (Pyromancer/Cryomancer never see it). The Echo passive is vaulted (removed, kept for later).
  • New — Death Ray (Arcanist): free cast, 150 arcane damage / 100 BD / 8.0 int; benefits from Resonance, then CONSUMES every stack. Perfect restores 15 Mana.

2026-07-09 — Batch 1: fixes, tuning pass, Mana Shield, Berserker tree

  • Fixes: every skill check can now be cancelled, including after picking a target (the "healing wave" case); enemy targeting spreads out — 40% chance to hunt the weakest (was 65%), so the Mage stops eating every hit; rune equip logic was verified working across multiple heroes simultaneously (data + save round-trip tested) — equipped runes now show a green ✦ marker for clarity; Poison no longer ticks on the turn it lands; the Cryomancer's Chilled is now its own status separate from generic Slowed (same −25% speed); Eidolon Mirror removed from the relic pool (existing saves drop it safely).
  • Balance: all enemies +15% HP (Raider 132, Archer 104, Chief 242, bosses 368); Cripple now −25% damage (was −15%).
  • Terminology: damage against the Break meter is now Break damage (BD) in all tooltips and documentation.
  • Ability tuning: Overpower 15 base damage (still +0.5 per point of target Break), Perfect gives +5 Rage; Crushing Blow 20 BD, Perfect +5 BD; Flame Surge 15 damage / 10 BD / 3.0 int, no longer applies Burn, Perfect +15 damage to already-Burning enemies; Echo now strikes at 25% power (was 50%); Arcane Cannon 15 BD, recoil is a flat 15% of damage dealt (no per-stack scaling), Perfect +5 BD; Quick Shot and Powershot lose their +10 Focus perfects; Barbed Arrow and Poisoned Arrow 15 BD / 2.5 int; Aimed Shot 45 damage / 15 BD / 3.0 int, Perfect +25% crit; Powershot 3.0 int with plainer wording; Mocking Blow 20 damage / 15 BD / 2.0 int, builds 20 Rage, taunts the target AND one other enemy for 4 turns (Perfect 5); Shieldwall 3.0 int, party takes 25% less damage for 2 turns (Perfect 3); Summon Canis 20 damage / 20 Bleed; Tripwire tooltip simplified; Shrapnel renamed Shrapnel Charge — 20 damage / 25 BD, applies Slowed (not Chilled).
  • New — Mana Shield (Mage core, replacing Arcane Surge): 15 Mana, 2.0 int, for 3 turns 50% of damage the Mage takes converts into Mana; Perfect casts at 1.5 int. Arcane Surge and Reality Fracture are vaulted (removed from kits, definitions kept for later).
  • Berserker talent tree v2 — built from the RPG Skill Tree Generator export (data/berserker_tree.json): a fixed 3×3 tree. Tier 1: Savagery (+2 Bleed on bleed abilities /rank ×3), Bloodthirsty (Blood Frenzy +3%/rank ×3), Vitality (+5% max HP/rank ×3). Tier 2: Crushing Force (ignore 5% armor/rank ×3), Rampage (new ability: +1% damage per 10 Bleed buildup on the enemy party for 1 turn, 20 Rage), Reckless Fury (+5% damage dealt AND taken /rank ×3). Tier 3: Bloodlust (Hack and Slash strikes an extra time), Bloodcraze (heal 30 when an enemy bleeds out), Enraged (+1%/rank damage per hit taken, fades after 2 unhurt turns ×3). Gating: Tier 2 unlocks at 5 points in Tier 1; Tier 3 at 10 points across Tiers 1–2. The party screen renders fixed trees three columns wide.
  • Docs: Master Document converted through a new build script that unwraps paragraphs (fixes the strange spacing in Word); this changelog created alongside it.
