2026-08-29 — Batch DS: THE HUNTER CLASS GAP

DQ's largest structural finding, ruled and paid. The three Hunter specs held the three shallowest pools in the game (eight apiece), received none of DO's twenty-two, and not one of their twenty-four spec cards was a heal, a shield or hero-side mitigation — a hunter's entire defensive option set for a whole run was two of the six Hunter class cards he had to be lucky enough to be offered. It compounded with the Sharpshooter holding no defensive node in his 27, so both halves of his progression offered him nothing. Six cards, all three pools 8 → 10, matching the Cleric class. The draft goes 143 → 149.

§1 — SIX CARDS, TWO PER SPEC, AND EACH SPEC'S DEFENCE IS SHAPED BY ITS OWN ENGINE. That is DR's standing rule applied rather than a new one: MIT-SELF is a shared AXIS and adding it dilutes nothing, because the engine still gates every one of them.

  • BEAR THE BRUNT (Beastmaster, 20 Mana, 1.0, 4cd) — the next blow that would fell him is REFUSED and his deepest bond takes all of it, and it can kill the beast. Battle-long, waits until it fires. It is BLOODBOND exactly inverted, clause for clause, and the pair is meant to be read together on the draft screen.
  • BRING IT DOWN (Beastmaster, 25 Mana, 2.0, 5cd) — for 4 turns EVERY hero deals 2% more damage per stack of Loyalty on his deepest bond, up to +20%, snapshot at cast and the bond is not spent. AMP-TEAM, absent from all twenty-four, and the answer to a pool measured 8-of-8 on one engine: it makes the other three heroes care what the Loyalty meter reads.
  • DUG IN (Sharpshooter, 20 Mana, 1.0, 4cd) — for 4 turns he takes a quarter of whatever Focus stands when the blow lands less damage, up to 25%. Read live, so it rises as he commits and falls to nothing when a switch breaks the meter. Deliberately not evasion: Camouflage and Ghillie Suit already stack, and Camouflage's own header reserves evasion as the class's shape so it never overlaps a Barrier or a Block.
  • HEADS DOWN (Sharpshooter, 25 Mana, 2.0, 4cd, 20 dmg / 8 BD) — for 3 turns (Perfect: 4) that enemy can bring nothing but its basic attack to bear. The first silence in the project, written as a downgrade rather than a lost turn so it is not a fourth Stun.
  • THICK HIDE (Survivalist, 20 Mana, 1.0, 4cd) — for 4 turns he takes 6% less damage per DIFFERENT affliction standing on whoever is striking him, up to 30%. The first card that reads Trapper breadth on the way IN, and Stalking Horse is what fills it.
  • SALVE (Survivalist, 25 Mana, 2.0, 5cd) — for 3 turns (Perfect: 4) he heals 2% of maximum health at the start of his turn per DIFFERENT affliction on the enemies, up to 10%, counted once each across the field so a copied poison does not pay twice. The first heal in any of the twenty-four Hunter spec cards, and HARVEST's deliberate inverse: that one burns the board for a burst and strips his own Trapper bonus, this drinks and consumes nothing.

§2 — THE BRIEF ASKED FOR A BREAK CARD AND THE MEASUREMENT REFUSED IT. The premise was "the Hunter class has no Break generation anywhere". Twelve of the thirty Hunter draft cards generate Break — pressure IS Break, so a card generates it without ever saying the word. UNLEASH already lands Break on the Beastmaster's own pool and FAULT LINE is a dedicated Break card the audit scores as one of the Sharpshooter's five distinct decisions. The proposed card would have been a second copy of a clause already in its own pool. AMP-TEAM was taken instead, and the rule is in CLAUDE.md: before declaring an axis ABSENT from a class, derive it from the DATA, never from the card text.

§3 — THE SHARPSHOOTER RE-AUTHOR WAS MEASURED AND DECLINED. Four of his eight cards are single-target strikes, the concentration the Swordmaster had at DR. Compared at the read site rather than by shape, they are four different decisions: Crossfire converts crits into area damage and reads no Focus at all; Trophy Shot is a decay-RULE change read inside _sharpshooter_focus's victim.dead branch; Calibrating Shot and Drumfire are the only two that are alike, both being meter GENERATION on a strike — and they are not a domination, splitting on tempo (initiative 1.5 against 3.0) and on damage. Nothing was re-authored, which is what the brief's own escape clause asked for if the four turned out distinct. One thing the audit's DMG-ST label hides is recorded instead: Trophy Shot and Reacquire are near-siblings — both are "do not lose Focus when the target changes".

§4 — TWO STALE CLAIMS CORRECTED, BOTH FOUND BY THIS BATCH'S OWN WORK. FIELD DRESSING said "The only self-heal a Hunter can get" and it was already false when BR wrote it: HARVEST heals, and it sits in the Survivalist's boss pool and in CLASS_POOLS["hunter"]. Salve would have made it wrong a third way. §5 — WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE. No talent node moves — the Sharpshooter's missing defensive node is real and is not this batch's, because a cell that changes row mis-prices the ledger. 2026-08-28 — Batch DR: THE ENGINE / AXIS FRAMEWORK, AND THE SWORDMASTER'S POOL

DQ found two grade-2 dominations and measured that the pools vary enormously. This batch repairs both, widens the most concentrated pool in the game, and records the framework the audit made visible so future pools are authored against it rather than by feel. The draft moves twice and the net is +1: 142 → 143.

§1 — THE FRAMEWORK, AND ONE OF THE THREE "EXCLUSIVE AXES" WAS NOT ONE. Recorded in CLAUDE.md: an ENGINE is the spec's own currency (stances, Loyalty, Focus, Resonance, Ruin, Faith, Mercy, Burn, Chilled, Frenzy, Block) and is exclusive by construction; an AXIS is an effect type (single-target damage, area damage, healing, shielding, mitigation, control, tempo, Break, resource generation, meter manipulation) and is shared, deliberately. A pool is one exclusive engine plus a selection of shared axes, and adding axes to a spec does not dilute its identity — the engine still gates everything.

  • COMPANION SUMMONING — the Beastmaster. TRUE, verified over the whole corpus: special: "summon" exists on exactly three abilities, all of them his protected core, and _do_summon's only other caller is his own draft card Call the Wilds.
  • REVIVAL — the Holy Cleric. TRUE AMONG ABILITIES, and the brief asked this be verified and reported. BattleUnit.revive() has two callers — resurrection (hers) and the REVIVE POTION — and the revive_pct map event is a third channel. Exclusive as an ability; not exclusive as a channel.
  • COOLDOWN MANIPULATION IS NOT THE SWORDMASTER'S, AND THE ASSERTION WAS NOT AUTHORED. The brief held that Answering Steel and Battle Poise are "the only cards in the game that do it". _tick_cooldowns has SEVEN callers: those two, BLINK — a MAGE CLASS-WIDE DRAFT CARD whose own comment names tempo as its axis, BLESSING OF ZEAL — the Devout's PROTECTED CORE, and three talent nodes (Frostbound Hours, which ticks EVERY hero's; Practised Hands; Follow-Through). Five more sites clear a cooldown outright through cooldowns.erase. What is one implementation is the FUNCTION (BQ extracted four hand-written copies); what is shared is the axis. A single implementation reads like a single owner and is not the same thing — that is the rule now recorded.

§2 — FLASH FREEZE IS RETIRED, AND THE COMMENT SUSPENDING THE RULE OVER IT WAS DELETED RATHER THAN UPDATED. DQ's Finding 1: comments and cosmetics stripped, its handler and Glacial Prison's were the same three steps in the same order, and Glacial Prison is cheaper (25 v 30), shorter on cooldown (4 v 5), faster (2.5 v 3.0) and is in RECAST_GATED where Flash Freeze was not — so the dearer card was also the only one of the two that could be spent on an enemy already held. The Cryomancer's pool goes 12 → 11.

The comment suspended "no ability may be a strictly better version of another in the same pool" for two named reasons, and both were dead: the acquisition channel died at DO and the Perfect at CR, and neither batch touched the comment. A DOCUMENTED EXCEPTION THAT OUTLIVES ITS JUSTIFICATIONS IS WORSE THAN AN UNDOCUMENTED ONE, BECAUSE IT READS AS DELIBERATE — recorded in CLAUDE.md's traps. Thirteen live sites read the card by name (classes.gd ×3, battle.gd ×5, test_batch_bt ×5 including _one_shield_door's slice anchor, test_batch_cb, test_batch_ax, check_cv, CLAUDE.md ×3, master.html ×2); none in runes, talents or the glossary. The mechanic is untouched — four Chilled stacks still flash-freeze, which is _apply_status's branch and belongs to no card.

AND ONE MORE STALE COUNT OF THE SAME SPECIES, FOUND WHILE REMOVING IT. _hold_freeze's header said THREE callers while DO's Cryoclasm had quietly made it four, and named a force argument no caller has passed since CR. Corrected with its reason.

§3 — BATTLE POISE: THE DEFENSIVE GUARD BUYS A FREE GUARD CHANGE. DQ's Finding 2 was that its whole payload was a subset of Answering Steel's — cheaper (20 v 25), longer (6 turns v 4), ungated, paying the same _tick_cooldowns constant plus +20% parry and 15 Rage a parry. Its stance requirement was pure cost with nothing bought by it. ONCE A TURN, A PARRY NOW LETS HIM GUARD CHANGE WITHOUT SPENDING AN ACTION. Answering Steel cannot have it — it has no requirement to reward — so the two are no longer nested, and the stacking-partners comment at the parry site stands: it answered do these combine and never answered which do you take.

  • THE PIVOT ONLY, _swordmaster_switch. Guard Change's own payload — its 15 BD, SUNDER GUARD's 40 BD to every enemy, No Quarter, Tempo — stays on that card. A Defensive Swordmaster holding Sunder Guard and parrying twice a turn would otherwise land 80 free Break damage across the field every turn.
  • ONCE A TURN, not once a parry, and it does not bank. The cooldown tick stays and still pays on every parry.
  • IT ASKS _ability_usable ABOUT THE REAL GUARD CHANGE — the same door — which makes three promises true at once and keeps them from disagreeing: FORMLESS refuses it (there is no stance to change), Guard Change's own 1-turn cooldown is respected, and it starts that cooldown, because respecting a cooldown a use does not pay into is respecting it exactly once.
  • THE PIVOT IS NOT FREE OF CONSEQUENCE: it throws away DISCIPLINE's accumulation, like every other call to the one pivot. A Discipline build wants it refused and gets that by not holding the card.

§4 — THE SWORDMASTER GAINS FOUR AXES ON THREE CARDS, 10 → 12. DQ measured his as the most concentrated pool in the game: ten cards making four decisions — strike one enemy (four), change or hold a stance (three), buy tempo off a parry (two), execute (one) — so a player who had drafted four had seen everything it decides. Every one of the three reads the stance engine, because that is what makes it his, and BW's rule decides how: READERS BRANCH AND FLIP; GATED ONES REQUIRE AND STAY.

  • LUNGE, RE-AUTHORED ONTO BREAK — the sharpest gap in the pool. An entire BREAKER lane is built around a Break window (Punishment, Off Balance, Guard Breaker, Overpressure, No Quarter all live inside it, and Sever's cooldown clears against a Broken target so it can be swung through) and not one draft card opened it. Two changes and nothing else moved: cooldown 0 → 3 — of 142 cards only it and Pyroblast repeated every turn, where all twelve cooldown-zero abilities in the protected cores are the free basic attack, and at the end of a lane the price was the node — and the guard now decides where the Break lands: AGGRESSIVE puts 15 more into that one enemy, DEFENSIVE puts 12 on every other enemy, because he does not overreach and the recovery leaves the whole line off balance. Damage 35 → 30, because it is a Break card now; Shatterpoint keeps its title as his heaviest single Break blow at 40. THE BRIEF SAID SHATTERPOINT IS A TALENT AND IT IS NOT — it is a SPEC_POOLS boss pick, which is why the lane depended on an offer he may never see. The card stays an ORDINARY ATTACK (a special would hand-roll the blow and lose the crit, the armour read, the parry roll, Overwhelm, Off Balance and Whetstone), and both riders read _eff_stance beside the wound it already left.
  • WHEELING CUT — AREA DAMAGE and SELF-MITIGATION, the two axes on one card. 30 Rage, 2.5, 4cd, every enemy takes 20% of Attack and 12 Break. His pool had no area damage at all; his only field-wide option for a whole run was Cleave out of the Warrior class pool. A reader, so BP's arriving-stance principle decides both branches — the thing that would most easily be got backwards: cast from AGGRESSIVE he lands DEFENSIVE, so it hands him defence (20% less damage taken, 3 turns); cast from DEFENSIVE he lands AGGRESSIVE, so it hands him offence (+20% damage dealt, 3 turns). Perfect: both guards' gifts at once — it runs a bar, so test_batch_bo §5's biconditional requires it to state one, and this batch adds no third name to DO's two debts there.
  • COUNTER TIME — CONTROL, and his pool could not take an enemy's turn away at all. 25 Rage, 2.0, 5cd, requires the Defensive guard: one enemy loses its next TWO turns and the card deals nothing. Pommel Strike is his only Stun and it is protected core, so a whole run's control was one core ability's one-turn rider. It is not a second Pommel Strike: that card is cheaper, ungated, deals 25% of Attack, lands 30 Break, builds Rage and Stuns for ONE turn; this buys a SECOND turn for a stance and a whole action that pays nothing. The boss rule is inherited, not re-written — stunned is one of the five ids _apply_status's carve-out refuses on an unbroken boss and this passes no force. It joins RECAST_GATED (its entire payload is a status, so a recast onto a saturated field buys nothing); Wheeling Cut deliberately does not, because a card that deals damage is never a wasted cast.

PRECISION STRIKE AND FEINT ARE UNTOUCHED — BW's original readers, and the branch-and-flip pattern is the spec's signature. His pool now makes eight decisions across twelve cards where it made four across ten.

§5 — WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT DONE. No other pool is touched: the Beastmaster's 8-of-8 companion binding, the Cryomancer's remaining 11-of-11 ice and the Pyromancer's one-currency problem are reported and unruled. The Hunter class gap is not addressed — three shallowest pools, none of DO's twenty-two, and not one of its twenty-four spec cards is a heal, a shield or mitigation. It is bigger than the Swordmaster's and it is next, not now. NO TALENT MOVES. No cell changes row or lane, so no migration is needed, and no save version moves. Pyroblast is still cooldown-zero and that ruling stays open.

§6 — VERIFICATION. check_dr.gd joins GATES (22 now) and asserts the exclusives as PROPERTIES, never as counts — DN's gate asserted two and there were five, DO's brief nine and there were twenty-two, and this batch's own brief asserted three exclusive axes when one of the three was false. It drives all three cards and Battle Poise's free pivot on a live board. FOUR NEGATIVE CONTROLS WERE RUN AND ALL FOUR BEHAVED: a summon authored onto the Sharpshooter, a third revive call site, a cooldown site removed, and the comment-stripping trap controlled from BOTH sides — the retired card's name put back as a COMMENT left the gate GREEN, the same name put back in CODE turned it RED. 2026-08-28 — Batch DQ: DO THE DRAFT POOLS ACTUALLY CONTAIN DIFFERENT BUILDS?

A REPORT. NOTHING WAS RETIRED, RE-AUTHORED OR RETUNED. All 142 draft cards compared against every card they can be offered beside, against the protected core kit of the hero who would be offered them, and against that hero's talent tree. The comparison is on what the card DOES — handler bodies lifted out of _resolve_special with comments and every cosmetic call stripped, plus the read sites of the 27 cards whose whole handler is a self-flag. The audit is docs/draft-audit.html; this entry is the summary.

§1 — TWO GRADE-2 FINDINGS, AND BOTH ARE THE STANDING RULE NOT BEING ENFORCED. classes.gd already says "NO ABILITY MAY BE A STRICTLY BETTER VERSION OF ANOTHER IN THE SAME POOL", so these are correctness rather than taste.

  • GLACIAL PRISON AND FLASH FREEZE ARE ONE CARD AT TWO PRICES. Comments and cosmetics stripped, both handlers are the same three steps in the same order: apply 3 Chilled if the target has none, note the debuff, _hold_freeze. Flash Freeze computes one extra local and uses it only inside its log string. Glacial Prison is cheaper (25 against 30), shorter on cooldown (4 against 5), faster (2.5 against 3.0), and it is in RECAST_GATED while Flash Freeze is not — so the dearer card is also the only one of the two that can be spent on an enemy already held. Batch DO created this and nothing else did: Glacial Prison was a talent grant in no pool until DO moved it into the pool Flash Freeze had held since CB.
  • BATTLE POISE'S ENTIRE PAYLOAD IS A SUBSET OF ANSWERING STEEL'S. Both are Swordmaster self-flags; both fire at the same parry site eleven lines apart; both call _tick_cooldowns with a constant of 1. Answering Steel is cheaper (20 against 25), lasts 6 turns against 4, has no stance requirement at all, and adds +20% parry chance and 15 Rage a parry. There is no board state in which Battle Poise is the better pick. The source comment calls the two "DELIBERATE STACKING PARTNERS rather than rivals", which is a good reason to hold both and not a reason for the pool to OFFER both. This one predates DO, and the source shows why it was missed: Answering Steel's own comment names Battle Poise as "the deliberate stacking partner rather than a rival". It asks whether the two stack and never asks which of the two you take, and a three-card offer only ever asks the second question.

§2 — AND THE ANSWER TO THE QUESTION THAT MATTERS IS THAT THE POOLS VARY ENORMOUSLY, IN TWO MEASURES THAT DISAGREE. Each pool was scored on distinct decisions (effect axis × target shape — how many cards a player must draft before the pool stops offering anything new) and on engine dependence (how many cards read or write the spec's own system). The two disagree on the Pyromancer and both readings are true.

  • THE SWORDMASTER IS THE MOST CONCENTRATED POOL IN THE GAME: TEN CARDS, FOUR DECISIONS. Strike one enemy, change or hold a stance, buy tempo off a parry, or execute. Four cards see all of it, and his healing, area damage, control and party buff are all in the Warrior class pool — his own spec pool holds none of the four.
  • THE BEASTMASTER IS 8 OF 8 ON ONE ENGINE. Every card requires a living companion or reads its Loyalty. Not one card in his pool does anything for the hunter himself — no mitigation, no heal, no shield, no control, no Break, no tempo.
  • THE CRYOMANCER IS 12 OF 12 ON THE ICE, and none of DO's four opened a new decision — the pool already spread Chilled, already froze, already relocated and already cashed a hold.
  • THE PYROMANCER COVERS THE MOST DECISIONS (10 of 13) AND IS THE MOST NARROWLY BOUND (12 of 13 touch Burn). Overburn's multiplier is literally _total_burn_turns(), so five of the thirteen are the same lever on the passive's own input at five prices. Ten things to decide, one currency to decide them in.
  • THE WARDEN IS THE HEALTHIEST: NINE CARDS, NINE DECISIONS — no two make the same one, the only pool in the game of which that is true. But four of the nine hang off one trigger (a block), and two of those four cancel each other by design.
  • THE CLASS POOLS ARE THE HEALTHIEST THING IN THE DRAFT AND THE AUDIT SAYS SO. The authoring rule — untied and general, feeds no passive — is honoured at 0 of 24, and three of the four cover six decisions with six cards, which no spec pool does.

§3 — THE DISTRIBUTION OF DO's TWENTY-TWO LANDED ON CLASS LINES AND NOBODY ASKED FOR IT. 2026-08-27 — Batch DP: THE MADNESS LANE COMES OFF THE DRAW

DO's OWN COST, PAID. Mind Flay and Mass Hysteria are the only appliers of Psychosis and Hysteria in the game, and DO moved both into the draft — so four Occultist Madness cells read a status he can no longer guarantee. Before DO those dependencies were TREE-INTERNAL, which the charter explicitly permits: a ruling created this without anyone making a mistake. All four are re-pointed onto RUIN, his own passive, guaranteed in every run. NO CELL CHANGES ROW OR LANE, so no save migration is needed and Profile is still v2.

§0 — THE BRIEF SAID "THE OTHER EIGHT" AND THERE ARE SIX, WHICH IS WHY EVERY BRIEF IS TOLD TO DERIVE. check_do §4 printed twelve node/status pairs across eight nodes, and the Occultist's four cells account for six of the twelve, not four — oc_delirium and oc_permanent each read TWO un-guaranteed statuses. That leaves six pre-existing pairs, which is what docs/state.md said and what the brief did not. The sweep is 12 → 6 now.

§1 — AND FOUR OF THE SIX SURVIVORS ARE INSTRUMENT ARTEFACTS RATHER THAN BETS. The sweep matches a rendered WORD, so it cannot tell a node READING a status from a node APPLYING one: sv_virulence ("Your Poison applications … apply Exposed for 3 turns") and ss_exposed_nerve ("Critical hits apply Exposed … and you deal +15% damage to Exposed enemies") are each their OWN source. Nor can it tell an enemy's debuff landing on the HERO from a hero's landing on an enemy: ss_no_cover is an IMMUNITY ("Blind and Dazed do not affect you"). Only sm_guarded is a real bet, and it is a bonus clause gated on sm_punish — a TREE-INTERNAL condition the charter permits — sitting beside an unconditional "vs Broken" base clause, so the node cannot go dead. Ruled on: none, as the brief instructed. Each of the six is carried in check_dp.KNOWN_PAIRS with the reason it is tolerated.

§2 — THE FOUR, AND THEY READ FOUR DIFFERENT THINGS ON PURPOSE. Four cells all reading "stacks of Ruin" would flatten a lane authored as a theme into one idea repeated four times, so each reads a different quantity — and none of the four reads a DETONATION, because Grim Focus (Ruin 5), Unraveling (Ruin 7) and Avatar of Ruin (Ruin 9) already own that event. check_dp §2 asserts the four are distinct.

  • Spread of Madness (row 1) — reads an APPLICATION, probabilistically. Was: Psychosis leaps to a fellow at 60% a turn. Is: "Each mark of Ruin the Occultist lands has a 60% chance to leap to another enemy, which catches 2 Ruin." It fires on a mark LANDING where Unraveling fires on a DETONATION, so the lane's two propagation nodes never read the same event.
  • Whispers (row 2) — reads an APPLICATION MAGNITUDE. Was: +45 percentage points on Psychosis's 50% seize chance. Is: "Every debuff the Occultist applies marks 4 Ruin instead of the base 2." This is the lever AY §8 identified as the real constraint — generation, not the threshold — and the field keeps its _step name because it keeps its SHAPE: an increase on a base the kit already pays. Only the base moved, from Psychosis's 50% to OLD_GODS_MARK.
  • Delirium (row 5) — reads an EVENT, and NOT ONE LINE OF CODE CHANGED. Its text named Psychotic and Hysterical; its read site names no status and never has (not attacker.is_hero and not strike_target.is_hero). The text now says what the code does — and says it in oc_cackling's words, one row down, because the same trigger deserves the same sentence. It was NOT narrowed to "Bewitched": that would UNDER-state the payload the moment Mind Flay is drafted, which is DM's seventh family, and an absent clause does not mis-say so no test catches it.
  • Ruined Mind (row 8, was Permanent Delusion) — reads the STACK COUNT, as a threshold. Was: Psychosis, Bewitchment and Hysteria never expire — two thirds of it a bet. Is: "A boss bearing 10 or more Ruin can no longer resist the Occultist's Bewitchment." This is the row-8 shape exactly: it REMOVES THE CONSTRAINT THE LANE HAS WORKED AROUND ALL GAME, stated in the lane's own header. It is self-enabling by construction and nothing had to be added to make that true — the boss refuses the charm inside _apply_status, but the bewitch handler marks Ruin on the very next line regardless, so a REFUSED cast still deepens the mark that eventually opens the gate.

§4 — THE WINDOW TIGHTENS, BUT THE SEQUENCE DOES NOT GET HARDER

THE MODEL IS WRITTEN DOWN BECAUSE NOTHING IN THE PROJECT CAN MEASURE IT. The numbers therefore come from a stated model rather than from play data: difficulty is time inside the window (CN's standing rule read as an equation), the player's timing error is Gaussian with an SD of about 60 ms, a press's miss-risk is that Gaussian's tail outside the Good window, and the sequence's risk is the sum of its presses'. §0 — THE SCOPE LIMIT, WHICH MATTERS MORE THAN THE RULE

Refusal is only correct when the status application is the ability’s ENTIRE payload. An ability that also deals damage, heals, or converts a resource must still cast when its buff would not improve: that half is worth the turn on its own, and refusing it would be a worse bug than the one being fixed.

The set was derived by walking _resolve_special’s call graph, the way Batch CN derived its skill-check tables — not by reading the damage and pressure fields, which are zero on Feint, Guard Change and Kill Command and every other card that hits hard from inside a handler. 211 abilities → 134 with no field-level payload → 58 whose whole payload is the status.

§1 — THE REFUSAL

It lives at _ability_usable, the door the stance gates use, so the greyed button, the bot’s available pool and the cast itself read one decision — the rule Batch CI set for Formless. It is the last gate in that function on purpose: every gate above answers “can this be cast at all”, and asking the cheap question first keeps the walk off the hot path.

The test is exact and computed at cast time. A cast is refused only when every unit it could touch already holds everything it would hand out, at no less duration and no less power. The powers are recomputed live — Vespers off the Cleric’s current maximum health, Magic Barrier off the Mage’s, Divine Shield off the Devout’s — because refusing a cast that would in fact have improved something is strictly worse than the bug being fixed. Two further exactnesses the arithmetic needed: Fleeting is normalised (the target’s own mod_status_turns is applied before the comparison, as add_status applies it), and a negative turn count reads as permanence rather than as a duration, so a battle-long status can never be “lengthened”.

Picked-target cards are judged against the whole pool the picker offers, so a button never darkens while a second enemy still stands clean.
