§0 — COVERAGE, STATED HONESTLY

CJ's audit reported 120 of 120 stat cells clean and was later found to have had no check for stated bonuses with no code path at all. An audit that overstates its own reach is worse than a narrow one that admits its edges, so this section is deliberately specific about what was NOT done.

What every one of the 324 got

  • Extraction from the live trees, not a hand-parse. A throwaway Godot script walked Talents.LANE_TREES and dumped all 324 with their rendered text (through Talents.desc_for, the same function the tooltip calls), payload, scale, granted_name and collision_kind. 12 specs × 27 cells = 324, confirmed.
  • A read site, opened. Every distinct stat field a payload writes (319 of them) was resolved to at least one line of shipped code that is not a comment and not the unit.gd declaration. Comment-only mentions were rejected as read sites — a field whose only appearance outside talents.gd is prose would be dead, and none are.
  • A magnitude comparison. Every number in every node's rendered text was compared against the numbers present in the payload and within ±8 non-comment lines of every read site, with unit conversions (0.12↔12, 1.15↔15, 0.60↔40) applied both ways.
  • The three targeted sweeps in the table above: base-quoting, Perfect-claiming, party-scope.

What only 68 of the 324 got — AND THIS IS THE GAP

Line-by-line semantic reading — the pass that catches caps, silent exclusions, conditions, interactions and "whose percentage is it" — was done node-by-node for berserker (27), swordmaster (27) and warden (14 of 27). That is 68 of 324. Every bucket-2 finding below except the scope sweep came out of those 68, which is the honest reason to expect more in the other nine specs: the yield was roughly one INCOMPLETE per ten nodes read, and 256 nodes have not been read that way.

The Mystic is the thinnest spot in this document. It produced zero hits in every automated pass, so not one of its 27 nodes was hand-opened. Zero flags is real evidence about magnitudes and nothing at all about caps or scope. Cryomancer, arcanist, holy, inquisitor, sharpshooter, beastmaster, occultist and pyromancer each had only their flagged nodes opened (between 1 and 7 apiece).

Two things nothing here checked at all: whether a node's magnitude is well balanced (out of scope — that is the work this audit exists to make possible), and whether the tooltip surfaces that re-render node text agree with it. One such disagreement was found by accident (§4, the Blood Frenzy floor) and there is no reason to think it is alone.

1.2 — dv_stalwart "Stalwart" — BOTH numbers wrong

inquisitor / Bulwark / row 5. Text: "Divine Shield absorbs 50% of the Devout's max health (up from the base 30%)." Payload: {"stalwart_step": 20}.

Two read sites agree with each other and disagree with the text: battle.gd:5431 (the intent preview) and battle.gd:14502 (the cast) both compute 0.35 + 0.01 * stalwart_step. At stalwart_step = 20 that is 0.55 — the shield absorbs 55%, not 50% — and the base it is added to is 35%, not 30%.

The cause is on the record. CQ's fold audit row for Divine Shield (changelog.html:906) reads base "absorbs 30%", perfect "absorbs 35%"; CN took the bar off, so the perfect's number became the base, and CQ's own summary states it plainly at changelog.html:1450: "Divine Shield absorbs 35% instead of 30%". Divine Shield was not among CR's three duration reverts. The ability moved and the talent quoting it did not.

The read site's own comment is a third answer. battle.gd:14498 still says "Absorbs 30% (perfect 35%)" directly above a line that does 35% unconditionally with no perfect gate at all. Three statements of one number, no two alike.

1.3 — wd_shieldwall "Shield Mastery" — the duration is 5, the text says 4

warden / Plate / row 5. Text: "Shieldwall's stance holds 2 turns longer — 4 turns, or 5 on a perfect cast." Payload: {"shield_mastery_ranks": 1}.

Both read sites compute 3 + 2 * shield_mastery_ranks — battle.gd:5358 (intent preview) and battle.gd:17719 (the cast). Base Shieldwall is 3; with the node it is 5. "2 turns longer" is right and "4 turns" is wrong, and the perfect clause on top of it cannot fire (§1.1). Both halves of one sentence are wrong for two unrelated reasons.

1.4 — py_rebirth "Phoenix Rebirth" — 25% stated, 15% taken

pyromancer / Inferno / row 9 (capstone). Text: "sacrifice 25% of current health and your Mana returns to full." battle.gd:14532: var sacrifice := int(attacker.hp * 0.15). The cost is 15% of current health. The Mana half is correct (resource = max_resource). Nothing else scales it — this is the whole of the cost.

1.5 — cr_rime "Rime" — the node contradicts the ability it installs

cryomancer / Winter / row 4. The node text says the chilling spread lasts "for 3 turns". The new_ability description in the very same payload says "For 4 turns". The handler applies {"id": "rime", "turns": 4 + icy_resolve_ranks}. The code and the ability description agree on 4; only the node text says 3. This one needs no convention ruling to be wrong — the node disagrees with a string it ships itself. (The Frostbite half — "-50% healing received, 2 turns" — is correct: turns: 2, and unit.gd:3109 halves the heal.)

1.6 — dv_resolve "Sacred Resolve" — both durations off by one

inquisitor / Zeal / row 3. Text: "split evenly among living heroes for 3 turns" and "Already owned: the split lasts 5 turns instead". battle.gd:14620: var res_turns := 4 + attacker.resolve_extra_turns, and the node's upgrade sets resolve_extra_turns: 2. Base 4, upgraded 6. Stated 3 and 5.

Read §2 before ruling on this one: under the acting-turn convention 4 is "3 turns", and this node would be right. It is filed here rather than in §2 because dv_bulwark, in the same tree, states its duration the other way — 3 applied and "for 3 turns" stated, 4 applied and "4 turns instead" stated, both correct under the calendar convention. The two nodes cannot both be right.

1.7 — sm_high_guard "High Guard" — 2 stated, 3 applied

swordmaster / Poise / row 5. Text: "Take 40% less damage for 2 turns after parrying an attack." The 40% is correct (battle.gd:9123, raw *= 0.60). The duration is _apply_status(strike_target, "high_guard", 3) at battle.gd:8058.

Three surfaces, three different durations: the read site's own comment (battle.gd:8056) says "hardens the stance for a turn", the node says 2, the value is 3, and STATUS_INFO (battle.gd:213) states no duration at all. sm_swordsmanship — also defensive, also the Poise lane — applies 2 and says "2 turns", which is what makes High Guard's 3 the outlier rather than the rule.

§2 — THE CROSS-CUTTING DESIGN QUESTION: WHAT DOES "N TURNS" MEAN?

This is one ruling that settles a dozen nodes, so it is set apart from bucket 1 rather than filed as a dozen typos.

tick_statuses() runs at the start of a unit's turn, before it acts (unit.gd:2171, called at battle.gd:2844). So a status applied with value N during a unit's own turn is present on the calendar for N turns (the cast turn plus N-1) and gives that unit N-1 acting turns. Both readings are defensible. §3 — NO READ SITE, AND UNREACHABLE

NO READ SITE — none at field level, and that is a real result. All 319 distinct stat fields the 324 payloads write have at least one live, non-comment read site. Comment-only mentions were explicitly rejected, so this is not the weaker claim that the name appears somewhere. The four dead clauses in §1.1 are the functional equivalent and are filed there because they are dead for a reason the designer needs to see.

UNREACHABLE — no node is ungrantable. All 324 were enumerated from the trees, so none is orphaned by a rename or absent from a tree; all 12 specs are reachable; every payload condition (has_node, owns_ability) names a node or ability that exists.

The reverse case does exist, and is reported because it is the shape a later batch will misread. Four fields have live read sites in battle.gd and are written by no node at all — seeding_ranks, emp_frostbolt_ranks, frost_ward_ranks, freezing_ranks. Each is commented "no node:" in unit.gd, so this is known and deliberate; the nodes that once wrote them (py_seeding, cr_emp_frostbolt, cr_frost_ward, cr_freezing) were re-specced onto different fields and their texts are correct against the NEW fields. Dead code, not a dead node — listed so the next reader does not trace a node to the wrong site.

4.2 — Caps and exclusions the text does not mention

  • sm_momentum_sm "Overwhelm" (swordmaster / Blade / 6) — "+8% damage for every debuff on the target". _status_count (battle.gd:19293) walks DEBUFF_IDS but skips "broken". Broken is the one affliction the whole Breaker lane exists to produce, and it is the one that does not count.
  • sm_dominant "Dominant Presence" (swordmaster / Breaker / 3) — "+15% armor for every debuff applied" is correct and relative (unit.gd:2270), but effective_armor() ends return minf(a, 0.85) (unit.gd:2304). The growth is unbounded and multiplicative, so the ceiling is reached in a long fight. Endurance, one line below it in the same function, documents its own +75% cap — so the omission is inconsistent within a single screen of code.
  • wd_tank_spank "Tank and Spank" (warden / Banner / 1) — "Empowers a random ally". battle.gd:10023 picks from heroes.filter(not dead), which includes the Warden himself and includes companions. It is the one node where a companion IS eligible, and it is the one that says "ally" meaning "anyone".

4.3 — Interactions stated on one node and not its partner

  • dv_apostle (inquisitor / Faith / 9) says Faith stacks are "worth double". dv_fervor (Faith / 6) says the same, and spells out "With Apostle the stacks count TRIPLE: the two nodes ADD to the base rather than multiplying each other" — which _faith_stack_mult (battle.gd:13846) confirms. Both are ownable. The interaction is documented on the row-6 node and not on the capstone, so a player who takes the capstone first is told "double" and gets triple.
  • ar_wrath says it doubles the curve's step, "1.5% to 3%". True alone; with ar_conduit (which raises the step to 2%) the result is 3.5%, and neither text mentions the other.
  • ar_harmonics — "Arcane Explosion builds 2 Resonance instead of 1" is the non-crit case; battle.gd:11029 shows a crit builds 2 (or more) before the node's +1. Accurate as far as it goes.

§5 — BUCKET 3: PROSE ONLY

Thin, and that is a finding in itself. CJ's audit found the ability corpus was "a prose job, not a repair job"; the talent corpus is the reverse. Node text is terse, states its number, and mostly carries no rationale — Batch AJ's re-author left the padding in the code comments rather than the descriptions. The systematic prose issue is §2's duration wording, which is a convention question and is filed there.

The one recurring stylistic note across the 68 nodes read closely: several state a percentage without saying whose it is — wd_stomp_drill's "restores 20% more resource" is +20 percentage points (10%→30%, battle.gd:10550), not 20% more of 10%. It is correct; it is ambiguous. Text standard §4.5's shape, applied to percentages rather than turns, would settle it.

§6 — WHAT WAS VERIFIED CLEAN, SO THE RULINGS ARE NOT APPLIED TOO WIDELY

  • All 27 berserker nodes. Including the three cross-row conditions, which resolve exactly as the Batch AJ header claims: Crushing Blows' step 20→15 with Savagery (battle.gd:7109), Scar Tissue's floor 85→100 with Unstoppable (unit.gd:2400), and Measured Rage zeroing the damage-taken term outright rather than summing −0.20 and +0.15 (battle.gd:9142).
