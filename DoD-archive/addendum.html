Dawn of Decay — Implementation Addendum

Records every design change made during prototyping (July 2026). Where this document conflicts with the original design docs, this document wins. Organized by the source document each change updates.

Updates to "Dawn of Decay.docx" & "DoD Narrative_Zones.docx" (Structure)

  • DESIGN PIVOT — no explorable overworld. Runs now use a Slay-the-Spire-style node map: the player picks a reachable node on a branching 8-floor path and combat begins immediately. Node types: Fight, Elite, Rest (+30% HP & Mana), Loot (+1 item), Boss (top of map).
  • Zones survive as flavor layers: each map region carries a zone's enemy roster, battle background, and (future) environmental modifiers. Forest of Old is the first zone; its boss slot is currently a stand-in "Withered Warden".
  • Roguelike layer lives between fights: rewards after victory (Rest +25% HP/Mana or Scavenge +1 item), talents/specs are changed from the map's Party screen.

Updates to "DoD Run.docx" (Run Flow)

  • HP and Mana carry over between battles. No automatic refill. Rage still starts at 0 each battle. Faith and Arcane Resonance are battle-scoped.
  • Fallen heroes return at 20% HP after a won battle. Full party wipe ends the run.
  • Shared run inventory: Health Potion (40 HP, targeted), Mana Potion (40 Mana/Rage, targeted), Bomb (50 true damage to all enemies), Revive Potion (fallen ally at 50%), Defense Potion (party +10% armor, 5 turns). Starting stock: 2/1/1/1/1. Loot drops favor potions.

Updates to "DoD Combat.docx" (Combat System)

  • Attack rolls: 5% miss (no effect), 5% parry (defender negates and counters with a free basic attack; Broken units cannot parry; heals cannot miss/be parried), 10% crit (1.5x damage, +25% chance vs Broken). Base rates; stats/talents will modify.
  • Guard action (free, every hero): -40% damage and -50% Pressure taken until the unit's next turn; 1.5 initiative delay; Perfect check sheds 10 Pressure. For the Mage, Guard also vents all Resonance.
  • Items in combat: one item per character per turn, does not consume the turn (dropdown menu next to abilities).
  • Skill checks: timing bar with Perfect / Good / Sloppy zones; every ability carries a unique Perfect bonus. Perfect grants +15% effect and the bonus property.
  • Statuses implemented: Broken, Slow (-25% speed), Burn (6/turn), Bleed (5/turn), Sunder (-35% armor), Ward (-50% Pressure taken), Fortify (+10% armor), Barrier (damage absorb), Focus (+10 Mana/turn), Renewal (+8 HP/turn), Surge (+20% attack), Guard. Status chips show under units with tooltips.
  • Tuned baseline (200-battle simulations): ~85% win rate, 7-9 rounds per fight, damage share Warrior ~42% / Mage ~40% / Cleric ~19%, ~2.5 Breaks per side per fight.

Updates to "DoD Classes.docx" (Classes)

Warrior

  • Rallying Shout (core): free, grants the Warrior 15 Rage, party sheds 15 Pressure, allies regain 20% of their max resource (25 Pressure / 30% on Perfect).
  • Crushing Blow applies Sunder (-35% armor, 3 turns) baseline.

Mage — reworked

  • Arcane Resonance: +15% damage dealt and +3% crit per stack, but +10% damage taken per stack. Stacks build on damaging casts (2 on spell crit), never decay, and are only removed by Guard (vents all stacks). Backlash Ward: +15 Mana on reaching max stacks.
  • Magic Bolt: 25 damage.
  • Arcane Barrier removed from the core kit. Replaced by Arcane Cannon: 30 base damage; recoils on the Mage for 5% of damage dealt +5% per Resonance stack; Perfect skill check = no recoil.

Cleric

  • Core kit per doc: Smite, Mend Wounds, Blessed Purge, Renewal. Faith builds +10 per action; Miracles (Faith spenders) arrive with specializations.

Updates to "DoD UI.docx" (Interface)

  • Map screen: gothic zone title, branching node buttons, party HP/MP readout, Party tab (full party screen: per-hero stats, abilities, specializations, back navigation) and Inventory dropdown.
  • Combat UI: initiative bar with cropped portraits (next 10 turns), Pressure bar shifts purple to pink near Break, numeric HP/resource bars, scrolling battle history log (all actions, damage, statuses, Breaks, deaths), item dropdown, Guard button.
  • Feel: attack lunges, hit flash + knockback, crit numbers explode on screen with shake, Break = hitstop + purple fracture flash + camera punch (per UI doc), 12 placeholder retro SFX.

Change Log

2026-07-04 — Specializations v1 and combat revisions

  • Specializations implemented (selectable and switchable on the Party screen between fights). Each grants a passive plus abilities: Warrior — Berserker (Blood Frenzy; Bloodlust, Wildstrikes), Warden (Bulwark; Shieldwall, Retaliation), Swordmaster (Duelist; Overpower, Momentum). Mage — Pyromancer (Ignite; Flame Surge, Phoenix Rebirth), Cryomancer (Chill; Razor Ice, Blizzard), Arcanist (Echo; Arcane Cannon, Arcane Rift, Reality Fracture). Cleric — Holy (Grace; Dawnbreak, Hymn of Hope), Inquisitor (Zeal; Burning Verdict, Condemn), Occultist (Corrupted Channeling; Hex of Ruin, Dark Benediction). Cleric Miracles are the first Faith spenders (40-60 Faith).
  • Taunt is Warrior core (all specs): free, forces all enemies to attack the Warrior for 5 turns. Warden's second ability is now Retaliation (counter stance, 3 turns).
  • Arcane Cannon is Arcanist-exclusive; removed from the Mage core kit. Focus removed entirely. Mage core = Magic Bolt + Arcane Surge.
  • Razor Ice (Cryomancer) replaces Frost Spike: 15 damage to 2 random enemies; always crits against Slowed targets.
  • Hero Pressure generation reduced ~20-30% across all kits; enemy Breaks now ~1.5 per battle (were ~2.5) so Breaking feels earned.
  • Combat UI: action bar is now Basic Attack | Abilities dropdown | Guard | Items dropdown; free abilities no longer labeled "(Free)"; target selection has a Cancel button to re-pick the ability before casting.
  • New systems supporting specs: AoE attacks, random-multi-hit attacks, Faith costs, armor pierce, missing-HP drain, Taunt targeting, Echo procs, statuses Shieldwall / Empower / Exposed / Cripple / Retaliation.

2026-07-04 (later) — UI flow and Cleric revision

  • Action bar moved to bottom-center of the screen.
  • Cancel is now universal: self-cast, AoE, and auto-targeted abilities get a Cast/Cancel confirm step; targeted abilities keep the Cancel button during target selection.
  • Hovering a portrait on the initiative bar highlights that unit on the battlefield (plus name tooltip).
  • Status effect durations standardized to 3 turns (Slow, Burn, Bleed, Ward, Fortify, Empower, Cripple, Defense Potion). Until-next-turn effects (Guard, Shieldwall), 5-turn Taunt, and 5-turn Renewal unchanged.
  • Cleric: Blessed Purge replaced by Divine Shield — grants an ally a shield absorbing 100 damage (130 on Perfect) that persists until it breaks.
  • Rallying Shout removed from the Warrior kit for now.

2026-07-04 (third pass) — Cast flow and kit trims

  • Confirm prompt removed. Targeted abilities cancel during target selection; auto-cast abilities (self/AoE/random) cancel via a button next to the skill check bar. Cancelling refunds nothing because nothing was spent yet.
  • Action bar now lives in a self-centering container at the bottom center (fixed the drift-to-left bug).
  • Razor Ice shards land 0.45s apart so each hit reads distinctly (applies to all multi-hit attacks).
  • Heavy Strike removed for now. Crushing Blow moved to Swordmaster, replacing Momentum. Warrior core kit: Strike + Taunt.
  • Party screen: hover tooltips on stats (definitions of Armor/Speed/Stability/resources), each ability card, spec cards (passive + granted ability details), and hero list entries.
  • Simulator bot now runs default specializations (Swordmaster/Pyromancer/Holy); baseline 92% wins, 6.4 rounds, 1.7 enemy Breaks per battle.

2026-07-04 (fourth pass) — Talent trees v1

  • Talent trees: every specialization now has a 6-talent tree (2 talents per tier; tier 2 unlocks at 2 picks, tier 3 at 4). Talents are stat passives, ability alterations, or entirely new abilities (Rampage, Bulwark Slam, Flurry, Inferno, Absolute Zero, Singularity, Sanctuary, Excommunicate, Soul Leech).
  • Talent points: every hero earns points from each combat node defeated — 1 per fight, 2 per elite, 3 per boss. Spent from the Party screen (Talents button on each spec card). Talents apply while that spec is active.
  • Boss "choice of three class modifiers" is deferred until runs extend past one zone (the run currently ends at the boss, so the choice would never matter). The 3 boss points are live.
  • Specialization passives now appear as a permanent gold ★ buff chip on the character with the passive's description.
  • Simultaneous AoE abilities (Condemn, Wildstrikes, Blizzard, Flame Surge, Inferno) hit all targets at once again; only sequential multi-hit attacks (Razor Ice, Flurry) pace their hits.

2026-07-04 (fifth pass) — Draft, permanent specs, damage types

  • Hero draft: runs open on a draft screen — choose three heroes from the roster (duplicates allowed). New Run always returns here.
  • Permanent specialization: heroes start unawakened with core kits. After the first combat victory (which also grants the first talent point), each hero must choose a specialization on a dedicated awakening screen — the choice is locked for the rest of the run.
  • Party screen consolidated: selecting a hero now shows one page — stats, class explanation, compact ability chips (hover for details), and the chosen spec's full talent tree. Separate spec cards/tree screens removed.
  • Talent trees grown to 8 talents each (T1 x2 / T2 x3 / T3 x3), including a new parry-chance stat (Swordmaster's Riposte Training).
  • Damage types introduced: arcane, nature, shadow, holy, physical, fire, frost. Every damaging ability is typed (e.g. Magic Bolt/Cannon arcane, Flame Surge/Inferno fire, Razor Ice/Blizzard frost, Smite/Verdict/Condemn holy, Hex/Soul Leech shadow, all weapon attacks physical). Units carry per-type resistances (negative = vulnerability): orcs resist physical slightly; the Withered Warden resists nature 50%, physical 10%, and is vulnerable to fire (-25%). Tooltips show the type; the battle log notes resisted/vulnerable hits.
  • Enemy damage +20% across all orc kits. Post-change baseline: 85% wins, ~0.9 hero deaths per battle.
  • Arcane Rift removed (Arcanist: Cannon, Reality Fracture, + Singularity talent).

2026-07-04 (sixth pass) — WoW-style talents, flow polish, stacking bleeds

  • Talent trees rebuilt WoW-style: nodes have ranks (multi-point stat/numeric talents, e.g. Thick Blood 3 ranks of +10 HP) and branching prerequisites ("Requires: Deep Wounds"); ability-granting talents stay single-rank. Tier N unlocks at (N-1)*3 points spent in the tree.
  • Flow: spec selection now happens immediately after the hero draft (before the map); after every battle victory the game routes through the Party screen before the node map; unspent talent points glow gold on the map's Party tab, the party list, and hero rows.
  • Bleed stacks additively — each application adds another 5-damage wound (chip shows Bl2, Bl3...). Applies to enemies and heroes alike; sim win rate dropped from 85% to ~70% as a result (enemy Jagged Cuts stack now).
  • Berserker's Blood Frenzy chip live-updates with the current bonus (e.g. "+24%").
  • Razor Ice re-targets each shard at launch — no more strikes on corpses or stalled sequences.
  • Node map shows the Forest zone art (from the new "imported files" workflow) behind a readability dim.
  • Distinct hero models require new sprite sheets — currently only Soldier and Orc exist. Drop new sheets in "imported files" using the Soldier format (100x100 frame rows: Name_Idle/Walk/Attack01-03/Hurt/Death PNGs).

2026-07-04 (seventh pass) — Bleed buildup, gold economy, shops, RELICS

  • Bleed reworked as a buildup meter: wounding attacks add Bleed (chip shows Bl35, Bl70...); at 100 the target BLEEDS OUT for 20% of max HP (true damage) and the meter resets. No more per-turn bleed ticks. Wildstrikes builds 35 per target; Berserker's Deep Wounds talent adds +10 per rank.
  • Orc raiders lose Jagged Cut (bleed) and gain Sundering Strike (60% chance to Sunder armor, 2 turns).
  • Gold: victories pay 25-35 (fight), 55-70 (elite), 110-130 (boss); runs start with 60. Shown on the map readout and victory panel.
  • Shop nodes ("The Wandering Peddler") appear on the map (~10% of nodes, at least one guaranteed per run). Sells consumables (potions 30g, bombs 45g, revives 80g) and runes — run-long class modifiers offered per party member (e.g. Rune of Fury: Strike +5 dmg; Rune of Deep Wells: +20 max Mana), one copy each.
  • RELICS — first meta-progression layer. Killing the zone boss unlocks one random relic, permanent across runs (saved to disk). Pool of six: Waystone Shard (+1 starting talent point per hero), Dragonbone Idol (+10% all damage), Chalice of Dawn (party heals 10% after victories), Gravewrought Coin (+80 starting gold), Eidolon Mirror (Warriors start battles with 25 Rage), Emberheart (fire/holy +20%). All unlocked relics are always active; per-character relic slots from the design doc come later.

2026-07-04 (eighth pass) — The Scarlands, rune economy, tuning

  • Second zone: The Scarlands. Clearing the Forest boss now continues the run into a fresh Scarlands map (own node background) instead of ending it. Enemies are reused orcs scaled +35% HP/damage with an ash-red tint; the zone boss is the Ash-Wrought Tyrant (resists fire 50%, vulnerable to frost). Every zone boss still unlocks a relic; the run ends after the final zone.
  • Runes reworked as generated loot: generic stat runes rolled per shop visit with rarity (Cracked/Polished/Radiant at 60/30/10%, power x1/x2/x3, priced 50/100/160g), offered only for classes in the current party. Bought runes go to the hero's rune pouch and are equipped on the Party tab (2 slots per hero); they are lost when the run ends. Full loot table to come.
  • Rest-or-Scavenge choice removed after battles — victories pay gold/points and continue; healing lives in Rest nodes, potions, and the Cleric.
  • Heroes now stand in the same staggered triangle as enemies (position still cosmetic).
  • Arcane Cannon Perfect reworked: recoil always applies; Perfect instead grants +7.5% damage per Resonance stack on that blast.
  • Cleric speed reduced 15% (100 to 85).

2026-07-05 — Shuffled talent trees, honest character sheet, Mocking Blow

  • Talent trees now shuffle every run. Talents are classified tier 1-6 by depth (deeper = stronger; capstone abilities sit at tier 5). Each spec has a tiered pool; when a spec is chosen at run start, that hero's tree is generated — 2 slots per tier drawn from the pool, padded with tier-scaled generic Training talents until more signature talents are authored. Tier N unlocks at (N-1)*2 points spent. Branch prerequisites were dropped (they fight the shuffle); pool sizes and slot counts are placeholders pending the final tree size.
  • Character screen now tells the truth: stats, ability chips, and tooltips are computed from the hero's effective build (talents + equipped runes applied), not base class values. Crit Chance (base 10%) and Parry (base 5%) lines added.
  • Taunt removed; Mocking Blow added (Warrior core): an attack (15 physical, 8 Pressure) that taunts only its victim for 3 turns (4 on Perfect) — the mocked enemy must attack the Warrior. Party-wide 5-turn taunt is gone; tank-and-heal no longer trivializes fights.
  • Sim baseline after changes: 77% wins over 60 battles.

2026-07-05 (second pass) — The Hunter, 4-hero parties, Orc Archers

  • Hunter class (Focus resource: builds on shots, spent on payoffs). Core: Quick Shot, Pinning Arrow (Slow), Hunter's Mark (party +15% crit vs the mark, Marked status), Camouflage (+15% harder to hit, next attack +20%). Specs: Beastmaster (Pack Bond crit-followups; Twin Fang, Bear Maul), Sharpshooter (Lethal Aim x1.75 crits; Aimed Shot auto-crits Marked targets, Piercing Arrow), Mystic Ranger (Wrath of the Wild slows; Thorn Volley and Sylvan Binding — first NATURE damage, resisted by the forest boss).
  • Parties are now four heroes (draft has 4 slots; two-damage/healer/tank compositions possible). Battle layouts, party/spec/shop screens updated; enemy compositions scaled up (3 enemies standard, 4-packs in the Scarlands) and orc damage raised another ~15%. Sim bot still wins ~100% with 0.7 deaths/battle — real-run difficulty needs playtest judgment.
  • Orc Archer: red-tinted orc, fast and fragile, Arrow Shot + Poison Arrow (70% chance of Poison, a 6/turn DoT — new status).
  • Burn ticks can now crit (10% chance, x1.5, shown with an !).
  • Fixes: spec passive stat bonuses (Bulwark, Duelist) now show on the character sheet via a shared passive applicator; initiative-bar hover highlight was invisible on white-tinted units — now a scale pop + gold marker over the hovered unit.

2026-07-05 (third pass) — Focus economy, bigger fights, UI declutter

  • Focus reworked: Hunters start every battle at 100 Focus; abilities spend it and +20 returns at the start of each of their turns. Focus classes come online immediately but have a fixed ceiling — distinct from Rage (build by fighting), Mana (carried pool), Faith (build by acting), Resonance (stacking risk). Quick Shot no longer generates Focus.
  • Enemy packs up to 5 (new battle layout): standard fights 3-4 enemies, Scarlands fights up to 5; elites/bosses field 5 in zone 2.
  • Pinning Arrow removed (Hunter core: Quick Shot, Hunter's Mark, Camouflage). Piercing Arrow deals +50% damage to Broken enemies.
  • Divine Shield moved from the Cleric core to the Inquisitor spec (core: Smite, Mend Wounds, Renewal). Hymn of Hope costs 40 Faith (Resonant Hymn talent lowers it to 30).
  • Initiative-bar hover now grows the unit 30% and draws a white shader outline.
  • HP/resource bars shrunk (72px wide, tighter stack) and pulled closer to the sprite — the bottom hero's info no longer clips off-screen.
  • Removed turn/targeting announcer text ("X's turn", "Choose a target"); the message bar now only speaks for events (Breaks, deaths, miracles).
  • Sim baseline: 90% wins, 1.3 hero deaths/battle vs the new 4-enemy default.

  • Initiative preview: abilities have always had individual initiative costs (delay 2.0-6.0); now selecting an ability instantly re-sorts the initiative bar with a gold ghost portrait showing where your next turn will land — visible through targeting and the skill check, reverting on cancel. Every ability tooltip lists its Initiative cost.

  • Burn crits now use the caster's crit chance (snapshotted onto the status when applied) instead of a flat 10%.

2026-07-06 (third pass) — Archetype outline, Guard removed, Sharpshooter rework, battle QoL

  • Class archetype outline (design north star; shown on the spec choice screen). Damage: Ramp (start weak, snowball), Rush (start strong, resources dwindle), Nuker (bank resources for one devastating turn, exploit weakened foes), Pressure (steady damage, debuffs/DoTs, Break pressure). Support: Healer (restore HP/mana, revive), Warder (mitigation, buffs). Tank: Tank (absorb/redirect, buffs), Bruiser (damage/tank hybrid, debuffs). Assignments — Mage: Pyromancer/Cryomancer Nuker, Arcanist Ramp. Cleric: Holy Healer, Inquisitor Warder, Occultist Pressure. Warrior: Berserker Ramp, Warden Tank, Swordmaster Bruiser. Hunter: Beastmaster/Sharpshooter Rush, Survivalist Pressure (renamed from Mystic Ranger).
  • Guard removed entirely — no universal defend action, no Guard status. Consequence: the Mage no longer has a Resonance vent; stacks now persist all battle.
  • Hunter's Mark and Camouflage removed (with the Marked/Camouflage statuses and their crit/miss hooks). Aimed Shot no longer auto-crits Marked targets.
  • Powershot (new, Sharpshooter): 25 Focus, initiative 3.5, base 20 damage +2% per 1% of the target's Break (Pressure) bar — up to triple damage at the Break threshold. Perfect: +10 Focus.
  • Quick Draw (new, Sharpshooter): 15 Focus, initiative 2.0 — all the Hunter's abilities act 50% faster (initiative delays shrink) for 5 turns; Perfect: 6 turns.
  • Warrior Rage economy buffed: +5 Rage at the start of each of his turns, and Strike now builds 20 Rage (was 15). Unchanged: +10 when hit, Perfect Strike +10.
  • Per-ability initiative preview: the Abilities dropdown is now a real button list — hovering each individual ability re-sorts the initiative bar with the gold ghost (the old menu only previewed from keyboard focus).
  • Announcer text removed: no more banner messages at the top of the battle; the combat log and floating numbers carry the information.
  • Initiative bar shrunk (portraits 66x90 to 48x66) so it no longer runs under the combat log.
  • Battle burger menu (☰): Restart Run, Settings (in-battle overlay — volume/fullscreen without losing the fight), Exit to Main Menu (run resumes from the last saved node).
  • Skill checks accept left click as well as Spacebar.

2026-07-06 (fourth pass) — Master document, node rules, warrior/hunter kits, fixes

  • Master Design Document created (docs/master.html → "DoD Master Document.docx"): the full authoritative reference — concept, run flow, node rules, complete combat math, every class / spec / ability / talent / relic / rune / enemy with numbers, UI and controls, saves and tooling. It supersedes the original concept docs; the addendum remains the changelog.
  • Node map rules formalized: every zone = 10 tiers × 3 nodes (30 interconnected nodes; the player interacts with exactly 10 before the boss) + a boss tier. Fixed composition per zone: 18 fights (60%), 6 rest (20%), 6 loot (20%); fights on tiers 6+ have a 25% chance to be elite; tier 1 always contains a fight. Consequence: shop nodes no longer spawn — the Peddler is unreachable until a design decision (flagged in master doc §10).
  • Spec choice screen: archetype now shows as just its name in bold under the spec title; the explanation moved to a mouseover tooltip.
  • Skill check left-click fixed: clicks were being swallowed by UI panels (including the check bar itself) before reaching the input handler; clicks are now captured ahead of the GUI (the Cancel button still cancels).
  • Boss heal fix: heroes' saved max HP now syncs with talent/rune bonuses after every victory, so the full restore on zone completion (and rest-node healing) reaches their true maximum instead of the base value.
  • Party screen: tinted class icon next to each hero's name in the list (crop of their idle sprite, matching their battle tint).
  • Warrior: ALL attacks now build Rage — Strike 20; Mocking Blow, Bloodlust, Wildstrikes, Overpower, Crushing Blow, and the talent attacks (Rampage, Bulwark Slam, Flurry) 10 each. Mocking Blow removed from Berserker and Swordmaster — it is now a Warden spec ability (the Warden taunt identity).
  • Hack and Slash (new, Berserker): 20 Rage, initiative 3.0 — three strikes of 10 at one target, each hit 40% chance to build 20 Bleed, builds 10 Rage. Perfect: 4 strikes. (New engine support: same-target multi-hits and per-hit bleed chance.)
  • Aimed Shot cost 30 → 20 Focus.

2026-07-07 — Audio & art drop, full-scene battles, shop nodes, elite spoils

  • New assets wired in: main menu background image; Scarlands battle scene art (battles now pick their background by zone); music — menu track (menu/draft/settings/relics), node map track (map/party/shop/spec choice, continues seamlessly between those screens), battle track, and a boss-entry tune that plays once when a boss battle starts and hands off to the battle track; bow SFX — Hunter and Orc Archer attacks use dedicated attack/impact/blocked sounds. New Music autoload manages tracks across scene changes (idempotent play, volume on the master bus).
  • Whole battle scene visible: camera now 1:1 over the full 1280×720 arena (was zoomed to 1.2); sprites scaled up ~25% to compensate (heroes 2.6 → 3.2, Chief 3.9, bosses 4.4); name plates, bars, and markers repositioned for the larger sprites; Break camera punch retuned.
  • Loot nodes replaced with shop nodes: zone deck is now 18 fights / 6 rest / 6 shops — the Peddler is back on the map (resolves the fourth-pass open question). Loot chests remain only as elite drops.
  • Path connectivity tuned: every node links to its own column plus a 70% chance of ONE adjacent column — never all three. Choice most tiers, but only one column of drift per step, so reaching a specific elite or shop takes route planning.
  • Elite spoils — the snowball reward: elites now drop a rune for a random hero, a random consumable, and more gold (80–100, up from 55–70), on top of their double talent points. Skilled players who route toward elites out-scale the zone.
  • Multi-hit attacks roll miss/parry per strike: a parried or missed hit no longer cancels the follow-up strikes (each parried strike still triggers its counter). Applies to Hack and Slash, Razor Ice, Twin Fang, Flurry.
  • Rune generation moved into the Run autoload (shared by shop offers and elite drops).
